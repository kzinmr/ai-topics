# Retrospective / Hindsight Analysis Section

When a transcript is significantly older than the current date (e.g., >1 year), consider adding a **retrospective section** at the end of the structured transcript (before Companion Resources). This adds significant value by connecting historical claims to current reality.

## When to add
- User asks "how does this look from today's perspective?"
- Transcript is >1 year old and covers predictions, recommendations, or technical claims
- The field has evolved significantly since the lecture date

## Structure
```markdown
## YYYY年振り返り — N年前の発言はどう見えているか

### 的中した予測・普遍的な洞察
- **「quote from transcript」** — What actually happened

### 変化した状況
- **「quote」** — How the situation changed

### この講義の歴史的意義
Brief paragraph on why this transcript matters as a historical document
```

## Pitfalls
- **Language**: Match the user's language for the retrospective section (Japanese if they write in Japanese)
- **Citation**: Reference specific timestamps or quotes from the transcript, not vague summaries
- **Balance**: Include both correct predictions AND things that changed — don't just praise
- **Date context**: Note what was known vs unknown at the time of the lecture (e.g., "Llama 3 was not yet released")
- **Placement**: Insert BEFORE the Companion Resources section, after the last lecture content section
