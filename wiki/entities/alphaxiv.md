# alphaXiv

**alphaXiv** (alphaxiv.org) is a social reading layer over arXiv: *"Discuss, discover, and read arXiv papers."*
It attaches per-section discussion and curation signals to arXiv papers, positioning itself as the
paper-discovery-and-commentary tool that arXiv itself does not provide.

## Why it is in the wiki

[[entities/andrej-karpathy|Andrej Karpathy]] named it on **2026-11-01** as the spiritual successor to his own
**arXiv Sanity Preserver** — which he disclosed he had **shut down** ("I had to bring it down (RIP) because it
was just too much work to maintain"), adding that *"it would have been a lot easier today"* and that alphaXiv
is a tool he *"likes & uses and is in the same spirit."*

That reply is worth keeping for two reasons:

1. **The maintenance-cost argument for agent-era tooling.** Karpathy's "it would have been a lot easier today"
   is the same claim he makes for [[concepts/hackertube|HackerTube]] (~2 h with Opus 5.5) and
   [[concepts/tristantube|TristanTube]] (3-minute one-shot): the binding constraint on long-tail research
   tooling is no longer engineering effort but *ongoing maintenance*, and it is still the part agents do not
   remove. arXiv Sanity died of maintenance burden, not of a missing idea.
2. **It closes a gap the wiki tracks elsewhere.** Karpathy's arXiv Sanity belongs to the same lineage as
   [[concepts/claude-science]] and the paper-discovery tooling discussed in
   [[concepts/reward-hacking-research-agents]] — automated reading/recommendation over the arXiv firehose.

## Known limitations

No independent evaluation is recorded here; the only tracked assessment is Karpathy's endorsement in a reply,
and his own framing is comparative ("in the same spirit" as a tool he built and retired), not a feature or
quality audit.
