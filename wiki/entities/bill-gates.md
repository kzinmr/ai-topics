# Bill Gates

Co-founder of Microsoft and chair of the Gates Foundation. In August 2026 he published a landmark essay, **"The turbulent AI era is here. The choices we make now are critical"** (gatesnotes.com), his most comprehensive AI-policy statement to date — and a notable shift of one of tech's most prominent figures into full endorsement of aggressive AI-labor-displacement concern plus state-level intervention.

## Core Thesis (Aug 2026 essay)

- AI is "the first technology to replace and even exceed human cognition"; adoption is unprecedentedly fast because it "runs on the devices we already have" and adapts to humans via natural language — so PC-internet analogies are **misleading**.
- Framing: "AI will either be the greatest equalizer ever invented, or the worst source of injustice."
- Rejects a slowdown as feasible ("geopolitical and economic incentives are pushing too hard to go full speed ahead") but says he'd *support* a credible global slowdown plan.

## Three Risks

1. **Permanent job loss** — entry/mid-level white-collar hit first (cites Stanford "Canaries in the Coal Mine" young-worker data); smart robots competing on physical tasks "by the end of the decade"; a robots+AI adoption spiral where market forces accelerate displacement; fundamental rethinking of an economy built on employment.
2. **Empowered harm** — fraud, deepfakes, disinformation, surveillance at every scale; cyberattack offense/defense imbalance; bioterrorism dual-use; autonomous weapons; eventually loss of control over AI systems themselves.
3. **Psychosocial damage** — AI companions as addictive "protected greenhouses" stunting development (cites Stanford/CMU study of 1,100+ companion users, arXiv 2506.12605, and Haidt); AI in education risking atrophy of critical thinking exactly when deepfakes make it most essential. Praises China's bans on AI romantic companions for minors as "the furthest" protections.

## The Plan (three prescriptions)

1. **A new domestic + international governance framework for AI** — "unlike any institution we have ever created," combining the nuclear-inspections regime, international aviation regulation, and the ozone-protection agreements.
2. **Tax AI tokens and robots; rebalance taxation of labor vs capital** — reviving his 2017 robot-tax idea, now extended to inference tokens; targeted so beneficial uses (medicine, education) aren't slowed; revenue funds retraining and a stronger safety net.
3. **Philanthropy and personal advocacy** — pushing the framework with lawmakers and frontier labs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft partner with the Foundation); Goalkeepers report focus on non-English-language AI models.

## Positioning & Reception

- Explicit bias disclosure: tech-industry financial ties; all AI-related profits go to the Foundation.
- Gary Marcus (Aug 26 2026) called it "excellent," noting it echoes his *Taming Silicon Valley* (2024) themes — while adding independent *scientists* to the table, which Gates omits. Notable: Gates converges with a long-time skeptic [[gary-marcus]] on risks while remaining optimistic on benefits (healthcare via Viz.ai, agriculture in low-income countries, government services, mental health, "productive struggle" education AI).
- Sits alongside Pope Leo XIV's May 2026 AI encyclical as a major non-technologist institutional intervention in AI policy.
