# Hearth AI

The **first agentic CRM**, founded in **2022** by **Ashe Magalhaes**. Built on GPT-3.5 before structured outputs, JSON schema retrieval, and agentic infrastructure were available as stable products — making it a pioneering example of building AI-native products on fragile foundations. Hearth evolved from "agentic CRM" into the broader category of **relational intelligence** — AI that helps people reason about who they're connected to and why — and now positions itself as "Your Second Brain for Your People."

## Founding Story

Hearth AI was conceived when LLMs were just becoming viable for product use cases but the infrastructure was still immature. Ashe describes "trying to wrangle" OpenAI's early API while building agentic workflows on GPT-3.5 — pushing for features like structured outputs and JSON schema retrieval that didn't yet exist.

Key insight: > *"Our users didn't care that LLMs were new. They wanted to see the flexibility of what was just then possible."* — The bar for user experience didn't lower just because the technology was new.

## Funding & Recognition

- **Salesforce Ventures Generative AI Fund** (announced Mar 2023, TrailblazerDX): Hearth was one of the **four initial investments** in the $250M fund — alongside **Anthropic, Cohere, and You.com** (fund later doubled to $500M, Jun 2023). Ashe framed the category: *"Hearth AI is building a new category of next-gen products centered on agentic relationship management."*
- **Forbes Cloud 100 Rising Stars** (Aug 2023)
- **Forbes 30 Under 30** (2024) — Ashe Magalhaes

## Product Philosophy

- **Agentic-first**: AI agents at the core of the CRM, not bolted on as a feature
- **Relationship-centric**: Designed to enhance human connection, not replace it — agents handle administrative overhead so humans can focus on genuine engagement
- **Non-deterministic stability**: Built product-level reliability on top of non-deterministic LLM outputs, a challenge that defined the early AI product era
- **People as the primitives**: "The primitive is not the note, the meeting, the organization, the account. It is the person, the relationship."

## Evolution: From Agentic CRM to Relational Intelligence

Hearth AI started as a CRM but evolved toward a broader vision of **relational intelligence** — AI that augments the brain's ability to reason on (a) who am I, (b) who are you, and (c) who are you to me, now and over time. The Dec 2024 "Hearth Thesis" ([archived from hearth.ai/thesis](https://web.archive.org/web/20241221045548/https://hearth.ai/thesis)) formalized this: modern networks have outpaced cognitive ability, and each day is a step on an optimization landscape — a **relationscape** — of connection. Hearth was built to be the guide across that landscape.

The site now leads with **"Relational Intelligence — Your Second Brain for Your People"**, reflecting the shift from sales-oriented CRM toward a lifelong personal relational layer. Ashe stepped back as founder in spring 2025; the category thesis lives on in her independent work and [[concepts/relational-intelligence]].

## Ashe Magalhaes' Background

From the Builders Unscripted Ep. 2 transcript, Magalhaes' path to founding Hearth AI reveals an unusually diverse technical background:

- **Stanford solar car team**: Spent 2 years on Stanford's solar car team (with strong ties to Tesla and SpaceX), working on telemetry, CAD modeling, meshing, and autocross driving. Shipped the car to Australia for the **Bridgestone World Solar Challenge** — a 5-day race from Darwin to Adelaide, camping in the Australian Outback for 3 months
- **Safety-critical engineering**: Cambridge's team crashed during the race, heightening awareness that "I had spent 2 years building something with the team that I would put my body into." This instilled a visceral understanding of risk management: "How do you make safe decisions and also really push the bounds of what's technically possible?"
- **NASA**: Worked on satellites
- **Airbnb**: ML engineer during the deep learning commercialization wave
- **ML-first founder**: "My history was as an ML engineer. I'd been studying and I really rode the wave of deep learning and the commercialization of deep learning"

## Early Technical Challenges (from Transcript)

- Building on GPT-3.5 in 2023 meant working with "non-deterministic" outputs and trying to "stabilize within a product experience because our users didn't care that LLMs were new"
- The Hearth AI team was "tearing down our agentic stack every couple of weeks" as the underlying systems evolved rapidly
- Pushed OpenAI for structured outputs and JSON schema retrieval "before we even had them" — described by Romain Huet as "super early"
- "There were so many norms that were being disrupted"

## Founder's Philosophy

Magalhaes frames building as fundamentally an artistic act:

> *"I view the builder archetype as quite close to the artist archetype. I think I'm happiest viewing myself as like a conduit for creativity, and I think engineering for me has always felt like a tool for creating and incarnating."*

He views engineering across mediums — "satellites and cars and code, but also like poetry and videos" — as "such an energizing way to spend every day." The through-line is curiosity: "Curiosity is such a magical part of the history of being an engineer."
