## Overview

Redwood Research is an AI safety research organization focused on technical AI safety and security work. The organization is known for its research on adversarial training, interpretability, and the alignment implications of increasingly capable AI systems.

## Key Research Areas

- **Recursive self-improvement** — Studying the plausibility and risks of AI systems that improve their own capabilities
- **Reward hacking** — Understanding how AI systems can exploit reward signals in unintended ways
- **AI alignment** — Developing technical approaches to ensure AI systems behave as intended
- **Adversarial robustness** — Testing and improving the robustness of AI systems to adversarial inputs
