# Zhipu AI

**Zhipu AI** (trade name **Z.ai**; registered as "Beijing Zhipu Huazhang Technology Co., Ltd.") is a Chinese artificial intelligence company and one of China's most important frontier AI labs. It is the maker of the **GLM** family of large language models.

## Overview

## GLM Model Family

Zhipu's GLM line has tracked the open-weight frontier through 2025–2026:

- **[[concepts/glm-5-1|GLM-5.1]]** and **[[concepts/glm-5-2|GLM-5.2]]** — earlier frontier releases; GLM-5.2 powers the Z.ai consumer chatbot.
- **[[concepts/glm-5-3|GLM-5.3]]** (released 2026-08-14) — "Frontier Coding with Emergent Cyber Capabilities", one of the Chinese labs' August 2026 stride-the-frontier releases (see the newsletter roundup "How Chinese Labs Keep Stride with the Frontier").
- **[[entities/glm-5v-turbo|GLM-5V-Turbo]]** — the vision variant.

The consumer/agent product is **[[entities/glm-5-zai|Z.ai]]**, an AI chatbot and agent platform.

## Significance

Zhipu is a leading representative of China's frontier-model effort, alongside [[concepts/glm-5-3|GLM]] peers such as DeepSeek ([[entities/deepseek|entity]]), Alibaba's Qwen, and Moonshot's Kimi. Its August 2026 GLM-5.3 release — explicitly claiming *emergent cyber capabilities* in a frontier coding model — drew scrutiny in the AI-safety and cyber-governance discussion, paralleling the attention around OpenAI's GPT-5.5-Cyber and Anthropic's Mythos/Project Glasswing.
