## Overview

Christian Catalini is a MIT-trained economist (Ph.D., MIT Sloan) and co-founder of **Lightspark**, the Lightning Network payments company. His research has spanned digital currencies, platform economics, and most recently the strategic implications of open-weight AI on competitive moats.

## AI Strategy Views

### "The Only Moat That Survives AI" (Aug 2026)

In a Superintel+ interview (2026-08-02), Catalini argued that the AI moat debate is fought on the wrong terrain:

- **Measurable things get automated**: "Anything that can be measured will eventually be automated" — the durable question is what *cannot* be automated.
- **Open-weight lag ~4 months and flat**: open-weight models sit ~4 months behind the closed frontier on Epoch's capability index, and the gap has stayed flat — evidence that *the gap is not the moat*.
- **Value moves to judgment**: value concentrates in the human act of deciding whether a machine's output is good enough to ship.
- **Routing as aggregation layer**: model routing may become the aggregation layer, but with open questions; the application layer is not a defensible moat either.
