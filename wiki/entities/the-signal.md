# The Signal (Alex Banks)

**The Signal** is a weekly AI newsletter written by [[entities/alex-banks|Alex Banks]] on the [[entities/substack|Substack]] platform. Launched in April 2022 (originally as "Sunday Signal"), it has grown to over 55,000 subscribers with a 46% average open rate. The newsletter delivers one curated issue per week on Sunday, with occasional mid-week deep dives.

## Mission

The Signal's mission is to "democratise AI education for everyone" — cutting through the noise of constant AI announcements to deliver what actually matters for busy professionals. The publication's name reflects its core philosophy: **signal over noise** in an ecosystem drowning in hype.

> "In a world drowning in AI hype and headlines, we cut through the chaos. While thousands of AI updates flood feeds daily, we filter the drama, fear, and frenzy to deliver what actually matters."

## Approach

The Signal operates on three core principles:

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Useful** | Every issue helps readers apply AI to their life and work today |
| **Digestible** | AI concepts broken down into clear, actionable takeaways |
| **Insightful** | Fuels curiosity, not confusion |

Rather than publishing daily updates, Banks spends the week researching, filtering, and refining to deliver one focused issue — a deliberate signal-to-noise optimization that respects readers' time.

### Philosophical Deep Dives
Long-form essays exploring the broader implications of AI on work and society:
- **"You're Underestimating AI on Purpose"** (Jun 2026) — AI Perception Paradox, Amara's Law, systematic underestimation of AI progress as tools become invisible through reliability
- **"AI Is Quietly Thinking for Us"** (Jul 2026) — Cognitive Atrophy Paradox, the McGill GPS study analogy for AI dependence eroding human judgment
- **"AI Will Never Give the Same Answer Twice"** (Jul 2026)
- **"You're Generating AI Workslop"** (May 2026) — AI-generated content quality critique
- **"Where AI is really taking jobs"** (Jun 2026)

### Tutorials & Workflows
Practical guides focused on Claude ecosystem and AI tooling:
- **"How to Master Claude Design (for Beginners)"** (May 2026)
- **"How to run multiple tasks at once in Cowork"** (Jun 2026)
- **"Microsoft Copilot Skills in Excel"** (Jul 2026)
- **Claude Cowork series** (Apr 2026): setup, Skills, Projects, mobile Dispatch

### Tool Recommendations
Curated lists for practical AI use:
- **"6 AI tools for the people still doing everything in ChatGPT"** (May 2026)

### Content Moderation & Platform Policy Analysis
Critical examinations of AI content detection and platform governance:
- **"The good, the bad and the ugly of AI writing"** (Jul 2026) — Analysis of Substack's "Scan for AI text" feature, Pangram AI detection tool, the Claudefishing concept, and the Mia Ballard / Hachette false positive case

## The Signal vs. Alex Banks

While [[entities/alex-banks|Alex Banks]] is the sole writer and creator of The Signal, the publication is distinct from his broader work. Banks also runs multiple AI ventures (Harley, GrowHub, Tribescaler) and teaches on DataCamp. The Signal is his primary platform for AI commentary and education — the most visible channel in his multiplatform audience of 300,000+.
