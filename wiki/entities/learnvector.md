## Overview

LearnVector is a new AI-native [[ai-education]] company founded by [[ai-education|Andrew Ng]], launched in mid-2026. Backed by a $100 million investment from Coursera, the company's mission is to "accelerate human development" by building one-to-one learning experiences powered by AI. LearnVector represents a significant shift from the massive open online course (MOOC) model that Ng pioneered at Coursera, instead focusing on deeply personalized, adaptive instruction that stays with each learner until mastery.

## Mission & Approach

LearnVector's core thesis holds that AI will not replace people but instead create unprecedented opportunities — and that building skills is the key to ensuring every person can take part in that future. The platform builds personalized learning paths that plan collaboratively with each learner, uses adaptive AI that adjusts to individual learning styles and pace, and provides persistent tutoring that remains engaged until the student achieves true mastery. This one-to-one model contrasts sharply with the [[cohort-based-learning]] approach of traditional MOOCs, positioning LearnVector as an AI-native alternative to cohort-based and self-paced online education.

## Funding

LearnVector is backed by a $100 million investment from Coursera, the MOOC platform Ng co-founded in 2012. This substantial funding positions LearnVector as one of the better-capitalized startups in the [[ai-industry-economics|AI edtech space]], reflecting strong investor conviction in Ng's vision and the growing market for AI-powered personalized learning. The investment signals a strategic bet that AI-native education platforms will disrupt the very MOOC model that Coursera itself helped establish.
