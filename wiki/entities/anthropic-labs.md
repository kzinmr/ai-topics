# Anthropic Labs

**Anthropic Labs** is the experimental product division within [[entities/anthropic]], responsible for launching products outside the core Claude AI assistant. It productizes features and tools that extend Claude's capabilities beyond the chat interface — spinning up fast-moving, high-judgment bets that would otherwise stay as internal research capabilities.

## Overview

Anthropic Labs represents a strategic expansion of Anthropic beyond the single-product (Claude chat) model. Rather than bolting every new capability onto the assistant, the company created a dedicated division that can move quickly on standalone products. Its output is positioned as "high judgment, high taste" experiments powered by the newest frontier models.

The division's formation was publicly confirmed at the **"Code w/ Claude" developer event on May 6, 2026**, where keynote speaker and new Chief Product Officer Ami Vora was introduced as having "replaced Mike Krieger earlier this year (he's now the co-lead of Anthropic Labs)." Mike Krieger — Instagram co-founder and Anthropic's first CPO — stepped down from the CPO role to co-lead the new Labs effort, signaling that the division is treated as a top-priority, founder-level bet rather than a peripheral skunkworks.

## Products

### Claude Design (April 17, 2026) — flagship launch
A visual design collaboration tool powered by Claude Opus 4.7's vision model. Claude Design is the first concrete product launched from Anthropic Labs, marking the division's transition from an experimental group to a product-delivery org. See the full product breakdown at [[concepts/claude-design]].

- **Conversational design**: describe what you need, and Claude builds a first version
- **Refinement loop**: iterate through conversation, inline comments, direct edits, and custom sliders (made by Claude)
- **Design-system sync**: onboarding reads your codebase and design files, then applies your brand automatically to every project
- **Code-powered prototypes**: build prototypes with voice, video, shaders, 3D, and built-in AI
- **Export & handoff**: export to Canva, PDF, PPTX, or standalone HTML; one-instruction handoff to [[entities/claude-code]] for implementation
- **Availability**: research preview for Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise subscribers

## Strategic Context

### The "Big Swing"
Claude Design was part of Anthropic's coordinated April 2026 "Big Swing" — the near-simultaneous release of Claude Opus 4.7, Claude Design, and Claude Code Routines. Labs is the vehicle Anthropic uses to make these kinds of product-level swings rather than only model-level ones.

### Relationship to core product lines
Anthropic Labs sits alongside — but separate from — Anthropic's core products:
- [[entities/claude-code]] — the coding agent (core, not Labs)
- **Claude** chat assistant (core, not Labs)

This division-of-labor lets the core teams focus on the assistant and coding agent while Labs explores adjacent surfaces (design, and future bets) with the newest models.
