# Fourier Intelligence

**Fourier** (Chinese: , pinyin: Fùlìyè — named after mathematician Joseph Fourier) is a Chinese robotics company headquartered in Zhangjiang, Shanghai. Founded in 2015 by **Alex Gu** (Shanghai Jiao Tong University graduate, formerly a sales executive at National Instruments), it started in rehabilitation robotics and expanded into humanoid robots. Backed by investors including IDG Capital and Saudi Aramco.

## Rehabilitation Robotics (2015–2019)

- **2017**: Unveiled **Fourier X1**, a walking-assist exoskeleton.
- **January 2019**: Launched **EXOPS** (Fourier Exoskeleton & Robotics Open Platform) — an "Android-like" open platform for exoskeleton development, built with National Instruments.
- **By 2019**: Intelligent rehab devices (exoskeletons) deployed in hundreds of hospitals and medical care centers across 10+ countries.
- **June 2020**: Acquired **Zhuhai RHK Healthcare**, a rehab services provider serving 200+ hospitals in China — expanded customer base, data feedback, and device-leasing/financing channels for lower-tier-city hospitals.
- **November 2023**: Launched **MetaMotus Galileo**, a biomechanics analysis and rehab platform integrating VR and robotics.

## Humanoid Robots (2019–present)

### GR-1
Development began in 2019 in a small lab on the first floor of Fourier's headquarters. Key milestones:
- **2022**: A 1.65 m GR-1 stood up and walked on both legs (video drew mixed responses — some overseas audiences thought it was CGI).
- **July 2023**: Unveiled at the World Artificial Intelligence Conference in Shanghai; already delivered in small quantities to universities and AI companies for R&D.
- **June 2024**: Vision system enabled real-time mapping, navigation, and obstacle avoidance with human-like precision.

### GR-2 (September 2024)
Upgrade of GR-1:
- 1.75 m tall, 63 kg, **53 degrees of freedom**, 3 kg single-arm load capacity
- Silver-gray body with black joints and a functional purple belt
- Detachable battery; more complex task capabilities; improved SDK

### GR-3 Series (2025)
- GR-3 (Kitty head variant) and **GR-3C Astronaut** edition — consumer/collector-oriented variants.

## International Presence

- Facilities in Singapore; partnerships with leading hospitals internationally.
- December 2023: Xi Jinping met Fourier's leadership during a Shanghai inspection tour and asked whether the bipedal robot could perform basic tasks — a signal of Chinese government interest in robotics.
