# Discovery Loop

**Discovery Loop** is an AI research company (Public Benefit Corporation) founded August 2026 by four of Google DeepMind's most senior departing researchers — Jeff Dean, Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals, and Quoc Le. Its stated mission is to **automate machine research** — using AI agents to accelerate the research process itself.

## Founding Announcement

Jeff Dean announced the company on X on August 5, 2026:

> "I am very excited to announce that, along with my longtime friends and collaborators @Sanjay_Ghemawat, @OriolVinyalsML and @quocleix, we are founding Discovery Loop (@DiscoLoopAI), a Public Benefit Corporation whose mission is to automate machine research."

The founding team combines Google's deepest infrastructure and research-execution expertise: Dean and Ghemawat co-created MapReduce, BigTable, Spanner, and TensorFlow; Vinyals led much of DeepMind's Gemini architecture work; Quoc Le was a core Google Brain architect and co-creator of Transformer-era techniques.

## Founding Team

- **[[entities/jeff-dean]]** — Google Senior Fellow for 27 years; co-founder of Google Brain; MapReduce/TensorFlow co-creator
- **Sanjay Ghemawat** — Google Senior Fellow; Dean's long-time collaborator on MapReduce, BigTable, Spanner
- **Oriol Vinyals** — DeepMind chief scientist; Gemini architecture lead; Transformer-era Sequence-to-Sequence pioneer
- **Quoc Le** — Google Brain co-creator; AutoML, attention, and large-scale training pioneer

## Context: The GDM Exodus

Discovery Loop's founding coincided with a wave of senior departures from Google DeepMind in 2025-2026:

- John Jumper (Nobel laureate, AlphaFold) → Anthropic
- Noam Shazeer (Transformer co-author) → OpenAI
- David Silver, Denny Zhou → departed earlier
- Demis Hassabis → moved from CEO to Chair of Google DeepMind / Chief Scientist of Alphabet
- Koray Kavukcuoglu → promoted to SVP of Google DeepMind

AINews (Latent Space, swyx) framed the departures as a coordinated signal: "It's hard to beat around the bush — today's most important story is the coordinated departures of Jeff Dean, Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals, and Quoc Le, some of the most senior engineering and research leaders in the field." Commentary from Nathan Lambert, Andrew Ng, and others described the move as "a historical inflection point for Google's AI efforts and a strong signal that AI-for-science is becoming a primary frontier, not a side quest."

## Significance

- **Autoresearch thesis**: Discovery Loop is the highest-profile explicit bet on [[autoresearch]] / [[concepts/agentic-research]] — using LLM agents to automate the research loop itself, not just code generation.
- **Google relationship**: Unlike a clean break, Google is a **founding investor and Cloud partner** — retaining strategic access while allowing founders to pursue the autonomous-research agenda outside Alphabet.
- **Founding-team concentration**: Four co-founders with combined Google tenures of ~100 years give Discovery Loop unusual credibility for an unproven research-automation thesis.
