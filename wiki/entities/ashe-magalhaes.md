# Ashe Magalhaes

Founder of **Hearth AI** (the first agentic CRM), former ML engineer at Airbnb, and a builder whose career spans solar car racing, NASA satellite work, and frontier AI products. Known for her philosophy that **the builder is an artist** — creating across mediums from code to hardware to poetry. Currently building **relational intelligence** — AI that expands our ability to reason and act on who we're connected to and why — through agentic network workflows, a frontier-tech interview film series, and a wearable+rolodex product. X: [@ashebytes](https://x.com/ashebytes).

## Bio

Ashe Magalhaes has lived at the intersection of frontier technology and creative building. While at Stanford, she spent two years on the **solar car team**, building a car end-to-end and racing it in the Bridgestone World Solar Challenge across the Australian outback. She then worked on **satellites at NASA** and rode the deep learning wave as an **ML engineer at Airbnb**.

Her career spans over 13 years in AI/ML plus hardware: Facebook (iOS, data-mining IDE), NASA Ames (ISS photo geolocation), Stanford's Space Development Laboratory (satellite watchdog software for the JPL-Stanford-Pumpkin LMRST project), Pear VC (Engineer in Residence), political tech at Civis Analytics ($100M+ ad spend allocation), Schmidt Futures (Rise program, Afghan Future Fund), and Apple (ML education program design).

In **2022**, she founded **Hearth AI** — the first agentic CRM, built on GPT-3.5 when the infrastructure was still fragile and non-deterministic. She gave early feedback to OpenAI's API team, pushing for structured outputs and JSON schema retrieval before those features existed as products. Hearth raised from **Salesforce Ventures' Generative AI Fund** (initial cohort alongside Anthropic, Cohere, and You.com, March 2023) and earned **Forbes Cloud 100 Rising Stars** (2023) and **Forbes 30 Under 30** (2024) recognition. She stepped back from Hearth in spring 2025.

Her current work focuses on **relational intelligence** — the thesis that the primitive of personal and professional software is not the note, meeting, or account, but **the person and the relationship**. She built a personal "second brain" agentic system called **Ashe AI** (Jan 2026) that manages her rolodex, goals, gratitude practice, and curated social feed, and she is an advocate for **building in public**, sharing her process and letting serendipity emerge from open creation.

## Core Ideas

### Builder as Artist

> *"I view the builder archetype as quite close to the artist archetype. I'm happiest viewing myself as like a conduit for creativity."*

Ashe refuses the engineer/artist binary. For her, engineering is a **tool for creating and incarnating** — whether the medium is satellites, cars, code, poetry, or videos. The common thread is curiosity and the desire to bring things to life.

### Agentic Personal Operating System

Ashe runs multiple AI agents as a **personal OS** — agents that handle context, memory, and follow-through so she can be more present in creative and relational domains. This is not about replacing human connection but **enhancing it**: offloading administrative overhead to agents frees up attention for what matters.

### Relational Intelligence (The Hearth Thesis)

> *"The primitive is not the note, the meeting, the organization, the account. It is the person, the relationship."*

Ashe's core thesis, articulated in the Dec 2024 essay "On Relational Intelligence: The Hearth Thesis" (originally at hearth.ai/thesis), argues that modern networks have **outpaced cognitive ability** — the human brain evolved for localized tribes, not millions of touchpoints across timezones and phases of life. We cope by reducing people to categories (coworkers, leads, prospects) and pushing them through funnels, which is why "networking" and "CRM" feel dehumanizing.

The proposed new category: **relational intelligence** — AI that augments the brain's ability to reason on (a) who am I, (b) who are you, and (c) who are you to me, now and over time. Each day is a step on an optimization landscape — a **relationscape** — of connection, and Hearth was built to be the guide across it.

### Ashe AI: The Second Brain Agentic System

In 2025 Ashe built **Ashe AI**, a personal "second brain" agentic system, documented in her Jan 2026 blog post "My Second Brain Agentic System." It lives in her rituals:

- **Morning ritual**: 5AM wake-up; birthday notifications with automated flower delivery (address book matching); goal tracker with contribution calendars; gratitude workflow with weekly summaries; a custom X feed of ~30 friends' accounts (max 2 posts each in the past 14 hours); throwback posts; virtual mail service with email→call-to-action extraction (incl. credit monitoring across Credit Karma/Equifax/Experian/TransUnion)
- **Throughout the day**: stream-of-consciousness voice notes systematized into her Rolodex (which also pulls from email and X accounts/threads); random notes + reminders stored separately; quotes channel auto-published to ashe.ai/stream
- **Evening ritual**: evening goals, gratitude, curated X feed
- **Future work**: self-alignment (agent access to time-tracking for proactive guidance on cognitive dissonance), product development thought-partner

She describes the goal as "an aligned AI extension of myself" — the question being *"what does an authentic and aligned extension of me feel like for each of the workflows I care about in life?"* The system is a concrete implementation of [[concepts/personal-ai]], complementing the file-based [[concepts/personal-os-for-ai-agents]] pattern with a Slack-native, product-experience UI layer.

### Build in Public

> *"Let it go out into the world, let it breathe, and allow there to be a surprise in what could happen."*

Rather than waiting for polish, Ashe advocates sharing work early. Building in public creates serendipity — unexpected connections, feedback, and collaborations that wouldn't emerge from private development.

### Frontier Rigor

Her solar car experience instilled a visceral understanding of safety-critical engineering: **"How do you make safe decisions and also really push the bounds of what's technically possible?"** This mindset — rigorous without being rigid — carries through to her AI work.
