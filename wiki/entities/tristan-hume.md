# Tristan Hume

Researcher and blogger at **thume.ca**. In the AI context he is best known for his writing on
**AI safety research practice** (including the widely-read work on AI safety strategy via
causal reasoning / "section 2"-style posts done with Neel Nanda) and for an unusually well-regarded
**taste in long-form technical YouTube** — his 2020 YouTube tier list became a semi-canonical
reference for "the best educational channels on the platform."

## YouTube Tier List, six years later (2026-11-01)

Hume refreshed his 2020 tier list of favorite "interesting" YouTube channels, grading
**S 5 / A 14 / B 12 / C 33 / D 92** (~156 channels). His stated thesis:

> *"I still find the best YouTube channels to be some of the most awesome content on the internet for
> learning interesting things."*

The S tier is hardware/hands-on engineering rather than AI or CS:

- **Applied Science** (Ben Krasnow) — S in 2020 too. Hume calls it "the flagship channel of engineering and
  science YouTube for many years": aerogel, water-jet cutter, plasma sputtering chamber, EDM drill, air
  bearings, electroluminescent display, LCD, and his original claim to fame, a home-built scanning electron
  microscope. Hume emphasizes that Krasnow is often *the first non-professional* to reproduce a procedure from
  a paper, after dozens of failed experiments.
- **Clickspring** — clockmaking; Hume's phrase: "watching it is wireheading on pure craftsmanship"
  (custom screws with blue-oxide finish, hand-rounded internal ends, original research + built ancient tools
  for his Antikythera mechanism reconstruction). Second channel **Clickspring Clips** carries 2–4 min
  non-narrated part videos.
- **Stuff Made Here** — newest entry into S tier.

## X amplification of AI-relevant curation (Nov 2026)

Hume's tier list was itself amplified into a wider AI-discussion circuit: on 2026-11-07
[[entities/andrej-karpathy|Andrej Karpathy]] quote-tweeted it ("Which looked excellent so clearly I had to
turn it into TristanTube"), turning the tier list into the curation corpus for
[[entities/tristantube|TristanTube]] — ~47,000 videos plus a TV mode, built "one-shotted with Claude Code in
3 minutes". See [[concepts/hackertube]] for the sibling project. *(logged; the tier list itself is not an AI
technical source, so no raw article was ingested.)*
