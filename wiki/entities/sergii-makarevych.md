# Sergii Makarevych

X: [@sermakarevich](https://x.com/sermakarevich) · bio: "building autonomous AI workers — contact me if you need help with your AI project"

AI engineer/consultant focused on autonomous AI workers and practical AI project delivery. Known in the evals community for a highly-shared Oct 2026 X Article, **"Evals: how to know whether an AI system actually works"** (~6.2K bookmarks / 362K impressions), a plain-language, source-cited primer written "for everyone who ships, buys, or signs off on software built on LLMs: engineers, product managers, and the CEO."

## Key Ideas

See [[concepts/evaluation/evals-how-to-know-whether-an-ai-system-works]] for the full 9-level framework. Signature positions:

- **Two numbers per eval**: the product's score *and* the grader's agreement with humans — "the most common way an AI team fools itself is a confident number from an unchecked grader."
- Every number in the article is traceable to a source: either his own runnable tutorial (bike-shop support assistant with triage/answer/agent components) or a published paper — an explicit contrast with vibes-based eval advice.
- Emphasises statistical honesty (bootstrap CIs, paired comparisons, detection floors) and grader verification (kappa, GAUGE audit) at a practitioner level rarely seen in survey articles.

## Notable Work
