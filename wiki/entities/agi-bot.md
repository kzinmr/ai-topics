# AgiBot

**AgiBot** (Chinese: , "Zhiyuan Robotics") is a Chinese humanoid robotics company headquartered in Shanghai, founded in February 2023 by former Huawei engineers **Deng Taihua** and **Peng Zhihui**. It develops humanoid robots for industrial and service applications and, as of 2025, is the second-largest Chinese humanoid robot seller after [[entities/unitree-robotics|Unitree]].

## Founding & Backing

- **February 2023**: Peng Zhihui launched AgiBot after leaving Huawei. Peng joined Huawei's internal "Top Minds" talent program in 2020 (a University of Electronic Science and Technology of China graduate known for posting inventions on Chinese social media, including an Iron Man-inspired robotic arm and a self-driving bicycle).
- **Investors**: HongShan, Hillhouse Investment, BYD.
- **January 2024**: Manufacturing facility established in Shanghai.
- **September 2024**: Opened the 3,000+ m² **AIDEA Giga Data Factory**.
- **November 2024**: Mass production announced; 962 units manufactured by Dec 15, 2024.
- **January 2025**: 1,000th general-purpose embodied robot produced.

## Products

| Robot | Type | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|
| **RAISE A1** (2023) | Industrial humanoid | 1.75 m, 53 kg, 49 DoF, up to 7 km/h, 80 kg payload; bolt tightening, vehicle inspection, lab work |
| **Yuanzheng A2 / A2-Max / A2-W** (2024) | Bipedal humanoids | A2: 1.75 m, 55 kg; delicate tasks (threading a needle); A2 walked 106.286 km in Nov 2025 — **Guinness World Record** |
| **Lingxi X1 / X1-W** (2024) | Bipedal humanoid | Open-sourced — design materials and code published for developers |
| **X/D1, X2** | Humanoid platform | X2 demonstrated to Malaysian Sarawak Premier at IDECS 2025 |
| **Genie G1 / G2** | Humanoid / industrial | G2 (Oct 2025): industrial-grade embodied robot with force-controlled arms, full-arm torque sensing, advanced spatial perception for manufacturing, logistics, guided tours |
| **C5** | Cleaning robot | Sweeping, scrubbing, mopping; auto-charging, self-cleaning |
| **OmniHand** | Dexterous hand | Companion to humanoid platforms |

**Software / data platforms:**
- **GO-1** (Mar 2025) — first generalist embodied foundation model.
- **Lingqu OS** (Jul 2025) — embodied intelligent operating system; first industrial deployment with continuous on-site operations.
- **Genie Envisioner (GE)** (Aug 2025) — unified video generation platform integrating prediction, policy learning, and neural simulation.
- **LinkCraft** (Oct 2025) — zero-code platform converting videos of human motion into precise humanoid robot actions.
- **AgiBot World** — open robot interaction database; claimed larger and higher quality than Google's Open X-Embodiment.
- **AIDEA** — comprehensive embodied AI data system for collecting and processing robot interaction data.

## Market Position (2025)

- **5,168 humanoid units shipped in 2025** (second only to Unitree's 5,500).
- China controlled ~90% of global humanoid shipments in 2025; Unitree + AgiBot outpaced every Western rival combined.
- Shanghai authorities support a data-collection site where hundreds of robots are tele-operated to generate training data for embodied-AI models (per Reuters).
- Certifications: May 2025 — humanoid obtained certification from China, the United States, and the EU.
