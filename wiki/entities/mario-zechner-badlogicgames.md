# Mario Zechner (@badlogicgames)

Game developer and open-source maintainer, best known for **libGDX** (the Java game framework) and **mdBook**. A prolific and opinionated commentator on AI-assisted coding on X ([x.com/badlogicgames](https://x.com/badlogicgames)) and Hacker News (username `mrguy`, 23,700+ stories submitted). Note: this page complements the existing [[entities/mario-zechner|mario-zechner]] entity, which covers his game-dev and AI-image-generation work in depth; this page records his recent AI-coding commentary surfaced by the X accounts scan.

## Core positions on AI coding

Zechner is one of the more vocal "taste and craftsmanship" skeptics of LLM-generated code quality, arguing that the problem is structural, not a model-capability gap:

- **"The developer has built an entire language around a field seemingly without reasoning about it"** (comment on the "Bend 2" HN thread, Sep 18, 2026): his widely-shared observation that LLMs produce locally-coherent but globally-reasonless abstractions. The accompanying quote from the Bend 2 author — "deciding what I should NOT include took me way more time and effort than any line that was shipped" — resonated with him as the inverse lesson. ([HN thread](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49753898))
- **On vibe coding and SWE-bench** (Apr 2025): rebutted critics who claim his libGDX/mdBook experiments with agents aren't "real" vibe coding — "You cannot simply tell an agent to 'fix that bug' and walk away. That's why you use benchmarks like SWE-Bench" — i.e., agentic coding still requires human verification loops.
- **Against "the models will get better" as an excuse** (Apr 2025): "If you think the output of LLMs 'sucks', then you should be able to build a model that's better... This whole 'AI will eventually get it right' meme is such an easy out, and keeps you from thinking about the actual problem."

## Recurring themes in his posts

His X feed intermixes: libGDX/mdBook maintenance and sponsorship appeals, AI image-generation tooling (ComfyUI workflows, LLM-API prompt generation — see [[entities/mario-zechner|mario-zechner]]), game-dev education (the "100 lines or less" game series), and pointed commentary on LLM code quality. A notable community moment: his **100-lines-of-libGDX games on itch.io** won the 2025 GDC Awards "Best Game" category (Feb 2025 post).
