## Overview

Chai Discovery is an AI-native drug discovery company at the center of the "BioAI phase shift": pharma is suddenly paying for Bio × AI tools, and Chai leads the cohort with four major partnerships. Its core thesis is that AI tools have crossed a trust threshold — structural models became binding (binding affinity) models — which unlocks drug design rather than mere prediction.

## Funding: $400M Series C

Chai raised a **$400M Series C**, one of the largest raises in the AI drug discovery wave. The round signals investor conviction that AI-native drug discovery is moving from research moonshots to commercial reality, with pharma budgets opening up for Bio × AI tooling.

## Pharma Deals

Chai's four major deals:

- **Eli Lilly** — flagship partnership, plus a **Lilly expansion**
- **Novartis** — partnership
- **argenx** — partnership

That is **four deals** (Lilly, Novartis, argenx + Lilly expansion), with three more major deals announced since June 2026 — making Chai the leading BioAI dealmaker in the current cycle.

## Why Now: The Trust Threshold Thesis

Until January 2026, the "we'll just partner / build our own drug" optionality was the only good path for pharma — buyers could always punt on AI-native tools. What changed: **tools got good enough for drug design teams to trust them**. The key technical shift: structural models became **binding (binding affinity) models**, which unlocks design — the step from predicting what a molecule does to designing molecules that bind.

## Deal Economics: Biobucks

"Biobucks" (milestone payments) are only **2–5% upfront** — the vast majority of deal value is contingent on clinical milestones. Chai's ability to close multiple deals on these terms suggests pharma's willingness to bet on AI-native design even under milestone-heavy structures.
