# Shrikar Seshadri (@sseshadri43)

**Shrikar Seshadri** (handle `@sseshadri43`) is a software engineer working on **Deep Agents** infrastructure at [[entities/langchain|LangChain]] (joined ~2026), previously associated with Georgia Tech computing research (`@gtcomputing`) and MathWorks. He is best known as co-author (with [[entities/sydney-runkle|Sydney Runkle]]) of **RubricMiddleware / Grading Rubrics** in LangChain Deep Agents.

X profile bio: *"ML, Robotics @gtcomputing | @LangChain, @MathWorks"* (joined X June 2020).

## Notable Works

- [**Introducing Rubrics: Build Agents that Evaluate and Correct Their Work**](https://x.com/i/article/2061868304654864384) (Jun 2, 2026) — Co-authored with Sydney Runkle. Introduced RubricMiddleware for LangChain Deep Agents: a grader sub-agent evaluates agent runs against a caller-supplied rubric, can call evidence tools (e.g., `run_test_suite`), and injects per-criterion feedback until the rubric passes or an iteration cap is reached. Launch post: *"Excited to launch Grading Rubrics in @LangChain DeepAgents! Attach a rubric to your agent invocation, and a grader evaluates and self-corrects output until it satisfies those requirements."* See [[concepts/rubric-middleware]].
- The article credits reviewers `@huntlovell`, `@masondrxy`, and `@hwchase17` ([[entities/harrison-chase|Harrison Chase]]).
