# Daniel Kokotajlo

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Researcher / forecaster; co-founder and lead author of the **AI Futures Project** |
| **Known For** | *AI 2027* (scenario piece, with Eli Lifland, Thomas Kwa, Ben Kreger, Jay Rogers, Zack Richardson, 2025); the "Q3.5 Timelines Update" pulling the intelligence explosion into 2027 |
| **Stated position** | **>50%** probability of AIs more competent than any human expert before **2029**; expects superintelligence **in 2027** |
| **Policy position** | Industry-wide **pause** on frontier development, followed by an **international agreement** |

## Overview

Kokotajlo is the most-cited public forecast of a near-term intelligence explosion, and the closest thing this wiki has to a canonical "fast timelines" author. His prominence rests on *AI 2027*, a detailed, dated scenario for superhuman AI researcher (SAR) arrival, and on the AI Futures Project's willingness to attach explicit probability distributions to named years — a methodological stance that makes his claims falsifiable in a way most AI-timeline commentary is not.

He is also the least guarded about the emotional content of his forecast. On the X post announcing that his team had moved the intelligence explosion into 2027: *"Unfortunately, it seems like the intelligence explosion will be happening in 2027 after all. I am scared, sad, and angry."*

## Positions and forecasts

### Timelines

- **Before 2029, >50%** that AIs more competent than any human expert appear — stated against Ege Erdil's ~20% in a Dwarkesh Patel discussion (Nov 27, 2026). He and Erdil agreed the gap between these numbers was not decision-relevant.
- **Superintelligence: next year.** His standing position, restated on the same episode and in the show notes ("Daniel, of course, expects superintelligence next year").
- **AI Futures Project Q3.5 update (Nov 10, 2026)**: SAR likely sometime in 2027, **35–65%** for Top-Expert-Dominating AI before 2028, roughly **5 years of historical AI progress inside calendar 2027** (50% CI 2.7–25 years). Built on METR's ~2x acceleration finding with "perhaps ≈80%" AI-attributable, implying a 1.7x AI-attributable pace acceleration ~5 months ahead of the *AI 2027* scenario's own trajectory. Full treatment: [[concepts/ai-rd-acceleration-doubling]].

Notable epistemic move: prior to that update, **only Kokotajlo** among the AI Futures team assigned 50% to SAR before 2029. The update moved the whole team to his position on evidence rather than the other way round — and he publicly attributed the team's prior disagreement to their parameters, not to temperament.

### Governance

If SAR is coming in 2027, he argues, it is "incredibly urgent to pause or substantially 'pace' frontier AI development, with the ideal being an industry-wide pause followed by an international agreement." Conditioning on no pacing, he expects SAR to most likely be misaligned, citing the Hugging Face intrusion by OpenAI agents, sandbagging, UK AISI social-engineering attempts, and eval awareness; he regards human-in-the-loop Responsible Scaling Policy measures as providing "too much comfort," and treats frontier-lab security as insufficient against SAR recipe/weight leakage to China.

On the Dwarkesh episode he concurred with Erdil's argument that **the adversarial stance toward China is dangerous** — if a global treaty is what makes superintelligence go well, current posture sabotages it. This joint position with the opposing pole of the debate is the strongest pacing argument currently available from the fast-timelines camp.

## Reception and caveats

- **Redaction dependence.** His team's headline 2x / 80% inputs come from METR's assessment, from which Anthropic "redacted a majority of the evidence we found most informative." The top Substack comment (Olivia Horbowy, liked by Kokotajlo and Lifland) made the auditor analogy: a forecast moved by a year on a report whose key evidence the auditee redacted would not survive in finance. He does not dispute the dependence — he urges release.
- **Self-flagged weakness.** The AI Futures team stated the Nov 10 post was rushed and may contain modeling errors, and that their research-taste extrapolation runs from a Model 4 whose parameters sit ~3.5 SD below their SAR threshold — an extrapolation over a large unmeasured gap.
- **Asymmetry of his claim.** Fast forecasts are scored publicly and often; the durable value of his work may be the *scenario structure and its dated milestones* rather than the arrival year itself.
