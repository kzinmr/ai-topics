# Andrew Ng

## Overview

Andrew Ng is one of the most influential figures in AI education and democratization. Co-founder of Coursera, former head of Google Brain and chief scientist at Baidu, he now leads [[entities/learnvector|LearnVector]] and [[entities/deeplearning-ai|DeepLearning.AI]], focused on helping developers acquire practical AI engineering skills. His August 2026 "AI Engineering Skills Map" article (47K+ bookmarks, 5.4M impressions) crystallized the four core competencies every developer needs in the LLM era.

## The AI Engineering Skills Map (2026)

Ng's skills map, based on analysis of 10,000+ job postings and structured interviews with AI experts, identifies four essential skills:

1. **Building and deploying AI applications** — LLMs, context engineering, RAG, agentic workflows, evals and error analysis loops
2. **Software engineering fundamentals** — tradeoffs (cost, scalability, reliability, security), system design, steering coding agents with precise engineering language
3. **Using coding agents** — mental models of agent behavior, context management, planning vs execution tradeoffs, multi-agent orchestration, verifier/eval provision
4. **Shaping the build** — product sense, business context, spec authorship, ownership and agency, MVP vs careful-build decisions

Underlying all four: a mindset of **continuous learning**.

## Key Distinction: AI Engineering Skills vs "AI Engineer" Role

Ng explicitly distinguishes between **AI engineering skills** (needed by all developers) and the **AI Engineer role** (a specific job title). Analogy: all developers need cloud skills, but only some have "Cloud Engineer" titles. This framing positions AI literacy as a universal developer competency, not a specialization.
