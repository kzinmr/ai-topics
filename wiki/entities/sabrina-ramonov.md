# Sabrina Ramonov

**Sabrina Ramonov** (X: `@sabrina_share`, `@vibeceo`) is a content-marketing entrepreneur, YC-backed founder of **[[entities/blotato|Blotato]]** (unified social media API + MCP for AI agents), and author of *The Age of Vibe Marketing* (O'Reilly, June 2025). She is the primary popularizer of the **"Vibe CEO"** framework — the practice of running a business with a fleet of AI agents rather than employees.

## Overview

Ramonov built her career on **content-led growth**. She founded a content marketing agency serving startups and later pivoted to product, launching Blotato as the infrastructure layer that makes her "content engine" thesis executable by both humans and AI agents. Blotato crossed **667,000+ posts shipped per month** and is used by both marketing teams and coding agents (via MCP).

Her X presence (`@sabrina_share`, ~130K followers) documents the day-to-day of running a company on agent labor and codifies the vocabulary of the "vibe" workflow family (vibe coding, vibe marketing, vibe CEO).

## Key Ideas

### The Content Engine

Ramonov's central thesis: a founder's content output should be a **fully automated pipeline**, not a manual channel. She describes the loop as four stages — see [[entities/content-engine]] for the concept page.

> "Stop writing content. Build a content engine." — @sabrina_share, X, 2026

### Vibe CEO

An extension of vibe coding from software to **org design**: give an AI agent a goal + a brand voice + a budget, iterate on outputs rather than authoring them. Blotato's `blotato.com/vibe-ceo` page is the product landing for this framing.

### Agent-Native Distribution

Blotato's MCP server (12 tools) is explicitly designed so **Claude / ChatGPT / Cursor / n8n** can post on a founder's behalf. She argues distribution is the last un-agented layer of a solo business.
