## Who is Pieter Levels

Pieter Levels is one of the most prominent indie makers (solopreneurs) in tech. He gained fame through his **12 Startups in 12 Months** challenge in 2014 and has since built a portfolio of profitable SaaS businesses — all as a solo founder with no employees.

His philosophy: ship fast, use boring tech (single PHP file was his stack for years), focus on adding value rather than perfection. He open-shares his revenue numbers publicly.

Beyond the products, Levels is a prolific writer: his blog at levels.io has **800+ posts since 2013** (per his stats page), covering business, tech, travel, and AI. He has appeared on the **Lex Fridman Podcast** (Aug 2024) and publishes an "Everything Chart" of all his projects. In October 2025 he launched **levels.vc**, his own investment fund — an unusual move for an indie hacker, investing his solo-founder earnings into startups.

## AI-Era Thesis (2026)

By mid-2026, Levels had become a leading data point on how AI is reshaping the solo-founder economy — both as an early adopter and as a victim of his own tools. His July 2026 posts form a coherent thesis about the end of the classic indie-hacking playbook:

### "Indie hackers may be the first type of developer to go extinct" (2026-07-30)
[The classic playbook](https://levels.io/indie-hackers-first-to-go-extinct-with-ai) — learn to code, find a niche, ship a micro-SaaS, build in public, post MRR screenshots, repeat — assumed **execution was the scarce resource**. That equation has flipped: "execution now costs ~$20/month" (an AI API subscription), so "anyone can build personal software instead of paying for SaaS." His worked example: why pay $29/month for a product when Claude Code can recreate a better version in 20 minutes?

His framing: **AI raises the ceiling** (indie hackers become far more productive) **but lowers the floor** (the market is now crowded with "normies" who can vibe-code their own tools). Building in public on X makes it easy for others to steal your idea and roadmap. The silver lining: disruption "fertilizes the soil" for whoever masters **distribution, branding, or proprietary data** — the moats that survive when code is free.

### "BigAI is cannibalizing everything that used to be apps" (2026-07-26)
Levels observed [declining revenue and traffic across indie hackers](https://levels.io/bigai-cannibalizing-indiehackers-apps) — "on my own projects too" — and attributed it to frontier AI labs absorbing the use cases that used to be standalone apps. 2.15M views on this post; his verdict: "not bad btw, just times are changing and we have to adapt."

### "I cancelled all my SaaS subscriptions and vibecoded 100% of them myself" (2026-07-26)
The ultimate self-disruption: Levels now pays only for **domains, server hosting/storage, and AI APIs** — every SaaS he used has been replaced by his own AI-built versions. He noted the irony of "being replaced myself after I replaced everything I pay for," but stays financially comfortable via a decade of invested earnings.

### Minimal business stack (2026-07-14)
His recipe for a modern business: **100% free open-source software + a VPS + an AI API + R2/S3 storage** — "they don't want me to tweet that because it destroys their businesses."

### Practitioner details
- **Claude Code as daily driver**: "I've been coding almost solely on my VPS with Claude Code for almost a year now" (2026-06-28); documents Kimi K3 workflows too (2026-07-17)
- **Cost ceiling**: his sites get **4 billion requests/year hosted on his own VPS for $244/month** (2026-04-18)
- **Vibe-coded ops**: won his first big Stripe dispute in a decade using a vibe-coded responder (2026-04-30)
- **Distribution is the new bottleneck**: "Everyone can now build apps with AI so now distribution is the real challenge" (2026-06-06)
