# Koray Kavukcuoglu

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | SVP, Google DeepMind (since August 2026); Chief AI Architect, Google |
| **Previous Role** | CTO, Google DeepMind |
| **Tenure at DeepMind** | 13+ years (since early days) |
| **Known for** | Starting DeepMind's deep learning team; WaveNet; DQN |

## Overview

Koray Kavukcuoglu is the newly appointed **SVP of Google DeepMind**, reporting directly to Google and Alphabet CEO Sundar Pichai. He was promoted on August 5, 2026, as part of a leadership reorganization that saw Demis Hassabis transition to Chair of GDM and Chief Scientist of Alphabet.

## Responsibilities

As SVP, Kavukcuoglu oversees:
- **Gemini model development** — the frontier model family powering Google's AI products
- **Frontier AI research** — next-generation AI capabilities
- **Gemini app and developer teams** — consumer and developer-facing AI products

## Career at DeepMind

Kavukcuoglu has been at DeepMind since its early days, spanning over 13 years. Key contributions include:
- **Founded the deep learning team** at DeepMind
- **WaveNet** — breakthrough generative model for raw audio (2016), later deployed in Google Assistant
- **DQN (Deep Q-Network)** — foundational reinforcement learning work that learned control policies from raw pixel inputs on Atari games (2013)

## Leadership Context

In Sundar Pichai's announcement:

> "Koray has been at DeepMind since its early days, and over his 13 years there, he has started our deep learning team and led the way on breakthroughs like WaveNet and DQN. I look forward to seeing him lead GDM into this next chapter."

Demis Hassabis added:

> "Koray and I have been working together for over 13 years, since the early days of DeepMind. He is one of the world's foremost AI experts and has been championing GDM's mission from day one. I have total confidence in Koray, Josh, and the rest of the GDM exec team."
