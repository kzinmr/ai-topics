Harvey Brings Intake to Claude for Legal Artifacts Launch
The Harvey Intake artifact brings legal support into Claude, giving business teams a place to ask questions and submit requests while legal teams manage the work in Harvey.
by
Harvey Team
•
Oct 28, 2026
Legal teams need to help the business move faster while keeping control of the advice it relies on. Many business teams already bring their questions to Claude, including legal ones. When those questions get a general answer instead of reaching the legal team, Legal loses visibility and control.
Harvey is proud to be a launch partner for the latest Claude for Legal release, which adds a gallery of ready-to-use artifacts that legal teams can adapt and share across the business. For the launch, we built the
Harvey Intake artifact
. It connects business users in Claude to the legal team in Harvey, so the business gets help where it works and Legal stays in control.
Watch Video
How it Works
The Harvey Intake artifact is a ready-to-use artifact in Claude that legal teams can share with the business to give employees a place to ask legal questions, submit requests, and track responses. It connects to Harvey through our MCP server, so when a business user submits a question or request:
Routine questions are answered using Legal’s guidance, playbooks, and prior work in Harvey.
Requests that need legal judgment are routed to a lawyer in Harvey, with responses returned to the requester in Claude.
Every request is logged in
Intake
, where Legal can set assignment rules, track progress, and triage incoming work.
Requesters can check status and follow up on their requests within Claude.
For example, when a prospect returns a marked-up contract, the account executive submits it through the Harvey Intake artifact in Claude. The assigned lawyer reviews the changes in Harvey and returns the revised contract to the AE in Claude. Legal can manage the request and review in one place, while the AE can track progress and get the contract back without switching tools or chasing updates, keeping the deal moving with Legal’s oversight.
“
Business teams are already bringing their questions to Claude. Harvey meets them where they work, giving Legal the ability to set the rules, see what’s being asked, and step in to manage risk. We’re proud to build with Anthropic to make that possible.
”
Matt Zerweck
Head of In-House Product at Harvey
Legal teams can also connect to Harvey inside other Claude Artifacts, bringing in the context they already keep in Harvey with no manual uploads.
Extend Legal’s Reach Across the Business
With Harvey, the business can ask Legal for help in the tools it already uses, and every request lands in one place for the legal team to manage through to resolution.
The Harvey team will continue building with Anthropic to bring legal guidance into the tools business teams already use, helping them get answers faster and giving lawyers more time for work that needs their judgment.
