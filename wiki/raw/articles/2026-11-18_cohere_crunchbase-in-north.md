# Crunchbase in North

**Source**: [https://cohere.com/blog/crunchbase-in-north](https://cohere.com/blog/crunchbase-in-north)

We’re excited to announce the launch of the Crunchbase Connector in
North by Cohere
, bringing data across seven million companies to the AI agents and automations that your enterprise relies on.
This connector is especially valuable for enterprises researching the venture capital and startup ecosystem, with access to
Crunchbase’s predictive market models
. Simply bring your Crunchbase license, authenticate, and start equipping agents and automations with Crunchbase data.
For a full list of available Crunchbase data, check out
North documentation
.
Using Crunchbase and North
Ask questions, run analyses, and generate documents that combine Crunchbase data with your organization's internal knowledge.
Crunchbase brings more than seven million company profiles, funding round histories, and predictive intelligence on funding, growth, risk, and acquisition likelihood.
North
brings that data together with your internal systems, so teams can move from research to a finished document, a flagged risk, or a running automation in one place.
Investors and M&A
Spot early opportunities and risk across your pipeline with Crunchbase predictions on funding, growth, acquisitions, and potential IPOs. Then, use North to turn those signals into documents — like memos, target lists, or risk analyses — for companies you hold and set up
automations
to keep them up to date.
GTM strategy and market research
Reach deal close before your competitors do with private markets data that identifies the right companies and the right people to sell to, with your expertise on when to reach out. Combined with North, automate this work forward across the tools your team uses, whether that be checking a record in Salesforce, automating outreach, or building documents via natural language to support a potential deal.
Analysts
Use Crunchbase data to map markets and deliver insights faster, with less manual research, and predictions that help you appear ahead of the game. Combined with North, build workflows to surface new entrants as they move out of stealth and connect them to the channels where your work happens with North’s other default connectors, like Slack and Gmail.
Built for enterprise control
Crunchbase in North is bring-your-own-license, and the connector respects the entitlements and permissions that your organization already has in place across Crunchbase and North.
Every figure North pulls from Crunchbase comes with citations for quick human-in-the-loop verification. Predictive outputs are clearly identified as forecasts, and estimated valuations are distinguished from reported ones, so reviewers always know which numbers are observed and which are modeled.
Availability
The Crunchbase connector is available starting November 17, 2026 for North customers with an active Crunchbase license and single sign-on (SSO) enabled. Admins can enable it from their organization's connector settings.
