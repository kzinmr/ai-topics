The Brief: November 2026
by
Harvey Team
•
Nov 13, 2026
Welcome back to The Brief. This month's releases let agents take on routine work with the context of your matters and projects, keep large document reviews current as new files arrive, and ground answers in the sources your team already relies on. Here's what's new.
Delegate Routine Work to Agents That Know Your Context
Harvey II gives agents the context of each matter or project and memory of how you work, so you can hand off tasks and keep every workstream organized in one Space.
Harvey II
Hand off more of your work as agents draw on context from your matters and projects and remember how you prefer to work.
Learn more.
Updated Spaces
Keep every document, thread, agent, and task for a matter or project in one place with Spaces, rebuilt as the central home for your team's work.
Learn more.
Custom Agents and Agent Builder
Turn your team's processes and standards into reusable Custom Agents, so routine tasks follow your approach every time they run.
Learn more.
Space Templates
Start each new matter or project from your team's best setup by saving a Space, with its phases, tasks, agents, and resources, as a reusable template.
Learn more.
Agent Builder Enhancements
Build and iterate on custom agents faster by editing details directly on the page, adding files and sources from the same menus you use in threads, and using review tables as agent context.
Learn more.
Query a Space from a New Thread
Ask about a matter's materials without opening its Space by attaching a Space and a client matter to any query from the "Work in" selector.
Learn more.
Tagging a Space to Client Matter
Keep work tied to the right engagement by tagging each Space to a client matter in workspaces that use client matters.
Learn more.
Agent Nudges and Routing
Reference the right resources for better responses with Harvey suggesting relevant vaults, review tables, knowledge sources, connectors, and custom agents for your request. Learn more about
routing
and
custom agent nudges
.
Simplified Governance for Custom Agents and Workflows
Put new agents and workflows to work as soon as you save them, with admin approval applied only at the sharing levels your organization chooses and updated agent reporting in Command Center.
Learn more.
Templates in Library
Produce client- and court-ready documents and decks in your preferred format by uploading Word, PowerPoint, and Excel templates that Harvey applies automatically based on the task.
Learn more.
Create Outlook Drafts from Threads, Word, and Custom Agents
Get work back to clients and colleagues sooner by having Harvey draft emails (with attachments included), directly in your Outlook Drafts folder from a thread, the Word add-in, or a custom agent.
Learn more.
Simplified Navigation, Notifications Hub, and Refreshed Design
Find your agents, recent work, and Spaces from a redesigned sidebar and new Agents page, then see every task and comment that needs your attention in one Notifications hub. Learn more about
navigation
,
notifications
, and the
refreshed design
.
Composer Source Menu Update
Locate the files and knowledge sources you need more quickly through a simplified source menu in the composer, with dedicated entry points for each.
Learn more.
Spaces on Mobile and Mobile Navigation
Check on a matter from your phone by opening your Spaces, viewing and uploading files, and asking questions in Harvey mobile, now with a sidebar that mirrors the web app. Learn more about
Spaces on mobile
and
mobile navigation
.
Keep Large Document Reviews Current and Organized
Review tables and Vaults now keep pace with files as they arrive, so diligence, discovery, and contract reviews stay current without rebuilding tables or reorganizing folders by hand.
Review Table Sync
Keep a diligence or contract review current by connecting a review table to a Vault or selected folders, seeing when new documents land, and adding them all in one click.
Learn more.
Multi-Folder DMS Sync
Bring a whole matter's documents into one Vault by syncing multiple folders from iManage, SharePoint, and Google Drive instead of creating a Vault per folder.
Learn more.
Multi-select Cells
Verify, lock, or flag many review table answers in a single action instead of working cell by cell.
Learn more.
Agentic Vault File Organization
Reorganize a Vault by describing the folder structure you want in plain language from a new thread.
Learn more.
Vault Page and Thread Sharing Refresh
Start threads and review tables from a Vault with a single New button, and keep new threads private in your History until you choose to add them.
Learn more.
Review Table Starring
Return to the tables you use most by starring them and filtering the Review Tables homepage to show only starred tables.
Learn more.
Updated Suggestions in the Word Add-In
Work through a redline in one pass by scanning Harvey's suggested edits in a compact list and applying them all at once or one by one in the Word add-in. Learn more about
suggestions
.
Updated Look for Prompts in the Outlook Add-In
Get started quickly on the work landing in your inbox with an updated look for the default prompts in the Outlook add-in. Learn more about
prompts
.
Larger Excel Files
Upload cap tables, claims exports, and financial models without splitting them, with Excel files up to 200 MB now supported.
Learn more.
Ground Every Answer in Your Team's Own Sources
Get answers that reflect your team's full record by searching Teams chats, data rooms, your own documents in Outlook, and the in-house tools you connect through MCP.
Microsoft Teams Integration
Find negotiation context or build a chronology from chats by searching Teams channels, chats, and shared files from any thread.
Learn more.
Ansarada Integration
Run diligence on deal documents without downloading and re-uploading them by importing files from your Ansarada data rooms into threads and Vaults, or syncing a Vault to an Ansarada folder.
Learn more.
Gmail and Google Drive Integration
Let agents search Gmail and Google Drive for you, from any thread or on a schedule, and return answers with citations back to the source, with admins choosing which users get access. Learn more about
Gmail
and
GDrive search
.
BYO (Custom MCP)
Bring your own MCP servers directly in Harvey to connect proprietary tools, internal knowledge bases, and any third-party connector not already in the library, on your own terms.
Learn more.
Additional Sources in Harvey for Outlook
Answer client and colleague emails with full context by drawing on your organization's documents, jurisdictional research, and public web results directly from the Outlook add-in.
Learn more.
NetDocuments Document Selection
Analyze a broader set of NetDocuments files in a single workflow, now that you can now upload 200 documents at a time directly into Vault instead of 50.
Learn more.
Mobile Transcription Enhancements
Verify what was said in a recorded conversation by labeling speakers, editing transcript text, searching by keyword, and playing back audio line by line in Harvey mobile.
Learn more.
Track Regulatory Change and Research Across More Jurisdictions
Monitor regulatory developments, research patents, and work across borders with new local authority in Latin America, 265+ new sources worldwide, translation, and the latest frontier models in Harvey.
Horizon Scanning
Stay ahead of regulatory and legislative change by describing what to track in plain language, then assess each development and draft alerts or briefings in Harvey, available as an add-on.
Learn more.
Patent Research Knowledge Source
Run prior art searches, invalidity analysis, and competitive analysis against US patents, PTAB decisions, and Federal Circuit opinions without uploading files.
Learn more.
Tirant lo Blanch Knowledge Sources
Research and draft with authoritative case law and legislation from Tirant lo Blanch, now available for
Argentina, Brazil, and Colombia
and
Peru
.
265+ New Legal Research Sources
Ground research in current local authority with new sources across jurisdictions including the EU, UK, US, Brazil, India, South Korea, Singapore, and Switzerland. See the releases from
October 15
,
October 21
, and
November 3
.
Translate Documents with DeepL
Work across languages by asking Harvey to translate uploaded documents or pasted text from any thread, with translated files returned in their original formatting.
Learn more.
New Models
Choose the right model for each task with Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6 Sol, GPT-6 Luna, and Gemini 4 Argon, now in the model selector. Learn more about the
October 14 models
and
Gemini 4 Argon
.
Enforce Your Existing Walls, Retention Rules, and Access Controls
Risk, conflicts, and IT teams can extend the ethical walls, retention policies, and identity controls they already maintain into Harvey, and review activity and exports without a support ticket.
Ethical Walls for iManage SPM
Approve Harvey for walled matters by enforcing the walls you already manage in iManage Security Policy Manager on every share, with iManage remaining the system of record.
Learn more.
Document-Level Client Matters and Ethical Walls
Hold files from several matters in one Vault while each iManage file keeps its own client matter, so ethical walls hide files from anyone who isn't cleared.
Learn more.
Sync Client Matter Names
Find and tag the right matter by name rather than number, with client and matter names imported through your Intapp Walls for AI or iManage SPM sync.
Learn more.
Retention Policy Configurations
Manage workspace, Vault, and client matter retention from one Command Center page, and see a deletion estimate before you save any change.
Learn more.
Audit Logs in Command Center
Answer questions like who exported a Vault and when by viewing, filtering, searching, and exporting audit logs directly in Command Center, with no API integration required.
Learn more.
SCIM Guest Provisioning
Give colleagues outside legal, such as finance analysts on a vendor review, guest access through your SSO by mapping an Okta or Microsoft Entra ID group to the Guest role.
Learn more.
New Login Page
Sign in with just your work email or a remembered account, and Harvey routes you to your workspace's sign-in method across web, mobile, and the Word and Outlook add-ins.
Learn more.
