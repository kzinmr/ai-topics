I somehow missed this paper from last year analyzing the impact of International Building Code changes from 2012 to 2021 on construction costs. “
Between 2012 and 2021, the International Code Council (ICC) adopted 130 revisions to the International Building Code (IBC) affecting multifamily development. Using a detailed prototype of a three-story apartment building and holding material prices and labor productivity constant at 2024 levels, we find that 16 of these revisions raised national average construction costs by 9.9 percent relative to a 2009 IBC baseline, with fire protection requirements accounting for the largest share.
” [
SSRN
