I doubt that that will happen of course. OpenAI’s President Greg Brockman is a huge donor to Donald Trump. Josh Kushner, brother of the President’s son-in-law Jared Kushner, runs investment firm, Thrive, with many billions of dollars in OpenAI stock. (And now

.)
And so far the White House has done nothing. Instead, last night the President invited OpenAI’s to his state dinner. (Al Capone was unable to make it.)
History will undoubtedly judge us by what we did to contain the risks of AI.
An AI firm hacking a government website is a crisis.
Jensen Huang has given us perfectly clear logic for what we should do in such circumstances. But we aren’t doing it.
§
What we should do is temporarily shut OpenAI down, perhaps put them into  receivership until they clean up their act, and charge them with computer crimes. That’s what we do with humans who do this kind of thing, and it’s what we should with companies that do the same.
Journalists today should be calling Huang, asking “
does the Australian incident put OpenAI over the line? Would you in fact shut them down? If not, why not? And under what circumstances would you actually call for a shutdown of a lab? How bad would things have to get before you would? What would you do if a company repeatedly broke laws but the government did nothing to enforce them? Is liability and litigation really enough if a government won’t do its part?
”
And they should be asking Trump parallel questions: How far will his administration let AI-caused hacking to go, before he does in fact intervene?  How bad would things need to be?

To sum up, the time has come to shut OpenAI down, at least temporarily, and charge them with computer crimes.  (Or at least investigate.)
Their software has repeatedly been shown to be reckless, and because they have consistently covered things up we cannot trust them. They seem neither competent nor trustworthy.
If the federal government will do nothing about this situation, we can’t trust them, either.
And if we can’t trust them now, and we know that things will likely get worse, we are in serious trouble.
