Summary:
LearnVector is a new AI company from Andrew Ng, backed by a $100M investment from Coursera. The company's mission is to "accelerate human development" by building one-to-one learning experiences powered by AI.

Core thesis: Andrew Ng believes AI will not replace people but create unprecedented opportunities — and LearnVector helps people build skills so every person can take part.

Key features described on the website:
- Personalized learning paths that plan with the learner
- Adaptive AI that adjusts to how each person learns
- Patient, persistent tutoring that stays with the learner until mastery
- One-to-one learning experiences at scale

The company is positioned as an AI-native edtech platform, distinct from Coursera's MOOC model. It uses AI to provide individualized instruction that traditional online courses cannot.

Andrew Ng is Founder & CEO. The site emphasizes hiring and building the team.
