Manage your agents from Claude
More of the work of managing agents now happens inside AI assistants - and your tools should meet you there. Once connected, Claude gets a curated set of tools for ElevenAgents. We scope that set deliberately so Claude has a clear set of actions to work with and can combine them to complete full jobs on your behalf.
For example, Claude can:
Surface topics from recent conversations, then analyze and discuss them with you
Duplicate an agent for a new market, updating the voice and first message accordingly
Compare configurations across your agents and flag inconsistencies
Estimate an agent's expected LLM usage and cost before a change ships
Sign in once and stay in control
The new ElevenLabs MCP is hosted by ElevenLabs, so there's nothing external to run or maintain. Securely sign in using OAuth, with no API keys to manage, and revoke the connection at any time from your MCP client or your ElevenLabs account settings. Plus, using settings within Claude, you control what your agents, and your team's Claude sessions, can access.
