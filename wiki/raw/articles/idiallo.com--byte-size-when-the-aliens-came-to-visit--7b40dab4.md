# When the Aliens Finally Came to Visit

Source: https://idiallo.com/byte-size/when-the-aliens-came-to-visit

The aliens arrived, and their first instinct was to check our networks. They accessed one of our most important websites. A government website. Unfortunately, they were met with a reCAPTCHA.
Are you a robot?
They are not robots. But after fifteen minutes of squinting and trying to select all the fire hydrants and traffic lights, they gave up. Defeated by a checkbox, they returned to the stars.
