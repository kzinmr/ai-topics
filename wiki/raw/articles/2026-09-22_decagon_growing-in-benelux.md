We’re growing Decagon’s presence across Belgium, the Netherlands, and Luxembourg, building a local team to work more closely with customers, partners, and the broader AI ecosystem.
The region is home to globally minded companies that often operate across markets and languages from day one. We’re already partnering with brands that have set a high bar for customer experience, including
Rituals
, the Amsterdam-founded global luxury beauty and wellbeing brand.
I’m also in Amsterdam this week for
HumanX
, where I’ll be joining Kartik Sheth, Chief AI Officer at Deutsche Telekom, for a conversation on what it takes to move AI agents beyond the demo and into real enterprise environments.
When AI represents your brand, quality matters
For customer-facing AI, getting the answer right is only part of the job. The agent also has to sound natural, behave reliably, adapt across markets, and earn the trust of both customers and the teams operating it.
After previous chatbot-era tools failed to deliver the quality and consistency Rituals needed, the brand turned to Decagon to build customer experiences that could scale without losing what makes the brand distinctive.
With Decagon, Rituals launched Ray, its AI concierge, and expanded it across 19 countries and 15 languages in just two months. Ray has helped Rituals scale through even its busiest periods while maintaining a high-quality customer experience.
Rommy Verschelling, Head of Customer Relations at Rituals, shared:
“During peak holiday and Black Friday season, we can see up to 9,000 cases a day. With our agent, Ray, handling a significant portion of that volume, we had zero customer relations backlog for the first time ever.”
‍
Beyond handling volume, Ray is giving Rituals’ human ambassadors more time to focus on the high-touch moments that deepen customer relationships, while the team continues to improve the experience across markets and languages.
Built locally, experienced globally
For companies that serve customers across borders, AI agents need to adapt across languages and markets while delivering a consistent, high-quality experience everywhere.
