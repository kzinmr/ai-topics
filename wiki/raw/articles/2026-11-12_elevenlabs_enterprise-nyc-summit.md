For AI to be useful for customers and employees, it needs to be able to do three things. It has to be able to speak the way people do, fully understand the context of situations, and collaborate with people, handing off tasks and escalating when it reaches its limits.
To address all three, ElevenLabs is building the interaction layer for AI, from the models that power conversation to the agents that get work done.
“LLMs are incredibly intelligent; now it’s about figuring out how to keep up with capacity demands, how to make tokens more cost-effective, and how to make sure the models are serving people at the intelligence level they need,” said Ashley Kramer, Chief Revenue Officer at ElevenLabs.
“I believe that as we make these models, and in our
latest v4 models
, they are more emotive and more context-aware, able to hold context from call to call for that specific customer," said Kramer. "We’re going to enter an age where people will prefer to talk to an AI agent in certain cases, because you can get things done faster."
Enterprises now account for more than 55% of ElevenLabs’ revenue. Our technology is part of daily operations at five of the 10 largest tech companies, five of the 10 largest insurers, and four of the 10 largest telecoms.
“I think a year from now, we’ll be having a much different conversation, which is: what’s the next era of interaction?” Kramer said. “Now we’ve broken down a lot of these barriers, getting it out of pilot and into production, no matter the industry.”
The enterprise customers below show what putting those tools to work looks like in practice. From Yahoo’s voice-powered Scout and Westgate’s front-desk agent, to Ramp’s “Voice of Ramp,” and here’s what they’re building with ElevenLabs.
Onstage at NYC Summit 2026
At the ElevenLabs Summit in New York today, several clients and partners joined team members from ElevenLabs to discuss how they’re using ElevenAgents and other ElevenLabs models and products to bring natural, expressive, interactions to their services.
“The next wave, and where you really see enterprise adoption, is when technology starts adapting to the human and not the reverse,” Kramer said. “If you think about what’s happened in recent years, we’ve forced people to adapt to the technology: typing, touch screens. That took us away from talking to each other in the most natural way we do, which is voice.”
“This next wave allows us to leverage all of that intelligence and move into an interaction phase where the technology adapts to what the human needs,” Kramer added. “And that’s what we’re really excited to solve in this next era.”

Westgate takes the burden off front-desk staff
Westgate Resorts welcomes more than two million Owners and guests annually across nearly 60 resorts in North America. Delivering a great experience at that scale requires resort teams to respond to thousands of guest inquiries and service requests, ranging from reservations and concierge questions to housekeeping and maintenance needs. Westgate is continually looking for innovative ways to make those interactions faster, easier and more responsive. That includes using technology to provide convenient assistance around the clock while enabling Team Members to devote more time to the personal interactions that define the Westgate experience.
Using ElevenAgents, Westgate developed “Sarah,” a voice agent designed to provide fast, knowledgeable assistance with common front-desk inquiries. Sarah’s knowledge was enhanced using real Westgate call data, including 8,000 support transcripts, helping the technology deliver accurate, resort-specific responses that reflect the questions guests actually ask. Westgate launched Sarah at its 2,100-room Orlando resort, where the technology is helping create a more seamless guest experience, and is expanding the solution to two additional resorts as part of the company’s broader efforts to use innovation to enhance guest satisfaction and support its resort teams.
How Doppel is protecting against AI-enabled attacks
Doppel is a social engineering defense platform that helps organizations detect, defend against, and dismantle AI-enabled attacks. Doppel uses ElevenLabs voice AI models through the API to power realistic, controlled vishing (or voice phishing) simulations that test and harden the people attackers target. As voice-based attacks grow more convincing, Doppel chose ElevenLabs for its voice quality and the flexibility to simulate realistic attacker call flows.
“Attackers already use AI voices, so defenders have to train against the same realism. ElevenLabs gives our team the voice quality and flexibility to build phishing simulations that help organizations spot the fake before it costs them,” said Doppel Co-Founder & CTO Rahul Madduluri. “Our customers need to hear what an AI-enabled attack actually sounds like before it reaches them. Working with ElevenLabs helps us make that training realistic and scalable.”
KPN brings voice AI to Dutch small businesses
KPN, the largest telecom provider in the Netherlands, wanted voice AI in services that millions of Dutch consumers and businesses use every day. It recently launched Mobiel AI, in collaboration with ElevenLabs, which gives small business owners instant voicemail transcription, call summaries with suggested next steps, and smart call routing.
Dutch is a demanding language for voice models, with dense compound words and strong regional accents, and the details that matter most, like a correctly captured post code or date of birth, are exactly the details that are hardest for a transcription model to get consistently right.
ElevenLabs' engineering team worked directly with KPN to close that gap. ElevenLabs and KPN fine-tuned the model on Dutch language data, which raised identity-verification accuracy from roughly 50% to 90%. Every Dutch language deployment on the platform now benefits from that improvement.

Twilio gives its customers more human-sounding voice AI
Twilio is a customer engagement platform that enables developers to add voice calls, text messaging, video, and email to their applications, so businesses can engage with customers programmatically over those channels. It tapped ElevenLabs to power natural, real-time voice experiences at scale, integrating our voices into Conversation Relay — part of the company's conversational AI suite — for building conversational AI voice interactions directly on the Twilio platform.
Traditional text-to-speech often sounds robotic, but ElevenLabs voices adapt to context, sentiment, and pacing, and low-latency synthesis keeps conversations flowing in real time. The result is that Twilio’s customers can deliver expressive, human-like speech, fine-tune voice experiences for different languages and industries, and build more engaging voice interactions across markets.
