Marlow B. Hooper
Legal Engineer - Product Specialist at Harvey
Marlow is a Legal Engineer - Product Specialist and founding member of Harvey's office in Dallas. He partners with in-house legal, compliance, and government affairs teams to design and implement custom Harvey agents and workflows that handle complex, high-volume work. Before Harvey, Marlow spent five and a half years as Managing Counsel at Toyota's North American headquarters, supporting Toyota Financial Services and Toyota Insurance. While in-house, he advised on marketing & advertising, regulatory compliance, data privacy, and transactional matters. He began his legal career in the courtroom, first as a prosecutor and later in private practice. He holds advanced degrees from Brown, University of Missouri, and Florida A&M University College of Law.
