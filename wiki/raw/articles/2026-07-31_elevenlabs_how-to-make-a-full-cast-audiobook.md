What you need before you make a full-cast audiobook
Before you make an audiobook, you’ll need to have three things to work with. Everything else happens inside the creation flow.
Here’s what you need to make an audiobook:
A finished manuscript:
Any mistakes in your manuscript will carry over into your audiobook audio, meaning proofreading the entire book before processing is important.
A sense of character:
An understanding of which characters form your main cast. If you don’t have a clear outline of characters, ElevenCreative Audiobooks automatically detects them from the text and proposes a cast for you.
Character voice notes:
While optional, having clear notes about what a character should sound like will help expedite the process. We’ll circle back to this in step 2.
Unlike the traditional process of making an audiobook, you don’t need editing skills or production experience to create a high-quality final audiobook. The ElevenLabs Audiobook flow will walk you through every choice you need to make in order, showing you how to iteratively build a compelling audio representation of your novel.

Step 1: Upload your manuscript and select chapters
The first step to creating an audiobook is to upload your manuscript to ElevenCreative. We automatically parse the structure of your book, dividing it into sections and chapters based on what you upload. Having clear structural divides helps keep things organized both for you during the audiobook creation process and for your readers once the book is published.
After uploading, it’s time to select the chapters you want to work on. You could choose the entire book or start with a subsection, such as the first couple of chapters, to see how your characters feel in action.
At this stage, character detection and generation begins. ElevenCreative Audiobooks will read the text and identify which characters speak throughout, in preparation for the steps that follow.
Step 2: Cast your characters
This is where the new ElevenLabs flow departs from every audiobook process that came before it. Audiobooks infers the characters in your manuscript and finds the perfect voice for each one. With the Character Casting feature, casting for your audiobook transforms from a long, manual process into a single decision per character.
The pre-generated voice options provided for you are already filtered based on the character’s details in the text. Traits like their age, gender, tone, and narrative purpose will all help filter the
voice library
to match how the character reads on the page. We provide a shortlist of options that already fit the character.
Casting happens on your own novel’s text, allowing you to hear how each of the examples sounds when speaking lines of dialogue from your book. Recast a character once, and every paragraph they speak automatically updates.
Step 3: Set pronunciations once
ElevenCreative Audiobooks scans your manuscript for unusual names, places, and terms, and pre-fills a pronunciation dictionary with what it finds. You can assign a pronunciation once and watch as it’s applied across your entire audiobook.
Consider a fantasy town in your novel called “Halynwn.” Without a dictionary entry, a narrator would only be guessing at how it’s properly pronounced. Once you’ve added it to the dictionary with a guide, it’ll be correct across the board.
Step 4: Direct the delivery
Pacing shapes how your audiobook feels, creating consistent rules across every generation. A literary fiction novel may take longer, more deliberate pauses between paragraphs or sections. A fast-paced thriller might land on the more rapid end of the spectrum.
By making these decisions upfront, you bring a level of consistency to every single page, rather than repeating your editing configurations at the start of each chapter.
Step 5: Generate, review, and publish
With the cast of characters set and your voices ready to go, it’s time to generate your audiobook. For best results, review the output of each chapter. If you want to work on any line that doesn't sound right, you can regenerate that section by itself while keeping the rest of the book the same.
This step is only meant to be a quick editing pass, as the internal rules you laid out with pronunciation and pacing will have set the bar for consistency already.

What is a full-cast audiobook, and can you make one yourself?
A full-cast audiobook is where each character has a defined voice, instead of having a single narrator that performs every role. Dialogue plays out between different voices, while the narration itself has a distinct voice.
Traditionally, full-cast audiobooks were the most expensive to produce, only reserved for high-budget productions or specific audiobook editions. It meant you’d have to hire multiple actors, rent
studio
time for each of them, coach each one of them, and then coordinate with an audio designer to produce a final draft.
Character Casting by ElevenLabs changes the formula, allowing Audiobook flows to assign every paragraph to a character and every character to a voice. In just a few clicks, you’ll have a full-cast audiobook ready.
ElevenCreative is especially useful for any smaller publishing studios or authors who want to self-publish audiobooks.

Creating a full-cast Audiobook FAQ
What is Character Casting in Audiobooks on ElevenCreative?
Character Casting is a new step in the ElevenLabs Audiobooks flow that detects characters in your book and proposes a voice for each one. Every paragraph of your book is then voice-acted by the correct character, meaning your single approval carries you through the entire production process. From the first line of dialogue to the last, Character Casting supports full-scale voice acting.
How is Character Casting by ElevenLabs different from the old audiobook flow?
When building out an audiobook in a traditional workflow, you would have to assign a different voice from the full library to each paragraph. This would make it easy to end up with a stray voice on a single paragraph. With Characters, all voice work by a single character in your book is owned by that specific character voice.
What happens when I change a character’s voice?
When you assign a voice to a character, all of that person’s voice lines are regenerated in the new voice. ElevenCreative Audiobooks flags the change before you confirm it and handles the full-scale change automatically. With this flow, you can easily swap out voices that don’t fit. You’ll never be left hunting down individual paragraphs with rogue voices, with Character Casting providing full consistency to your novel.
Do I need voice acting or audio experience to make an audiobook this way?
One of the main benefits of the ElevenCreative Audiobooks flow is that you need absolutely no experience to make a high-quality audiobook. Across casting, pronunciation, pacing, generation, and exporting, we support you across every step of the journey. If you have a finished manuscript, then your audiobook is only a few steps away.
How do I self-publish my finished audiobook?
Once you’ve completed your audiobook and have exported it, you’re ready to upload it to retail and library platforms. You’ll only need to follow each platform’s file format and upload specifications.
