A voice can introduce a character before you see their face. A sound can make an ordinary scene feel unfamiliar. An image can suggest a world, and a few seconds of video can pull you into it.
Today, ElevenLabs is launching The Year of More, an ElevenCreative competition with $200,000 in prizes. It’s open to free and paid users, and the $50,000 Grand Prize will be announced on December 1, 2026. Voice is where ElevenLabs began, and creators can now use ElevenCreative to work across image, video, and audio in a single finished piece.
Show us what more looks like
The brief is simple: create something with images or video that shows ElevenCreative is more than voice. That could be a short film built around a single line of dialogue, an ad for a product you’ve imagined, or a series of images that tells a story without words. You might start with a voice and build a world around it, or start with a visual and find out what it should sound like.
Treat these as starting points and take them wherever you want. The tools are part of the process, but the creative decisions are yours. A character’s delivery, the timing of a cut, and the sound beneath a scene give a piece its point of view.
$200,000 in creator prizes
The Year of More is open to free and paid users, and no purchase is required to enter. Judges will pick 5 winners at $10,000 each on November 20, 25 and 30, and announce a $50,000 Grand Prize on December 1. Submissions close November 30 at 11:59pm PT. The
official competition rules
cover eligibility, judging criteria, prize details, and submission deadlines.
Whether you’re trying a new format or finally developing an idea you’ve been sitting on for months, we’d love to see what you make.
How to enter
Create your piece.

Submit your entry.
Add your post links and tell us how you used ElevenCreative in the official entry form.

competition page

New Pro subscribers get 55% off annual plans, and monthly Pro subscribers who switch to annual get 50% off.
