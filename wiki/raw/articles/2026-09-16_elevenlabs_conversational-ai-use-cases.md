Businesses across all industries are adopting conversational AI to help with customer service, sales, scheduling, employee support, and other everyday interactions.
McKinsey's 2026 survey
found nearly nine in ten organizations now use AI in at least one business function, and 44% report AI scaling across the enterprise, up from 38% the year before.
Conversational AI use cases include answering questions, retrieving information, completing routine requests, and guiding users through multi-step processes. These are specific business tasks that AI agents handle from end to end or transfer to a person when human judgment is needed.
The examples below cover the most common conversational AI use cases across business functions, followed by industry-specific applications and practical guidance for choosing and deploying them.

The best use cases reduce friction for customers or employees while giving the AI clear boundaries for what it should handle and when to involve a person.

The examples below get into specifics and show how businesses are applying conversational AI to workflows specific to their customers and operations.
Healthcare
Healthcare organizations use conversational AI for administrative conversations around care, including patient intake, appointment scheduling and reminders, billing questions, and follow-up outreach. Agents can connect to scheduling and EHR systems to retrieve information or complete eligible actions, while clinical questions follow established escalation rules.
For example, CareCode uses ElevenAgents for patient communications, including appointment scheduling, confirmations, reminders, and routine questions. CareCode found that
43.9% of callers
had already tried another scheduling channel before turning to voice AI, showing how voice AI can give patients another way to complete scheduling when digital channels don’t meet their needs.
Banking and financial services
Banks and financial services companies use conversational agents for account and transaction questions, card services, transfers, onboarding, and customer support. After appropriate authentication, an agent retrieves information from banking systems or routes requests that require a specialist.
Customers Bank is rolling out
conversational AI across customer and employee support
, including 24/7 help with account questions, card services, and transfers, guided onboarding for new customers, and real-time assistance for employees during live calls. The rollout is designed to give customers faster answers, shorter hold times, and consistent access to support outside normal business hours, while keeping human escalation available when a conversation needs it.
Insurance
Insurance policyholders often reach out during high-stress events like auto accidents or property damage, where tone and responsiveness matter just as much as speed. Conversational AI voice agents can detect vocal tone and emotional context, adjusting their delivery to match caller sentiment while gathering claim details, answering policy questions, and retrieving status updates from connected databases.
Insurely uses voice AI
to recover abandoned quotes, answer routine questions, and provide 24/7 support, while emotional context detection helps the agent adjust its response to how the customer sounds. The result is more completed quotes, higher sales, and lower support volume.
Retail and e-commerce
Retailers use conversational AI before and after a purchase, for everything from helping shoppers choose products to tracking orders and handling returns. Conversational agents also answer questions about availability, guide shoppers toward suitable products, recover abandoned carts, or help with order changes.
Getmobil, Turkey’s leading marketplace for refurbished electronics, uses AI voice agents to answer product questions, track orders, and assist with returns. Routine requests are resolved during customer conversations, while issues that require judgment or account-specific review are transferred to a human representative. The retailer is also expanding voice AI into outbound customer calls, vendor onboarding, and collection scheduling.
After
implementing AI agents
across customer service, Getmobil reports three times faster response times, 75% of customer queries resolved without human escalation, and a
60% reduction in contact-center costs
. Getmobil also reports more consistent customer service around the clock.
Hospitality and restaurants
Hotels and restaurants use conversational agents to handle reservations, booking changes, guest questions, takeout orders, and questions about menus or venue policies. AI voice agents also give businesses a way to answer customer calls after hours or when on-site staff are busy serving guests.
SevenRooms reports that its voice AI has handled more than 400,000 calls across 600+ restaurants, with one in five calls occurring after hours and venues seeing
up to 25% more reservations
.
Real estate and property management
Conversational AI helps real estate teams respond to inquiries, qualify leads, and book viewings while prospects are still engaged. They also collect details such as budget, preferred property type, and timing before sending qualified leads to the right agent, giving sales teams useful context before they take over the conversation.
Property management companies use conversational agents for a different set of interactions, including answering routine tenant questions, handling maintenance requests, and taking after-hours calls.
Anarock real estate consultants use
AI voice agents
to qualify real estate leads, re-engage inactive prospects, and handle overflow calls when sales teams are unavailable. In one financial year, the company reported
8.5 million successful AI voice interactions
versus 1.7 million human interactions the previous year.
Education
Conversational AI gives students faster access to academic help and support outside scheduled classes or counseling sessions. Voice-based tutoring can explain difficult material aloud, while student-support tools handle outreach and counseling conversations at scale. Multilingual voice also
makes those interactions easier
to access for students who are more comfortable learning in another language.
Physics Wallah uses voice AI across tutoring and support tools for learners preparing for competitive exams such as JEE, NEET, and government entrance tests. Its Ask AI tool delivers spoken explanations for academic questions, while other voice applications support counseling and student outreach. Voice users generate nearly three times as many queries per session as non-voice users, and Physics Wallah reports Day 7 retention is 2.2 times higher and
Day 15 retention is 2.4 times higher among voice users

Utilities and telecom
Conversational AI handles routine utility and telecom requests about billing, service changes, outages, and account questions through voice or chat. For technical issues, conversational agents guide customers through first-line troubleshooting, such as restarting a router, checking connection status, or confirming service settings.
Deutsche Telekom, Europe's largest
telecommunications
company, integrates AI voice directly into its network so subscribers can use a call assistant to answer questions, retrieve information, perform tasks in real time, translate conversations, and create call summaries for later review. The project also shows
what telecom providers require from voice AI
at a carrier scale: low latency, high concurrency, strong audio quality, security, and reliability.
As Christoph Hilz, Group SVP Core Network & Services at Deutsche Telekom, puts it: “It is about jointly learning how to make AI carrier-grade: fast enough for the AI-native world, but trusted, secure, and reliable for Deutsche Telekom and our customers.”
Automotive and repair services
Dealerships and automotive businesses use conversational AI to answer questions about available vehicles, follow up with leads, schedule test drives, and book service appointments. The agents check current vehicle availability and open appointment times, then transfer qualified leads or service requests to the appropriate dealership team.
For the launch of its XUV 7XO, Mahindra used AI voice agents for
large-scale customer outreach

Government and public services
Public agencies use conversational AI for informational and administrative services such as explaining processes, helping residents locate services, answering routine questions, scheduling appointments, and routing requests. Requests involving protected records or decisions that require a
government

Ukraine's State Employment Service uses a voice agent in its contact center to answer routine questions around the clock and transfer callers when human review is needed. In its first 80 days, the agent handled about 25% of total call volume and
reduced average answer time to 18 seconds
, down from 30–40 seconds.

Conversational AI use case FAQs
What is a typical use case for conversational AI?
Customer service is one of the most common conversational AI use cases. Voice and chat agents answer routine questions, retrieve account or order information, help customers troubleshoot problems, complete defined requests, and transfer more complex conversations to a human representative.
What are some examples of conversational AI?
Examples include a voice agent scheduling a patient appointment, a banking agent retrieving an account balance, or a retail agent checking an order status or helping with a return. Businesses use
conversational AI
through voice or chat, depending on how customers or employees need to interact with them.
What are the benefits of conversational AI in business?
Conversational AI gives customers and employees faster access to information, extends service beyond staffed hours, supports conversations in multiple languages, and handles routine requests consistently. When agents connect to business systems, they also complete tasks such as booking appointments, checking orders, or updating account information during the conversation, reducing the need for customers and employees to switch channels or wait for manual assistance.
How do you implement conversational AI?
Start with a specific use case and define what the agent needs to accomplish. Give it access to approved company information and the business tools required for the task, then establish rules for when to transfer a conversation to a person. Test realistic conversations before launch and continue reviewing performance after deployment.
