# Zhipu AI — company reference

Zhipu AI (trade name **Z.ai**; native name 北京智谱华章科技有限公司 / "Beijing Zhipu Huazhang Technology Co., Ltd.") is a Chinese artificial intelligence company and one of China's most important frontier AI labs. It is the maker of the **GLM** series of large language models.

## Company facts (Wikipedia, Aug 2026)

- **Founded**: 2019
- **Founders**: Tang Jie, Li Juanzi (both Tsinghua University-affiliated researchers; Zhipu originated from Tsinghua's knowledge-engineering group)
- **Headquarters**: Beijing, China
- **Key people**: Zhang Peng (CEO)
- **Type**: Public company — traded on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange as **SEHK: 2513**
- **Employees**: 800+ (2024)
- **Products**: GLM (AI) — the GLM family of models
- **Websites**: https://zhipuai.cn/ and https://z.ai/

## GLM model family

Zhipu's GLM line has tracked the open-weight frontier through 2025–2026: GLM-5.1, GLM-5.2, GLM-5.3 (released 2026-08-14, "Frontier Coding with Emergent Cyber Capabilities"), and GLM-5V-Turbo (vision). The consumer product is the Z.ai chatbot/agent, powered by GLM-5.2 at time of scrape.
