# Third Bridge Now Available in Hebbia

**Source**: [https://www.hebbia.com/blog/hebbia-and-third-bridge-launch-strategic-integration-to-enhance-financial](https://www.hebbia.com/blog/hebbia-and-third-bridge-launch-strategic-integration-to-enhance-financial)

Third Bridge Now Available in Hebbia
Partnerships
By Paul Boland
07.15.25
Hebbia now integrates with Third Bridge, bringing expert network insights directly into its AI platform.
Hebbia —the leading AI platform for finance and law— today announced an integration with Third Bridge, a leading global expert network and investor-centric provider of research and market intelligence. The integration delivers Third Bridge’s expert insights directly within the Hebbia platform, giving users a decisive edge in decision-making.
Third Bridge provides investors and business leaders with access to the unique expert insights they need to accelerate and improve their decision-making. Its network of industry experts includes high-profile figures from the private and public sectors, academia, and leading economists.
With the launch of this integration, users can easily augment the insights generated from public documents with those derived from Third Bridge's unparalleled library of expert interviews, via their existing research workflow. For example, analysts performing buy-side due diligence during an acquisition can now benefit from Third Bridge's expert interviews to provide essential context that might otherwise be missing from the documents available at hand.
With this integration, Hebbia customers receive direct, real-time insights from industry experts, allowing them to rapidly validate assumptions, uncover nuanced risks, and gain an edge in decision-making that standard research doesn’t offer.
“At Hebbia, we’re always listening to our top customers and building the integrations they need to stay ahead. Bringing Third Bridge’s library of expert interviews directly into Hebbia means our users can seamlessly cross-reference industry insights with VDRs and deal materials in real time—sharpening their diligence, speeding up the investment process, and driving to conviction faster than ever.” - Danny Wheller, VP of Business Strategy & Growth
Mergen Davaapil, Managing Director at Third Bridge, added, “We’re committed to providing the world’s decision makers with access to the high-quality insights they need, when and wherever they need them. Together with Hebbia, we’re giving our clients a faster, more streamlined way to gain real insights, from real people as part of their existing research workflows.”
