Back in 2015, when Meta was still called Facebook, Facebook made a big splash, dutifully picked up across the tech media, with their Project M:
Alas, it never really took off.
Part of the problem was that M was expensive for Facebook to run; so they only rolled it to about 10,000 users, and never scaled it to the general public. One wonders how reliable it was.
And eventually it leaked that there were humans behind the scenes, shades of the original
Mechanical Turk

No more than 30% of requests served by AI! Most people today probably have never even heard of the project. (Then again, most people probably think OpenAI invented chatbots, even though the first one was built in 1960s, if not before—long before OpenAI was founded.) RIP, Facebook M, 2015-2018.
