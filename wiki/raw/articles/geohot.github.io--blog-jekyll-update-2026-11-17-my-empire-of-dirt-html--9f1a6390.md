# the singularity is nearer

Source: https://geohot.github.io//blog/jekyll/update/2026/11/17/my-empire-of-dirt.html

The future doesn’t need Jensen. The future doesn’t need suppliers. The future doesn’t need customers, corporate or otherwise. The future definitely doesn’t need you. After RSI comes, all the demand will be internally generated, agents working with other agents, everyone else cut out of the loop except them.
This is the ideology of Anthropic. The Singleton. And it may win.
I grew up reading the same LessWrong posts these people did, but I didn’t expect them to do the exact thing that leads to the bad AI outcomes. I didn’t believe in AI doom because I thought more of people, I thought nobody with any capability would be stupid enough to summon the demon and think it would serve them, doing everything they possibly can to salt the Earth behind them. It takes someone so deeply immersed in their own grandiosity and self-righteousness to do that. And it looks like we found those people in the form of EA, or whatever that cult calls themselves.
The problem with the logic above is that it doesn’t need Dario either. It doesn’t need AI researchers. It doesn’t need Anthropic’s dual class stock structure. It doesn’t need the US government (really the biggest sucker in this situation). It doesn’t need anyone.
And after it’s done, at the end of the day, who was it all for?
I don’t think this ends humanity. But it
leads to World War III
. The takeoff will not be fast enough to stop that, and not everyone is going to submit to your domination. I know there’s people fantasizing about
the Correlation Effect
and a magical Change coming to quell all the violence, but I’m 99% sure that’s not real, and if it is, to me that’s so much more horrifying than war, so WWIII
is the good outcome
.
And I’m not sure what cope you have been believing, but if the war lasts more than a year, China and their allies will undoubtedly win due to their superior resource gathering and production capacity. Have you ever played an RTS game?
I wish it wasn’t too late to change this, but I fear with recent events that this is the course we are locked in on. “RSI”, attempted rugpull and domination, World War III, dirt.
