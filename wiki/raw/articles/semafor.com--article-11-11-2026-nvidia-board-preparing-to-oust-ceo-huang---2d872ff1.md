# Nvidia board preparing to oust CEO Jensen Huang, name interim CEO

Source: https://www.semafor.com/article/11/11/2026/nvidia-board-preparing-to-oust-ceo-huang-wsj

Some members of Nvidia’s board favor appointing Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress as interim CEO at the beleaguered chipmaker if CEO and founder Jensen Huang is ousted, according to people familiar with the matter.
The full board has yet to make a decision. But the company’s directors are scheduled to meet Wednesday evening, these people said.
Semafor earlier confirmed that Nvidia’s board is
preparing to oust Huang
as soon as Wednesday, after media reports detailed that he had a second extramarital affair. The Nvidia board briefed the White House on its planned meeting, according to a senior administration official.
Nvidia has tapped an
outside search firm
to find the company’s next CEO, Bloomberg reported. One person being considered for the top job is Apple hardware chief Johny Srouji, who was among the executives considered for Intel CEO in 2019, according to another person familiar with the matter.
