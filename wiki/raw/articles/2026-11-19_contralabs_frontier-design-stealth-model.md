 Real design projects, judged by designers and scored by rubrics 
 Frontier Design Benchmark is the first benchmark built on real world, high value, and complex design projects. It is judged by professional designers in the Contra Labs network, ensuring the quality of evaluation. 
 In the benchmark, we also built a design critique rubric and a list of AI slop patterns. The rubric scores are highly correlated with the designers’ ratings of the models (r = 0.94). These additional metrics provide explainable design quality judgement on top of pairwise preferences.
