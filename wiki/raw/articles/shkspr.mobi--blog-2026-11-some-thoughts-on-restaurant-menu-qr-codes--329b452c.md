# Some thoughts on restaurant menu QR codes

Source: https://shkspr.mobi/blog/2026/11/some-thoughts-on-restaurant-menu-qr-codes/

Is anyone else SICK AND TIRED of the way hotels now foist plastic "keycards" on guests?
No! I don't want tap to open my door!
No! I don't want to keep the card in my wallet!
No! I don't want electronics in control of my life!
What if I need to stab an attacker? Keys are great for that by flimsy plastic cards are useless
Gives us PROPER keys with big keyrings that we have to hand in to reception when we go out and then ask for when we come back.
That is
exactly
what you sound like when you complain about restaurant menus now being available by QR code.
My wife and I took a
7 week Interrail through Europe
. While most places still had boring paper menu, a decent number had QR codes which allowed us to see the menu and order food. And they were
brilliant
!
An electronic menu offers so many advantages. Being able to instantly translate any terms by selecting the text is much easier than pointing a camera and trying to focus on a laminated bit of card.
That said, most of the online menus were already fully translated. They had all the legally mandated allergen information. Many of them showed when something was out of stock.
We had much the same experience visiting Japan
- getting to see a photo or video about the dish you're going to order is surprisingly useful.
The ones which allowed you to order were even better. So easy to select the right thing - no hoping that the waiter didn't mishear "no cheese" as "extra cheese". Notes to the kitchen about allergies were often supported. Oh, and no trying to catch the waiter's eye to let them know you're ready to order.
Speaking of which, no trying to catch the waiter's eye when you want to pay! Several of online platforms allowed for payment through the Web. Most supported splitting the bill between a group - so no more trying to work out who ate what.
So let's talk about the down-sides.
"What if there's no signal?!?!?" Get a roaming SIM, chief. But, as it happens, every restaurant had fast and free WiFi. Because, you know, they understand the service they're providing.
"I don't want a bloody app!!!!" Me neither. I didn't encounter any which required me to install a one-time app.
"Online ordering tries to upsell to me and I have no self control...." Sure. But waiters often recommend the most expensive wine to go with their cheapest dish.
"How can I flirt with a waitress if everything is mediated through a screen?????" Please reconsider your life choices.
"But I hate progress and love paper!" OK. In just about every restaurant I saw people being handed paper menus. Or wait staff explained what each dish was.
"Yes, but in the worst case scenario I've constructed in my head I have no signal, no phone, and no alternatives - and now I'm irrationally angry about that!" So walk out. Seriously, you'd leave a restaurant if the waiter was rude, or you saw a rat, or the food didn't come for an hour. Do the same if you're forced to use a service that you hate.
As I've said before,
I love living in the future
. I can't be bothered with fully grown adults throwing a tantrum over every bit of progress.
