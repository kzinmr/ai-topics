Anthropic announced **Claude Duet**, a "research preview" proactive personal
assistant available on Pro and Max plans in the US. Product framing:
"Get back to what needs you."

Key characteristics from the product page:

- **Proactive agent**, not a chat loop: "Claude Duet is a proactive agent that
  understands what needs your attention. It takes on tasks of all sizes, keeps
  working while you're away, and waits for your ok when it matters."
- **Powered by Opus 5.5**; surfaces in a single thread inside the Claude app.
- **Morning brief**: every morning it briefs the user, drafts next steps, then
  goes quiet until something needs the human. Example brief items: an expiring
  job offer, a redlined $410K renewal, a health permit that needs approval +
  a $640 fee, a Series A partner meeting deck with two slides flagged, 27
  mis-delivered orders with refunds drafted.
- **Human-in-the-loop approval gates**: monetary/payment approvals and
  irreversible actions are surfaced as "Approve / Not now" cards (e.g. paying a
  county health-permit fee), rather than being executed silently.
- **Long-horizon delegation**: "Send a message and it sets up a meeting or
  builds a dashboard, then shares when it's done."
- **Continuity from the Claude app**: memory, skills and apps carry over into
  Duet from the first message.
- **6,000+ connectors and plugins.**
- **User-built dashboards**: "Marlo Pulse" — a one-page daily health check
  assembled from point of sale, Shopify, CRM, support inbox, payroll and bank,
  refreshing before the morning brief; the agent proposes follow-up experiments
  (e.g. free-shipping threshold A/B test) when it detects a metric change
  (East Bay churn 2.6% → 3.4% after a shipping price change).

Positioning: Duet is Anthropic's answer to the "ambient / proactive personal
agent" category — the same territory as OpenAI's personal-agent push and
Glean's headless agent builder. The differentiators it leads with are the
morning-brief cadence, explicit approval gates on money/irreversible actions,
and reuse of the existing Claude memory/skills/connectors surface.
