# Stop Building Software Factories

**Speaker:** Justin McCarthy, Founder and CEO of Diffusion

## Description

Most of the time you don't know what you want yet, so putting new ideas through a factory wastes time and tokens. Justin McCarthy, founder and CEO of Diffusion, argues for brownfield factories instead: continuous loops that keep the promises of software customers already pay for, getting faster, cheaper and safer around the clock. He explains why these factories simplify with every model release and why one takes about a week to stand up. Then he raises the stakes: with code now abundant, engineers need to build a demand factory that finds what customers will actually buy.
