Peter Duffy
Founder and CEO at TITANS
Peter Duffy is founder and CEO of TITANS, where he advises leading law firms and corporates on AI strategy, technology investment and large-scale transformation. He previously led emerging technology at DWF, then the world’s largest listed law firm. With over a decade working in AI, he was among the first 10,000 users of GPT-3 in 2020 and advised on generative AI well before ChatGPT launched. His Legal Tech Trends newsletter is followed by 5,000+ readers for his legaltech and AI market insights.
