Creating an audiobook means getting the details right. A character’s name has to sound the same in chapter 12 as it did in chapter one, and a door slam has to land on the right word.
What’s new in audiobooks: pronunciation, sound, and timeline editing
ElevenCreative has been improving that workflow in
Studio
. The updates include a new pronunciation dictionary sidebar, sound effects and music on the timeline, and controls that keep related audio aligned as you edit.
Preview and manage pronunciations in a sidebar
The pronunciation dictionary has moved from a popup into a full sidebar, available in Settings and Studio. You can open it from any project and manage your rules while you work.
Changes save automatically. Voice previews let you hear each rule, and matching text highlights in real time so you can see where it applies.
Add sound effects and music
Studio now supports
sound effects
and music on the timeline. Generate or select a sound effect, then drag it into place alongside your narration. You can use it to set the atmosphere of a scene or place a sound at a specific moment in the story.
Keep related audio together
Magnetic snapping helps you align items across timeline rows. Linked clips move related elements as one, so a sound effect tied to a paragraph stays with it when you rearrange your audiobook.
Hear your audiobook sooner with streaming playback
Pressing play no longer means waiting for a whole paragraph to generate first. The new playback engine starts playing audio while it’s still being generated.
