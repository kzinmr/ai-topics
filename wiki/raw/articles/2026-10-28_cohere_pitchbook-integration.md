# PitchBook in North

**Source**: [https://cohere.com/blog/pitchbook-integration](https://cohere.com/blog/pitchbook-integration)

We're excited to announce the launch of the
PitchBook Data Premium Connector
in North, bringing trusted private market intelligence directly into your existing AI workflows.
With the PitchBook Premium Connector in North, users can draw on PitchBook's institutional-grade data on companies, deals, funds, investors, market benchmarks, and more. Simply bring your PitchBook license, authenticate, and start equipping agents with PitchBook data.
Using PitchBook and North
Ask questions, run analyses, and generate drafts that combine PitchBook data with your organization's internal knowledge.
Corporate development and M&A:
Research transactions and draft memos that pair PitchBook financials with your internal strategy docs.
Strategy and market research:
Map and automate competitive landscapes, track where capital is flowing in your space, and size markets with cited data you can refresh anytime.
Finance and investor relations:
Benchmark valuations, funding rounds, and peer growth to draft board updates and investor materials grounded in current market data in North.
Sales and partnerships:
Research prospects and partners before outreach, from recent funding and investors to leadership changes and growth signals, then use North to turn what you find into tailored messaging.
Executive briefings:
Turn a leadership question into a sourced one-pager in minutes by combining PitchBook data with North's document mode.
Built for enterprise control
PitchBook in North is bring-your-own-license, and the connector respects the entitlements and permissions your organization already has in place across PitchBook and North. Every figure North pulls from PitchBook comes with citations for quick human-in-the-loop verification.
Availability
The PitchBook connector is available starting October 26, 2026 for North customers with an active PitchBook license and Single-Sign-On (SSO) enabled. Admins can enable it from their organization's connector settings.
To learn more, contact your Cohere account team, or talk to sales if you're new to North. Don’t have a PitchBook license? Visit
PitchBook
to learn more.
