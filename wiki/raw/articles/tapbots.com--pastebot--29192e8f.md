# Pastebot 3 for Mac

Source: https://tapbots.com/pastebot/

Pastebot 3
Command Copy & Paste
Pastebot is a
powerful
clipboard manager that is
fun
& easy to use. Try for yourself or keep scrolling and see what you are missing!
Clipboard History
At its most basic level, Pastebot keeps a running history of everything you copy so you can easily find and paste again.
Start typing to quickly search for a clipping.
View useful metadata attached to clippings and add your own searchable notes.
View clipping details in a resizable preview window.
Add apps to the Blacklist and Pastebot will never store what you copy from them.
Custom Pastebins
Organize and store important and often-used clippings forever in Custom Pastebins and create rule-based Smart Pastebins.
Select a color and icon or emoji for your Custom Pastebins so they are more easily identifiable.
Create Smart Pastebins for quick access to clippings filtered by rules.
Add searchable notes to clippings to help find them faster.
Quick Paste Menu
The quick paste menu will shortly become an essential tool on your Mac. Just
in any app and the power of Pastebot springs up at your fingertips.
Paste any of the last 10 copies in Pastebot by hitting a number key.
Create quick paste key shortcuts for a custom pastebin or cycle between them with ⌘← or →.
Toggle a preview window that can be resized and docked to 3 sides of the quick paste menu.
Powerful Filters
The real power of Pastebot is revealed with filters. Create custom filters and quickly apply them before pasting.
Create complex filters by stacking base filters.
Create keyboard shortcuts to commonly used filters.
Open a preview window and watch your filters function live as you build them.
Create a Stack
Ever needed to copy multiple items and then paste one after another in sequence? Stacks allow you to do just this.
Hit the → arrow key on a clipping to add it to the stack.
Or with the stack window open, anything you copy gets added to the stack.
Paste each clipping in the stack sequentially with ⌃⌘V (or a hotkey of your choice).
Okay, deep breath. There’s more…
iCloud Sync
Use multiple Macs? Turn on iCloud Sync in Pastebot and have your clippings, custom pastebins, and filters stay in sync between computers.
Keyboard Shortcuts
Pastebot lets you assign keyboard shortcuts to individual clippings, filters, and more. All your shortcuts live in Settings, so you can 	review or change them anytime.
Blacklists
Keep sensitive or noisy apps out of Pastebot. Block an app entirely, or choose to ignore images, text, or shortcuts individually.
Universal Clipboard
Pastebot works seamlessly with Universal Clipboard. When it’s enabled and active, anything you copy on your iPhone is imported into Pastebot automatically. And any clipping in Pastebot is ready to paste on your phone.
Preview Window
A larger preview of any clipping is always a spacebar away, especially useful for images. Keep the preview open and it'll update as you browse.
Shortcuts & CLI
Pastebot supports both Apple Shortcuts and CLI access so you can automate clippings visually or from the terminal, whichever fits your workflow.
(Access via the Terminal: /Applications/Pastebot.app/Contents/Helpers/pastebot-tool)
Cheatsheet
Becoming a keyboard commander can take some time. Leave open Pastebot's handy cheatsheet so you can command copy and paste like a pro in no time.
