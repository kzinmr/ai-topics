# Progress on provable inference for large language models

Source: https://www.anthropic.com/research/provable-inference — HN: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50368626 (238 pts, 121 comments, Nov 4 2026)

Core problem statement (Anthropic): "When a user sends a request to an AI model, today they have no way of knowing for certain that the model they selected is the one that generated the response." The work builds cryptographic attestation that a response was generated by a specific committed set of weights; an integer (fixed-point) version is empirically tested to stay within a small error rate of true floating-point inference (not covering adversarially designed prompts). Repo docs: for open-weight models the provider commits to public weights and publishes the seed, so anyone can recompute the commitment from the Hugging Face files and verify the exact checkpoint and quantization claimed (demonstrated with a Maverick demo). Current limits: greedy decoding and short contexts only; compute overhead reported as ~90–100x.
