Harvey’s Commitment to Supporting Government Lawyers
Legal AI that meets public institutions’ security requirements can help government lawyers work through records and find institutional knowledge faster, making more time for analysis and advice.
by
Pat Manion
•
Nov 18, 2026
I have spent my career at the intersection of law, public policy, and technology. Over the past fifteen years, I have had the good fortune to work alongside — and across the table from — some of the most capable and dedicated lawyers in government. What has stayed with me is the sense of purpose they bring to their work: a deep commitment to serving others and taking on matters whose impact extends far beyond themselves. These roles demand deep subject-matter expertise, sound judgment, and an understanding of the institutions those lawyers serve.
Government lawyers perform some of the most consequential work in our profession. They advise the institutions responsible for delivering public services, help determine how public resources are spent, and handle complex litigation and investigations on behalf of the public. Their decisions can affect individuals, businesses, and communities far beyond the immediate matter in front of them.
This is why serving government lawyers matters to us at Harvey.
The Opportunity for Legal AI in Government
We’re working to transform the practice of law and improve the lives of lawyers everywhere. We cannot do that if we don’t support
public sector lawyers
. Our public sector lawyers deserve access to the best tools our profession has to offer, designed and deployed in safe and secure ways that meet the demands of their work. For Harvey, improving legal practice includes helping public institutions make better use of the expertise of the lawyers who work within them.
Those lawyers often carry substantial workloads, with competing demands on their time and limited flexibility to add capacity. Alongside the work that requires their judgment, they spend hours reviewing records, assembling facts, locating prior analysis, and preparing initial drafts. When experienced colleagues retire or move to new roles, deciphering the institutional knowledge behind an earlier decision can become a project of its own.
These tasks are essential to legal practice, but they also create opportunities for better tools to make a meaningful difference. Helping a lawyer work through a large record or locate relevant institutional knowledge can give them more time for substantive analysis, to test an argument, advise a colleague, or prepare for a consequential decision that affects the public.
That is the opportunity we see for legal AI in government. Its value should be measured by how well it supports the work lawyers actually need to do and the responsibilities they carry. Understanding that work requires listening closely to government legal teams and building alongside them.
Our Investment in Trust and Security
Supporting
government lawyers
also requires substantial and sustained investment in trust, security, and government-grade engineering. Public institutions have specific obligations concerning sensitive information, access, accountability, and the use of technology. Serving them means developing the security capabilities, legal expertise, and implementation support necessary to operate within those requirements. We do not take this responsibility lightly.
Lawyers must also be able to evaluate AI-assisted work and remain responsible for the legal judgments and decisions that follow, which is why Harvey is built with tools in place to provide users with visibility and audibility into the work Harvey performs.
Our achievement of
FedRAMP Class D (High) certification
and completion of an
IRAP assessment at the PROTECTED level
are steps in that commitment. The work continues as we help government teams assess where Harvey can be useful and establish how to use it effectively within their institutions.
Government lawyers have devoted their careers to work that affects all of us. Helping them apply more of their time and expertise to that work belongs at the center of Harvey’s mission. We intend to earn their trust through the tools we build, the care we take, and the support we provide.
