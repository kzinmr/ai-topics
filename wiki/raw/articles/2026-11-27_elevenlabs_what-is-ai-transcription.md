What is AI transcription: How it works and what it captures
AI transcription converts spoken audio into written text using speech to text technologies. Modern AI transcription includes speaker tags, timestamps, and event tags to enrich the generated transcript. Organizations use AI transcription software to augment manual transcription or note-taking, allowing marketers, brand strategists, and content creators to focus on high-value tasks.
AI transcription relies on deep learning and natural language processing to produce accurate, contextually consistent, and structured transcripts. Some AI transcribers also use generative AI for post-processing, such as summarizing, searching, and extracting specific topics.
Generally, here’s how AI transcription works.
Audio capture
: The system records spoken conversations, whether in calls, meetings, or video, and sends them to a speech to text model. This can happen in real time or as post-recording audio files.
Automatic speech recognition:
The model analyzes the audio and predicts the words the speaker said, accounting for accents and context. Then, the model turns the audio into written text based on context it derives from the conversation stream.
Formatting:
The transcription model applies speaker tags and timestamps and differentiates pauses and background events, such as laughter, to enrich the transcript.
Text delivery:
Lastly, the AI transcription delivers the final transcript to you, which you can save or process further.
The final output of AI transcription is a textual record of narration, conversation, or speeches, structured with supporting data. You’ll know who spoke what, when each word was spoken through word-level timestamps, and punctuation that makes the transcript easier to read.
Features to prioritize in AI transcription software
Organizations have unique transcription workflows that not all AI transcription tools can meet. Some might need a basic AI transcription tool, while others require advanced features that only select providers offer.
When choosing AI transcription software, consider the following:
Accuracy
: The transcription model should demonstrate high accuracy, regardless of use case. To know how accurate an AI transcription tool is, check its word error rate (WER). A
lower rate means the tool is more accurate
. WER also varies with audio quality and language, so test on your own recordings. For reference, ElevenLabs Scribe v3 makes 27% fewer word errors than Scribe v2 across 102 languages. ElevenLabs computes FLEURS WER per clip and then averages per language. Note that this methodology may produce different figures than alternative WER computation methods.
Speaker diarization
: A feature that allows the transcription system to identify different speakers in the audio and tag them accordingly in the transcript.
Speaker diarization
is useful for transcribing conversations involving multiple individuals.
Multi-language support:
The model transcribes audio in
multiple languages
. Some models also detect the spoken language automatically and handle switches between languages mid-conversation.
Keyterm prompting:
A vocabulary dictionary that helps the ASR model transcribe special phrases, such as brand names, correctly.
Export and integrations:
Beyond downloading transcripts directly to your computer, some AI transcription tools can send transcripts directly to CRMs and other enterprise applications.
API support:
Some AI transcription tools expose their transcription capabilities with an API. This allows engineering teams to embed transcription directly into existing products.

What are the benefits of AI transcription?
AI transcription offers multiple benefits to organizations, especially those that previously relied on manual transcription.
Improve focus and productivity:
During meetings or calls, participants can focus on the conversation instead of juggling note-taking.
Save time and cost:
Speech to text models can process large volumes of audio files quickly, letting human transcriptionists focus on high-stakes, complex tasks.
Power data analytics:
AI-generated transcripts, enriched with timestamps, audio tags, and speakers, support advanced data analytics. You can feed the transcript into an LLM-powered tool for summarization, entity extraction, or other analysis.
Support cross-team collaboration:
Team members across departments can review, edit, and annotate the transcript easily using collaborative features.
Enable multilingual support:
Some AI transcription tools support a broad range of languages, which fits enterprises operating across global regions.
Repurpose content:
Marketing teams can repurpose original recordings into blogs, case studies, and other textual formats to support their content marketing strategy.
Meet regulatory compliance:
Some industries require organizations to maintain conversation transcripts for compliance purposes. AI transcription provides a consistent, easier way to meet compliance goals.
AI meeting transcription: turn live calls into actionable notes
Meetings often produce hours-long recordings. Manually transcribing entire recordings is tough because it requires human transcriptionists to maintain unwavering focus. Plus, people may talk over each other, which complicates transcription efforts.
AI meeting transcription overcomes these challenges by capturing the conversation with microphones, sending the audio signal to a speech to text model, and compiling the transcript. There are two ways to transcribe meetings.

Some teams might also send post-meeting recordings to an automatic speech recognition model to generate a more refined and contextually precise transcript.

Are AI transcribers legal? Privacy, consent, and compliance basics
Organizations that use AI transcribers must follow the laws of the hosts' and participants' jurisdictions.
Some regions impose strict regulations requiring the host to inform all attendees before recording and transcribing the conversation. Some countries also require only single-party consent, where any party can record and transcribe the conversation.
Beyond consent, data privacy laws like GDPR and CCPA govern how organizations collect, store, and protect personal data. GDPR, for example, requires organizations to collect only the data they need, keep it no longer than necessary, and protect it with appropriate security measures. CCPA gives consumers rights over how businesses collect, retain, and share their personal information.
For sensitive audio, look for providers with zero retention options, formal compliance programs, and a signed business associate agreement (BAA) for HIPAA workloads. ElevenLabs Scribe, for example, is covered by ElevenLabs’ SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI DSS Level 1, and GDPR compliance programs.

What is AI transcription: FAQ
Can ChatGPT transcribe audio?
ChatGPT can transcribe audio and generate reasonably accurate transcripts for basic usage. ChatGPT may not include all the features available in purpose-built AI transcription tools. For example, file size and duration limits apply, and multilingual transcription consistency may vary. Purpose-built models like ElevenLabs Scribe v3 add speaker diarization, word-level timestamps, keyterm prompting, and audio event tagging, and handle files up to 5 GB and 15 hours long.
Will transcriptionists be replaced by AI?
AI is reshaping transcriptionists' roles. AI handles routine tasks that take up a lot of time, such as producing the first draft. This allows transcriptionists to focus on high-stakes, compliance-heavy, or complex tasks. For example, a human transcriptionist proofreads AI-generated transcripts to ensure drug names are accurate.
What audio formats and languages can AI transcription support?
Most AI transcription software supports common audio file formats, including MP3, WAV, M4A, and AAC. AI transcription tools usually support major global languages, but the range and accuracy vary. For example, ElevenLabs Scribe v3 supports 90+ languages, including English, German, and Spanish. ElevenLabs speech to text also supports FLAC, OGG, OPUS, AIFF, and WebM files, up to 5 GB and 15 hours long.
How much does AI transcription cost?
AI transcription pricing depends on the provider and plan you choose. Some AI transcription providers allow you to get started for free, while paid plans depend on monthly usage, duration, and features like speaker diarization. Is AI transcription legal?
AI transcription is generally legal, but you need to follow the privacy, consent, and data protection laws that apply to your organization and the people you record. Notify participants before recording, check whether your jurisdiction requires one-party or all-party consent, and choose providers with the right data protection controls.
