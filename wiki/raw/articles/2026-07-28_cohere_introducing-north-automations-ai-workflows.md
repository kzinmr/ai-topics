# Orchestrate Agentic Workflows with North Automations

**Source**: [https://cohere.com/blog/introducing-north-automations-ai-workflows](https://cohere.com/blog/introducing-north-automations-ai-workflows)

We're excited to announce the launch of
North Automations
, Cohere's latest step in enabling enterprises to close the AI ROI gap with ease, control, and governance built in from the ground up.
The rapid proliferation of AI agents across the enterprise has created a new set of challenges that are widening the gap between agentic AI potential and its practical implementation. These challenges include:
Narrow workstreams:
One-off agents solve narrow tasks vs end-to-end workflows that emulate how employees actually work across systems and teams.
Lack of governance:
Fragmented implementation of agents across the business prevents the ability to govern usage at scale.
Cost:
Using the same model across all steps in a workflow results in overblown budgets, where routing the right model for each step of the process is becoming a critical necessity.
These market challenges are yielding limited ROI.
"Current AI agent implementations are mostly built with individual, task-specific agents that provide incremental benefits, but create a value gap for enterprise-scale AI adoption,"
notes Gartner research analysts Anushree Verma and Alastair Woolcock in the Agentic AI Hype Cycle 2026 report. This fragmentation limits the transformative potential of AI agents and creates unmanaged security and risk issues.
Agent orchestration represents the next evolutionary stage and critical missing piece for enterprise AI: moving from isolated task automation to coordinated, outcome-driven workflows. This
$550B market opportunity
addresses the core ROI challenges that enterprises face today by providing a centralized layer to coordinate diverse agents across the enterprise, while delivering enterprise-grade governance and security at scale.
Key features of North Automations
Automations are designed to close the AI ROI gap with simplicity, control, and governance built in from the ground up. Powered by Cohere’s secure agentic platform,
North
, Automations enable employees, regardless of their technical abilities, to:
1. Turn complex workflows into simple outputs
Use plain language to describe your goals and connect your tech stack.
Set the frequency with scheduled runs.
Create a clear and auditable execution path with loops and branching.
2. Stay in control at each step
Choose the appropriate model at each step of the process to balance cost with performance.
Use Plan mode to review and edit your approach before building.
Use versioning to keep track of changes as you make updates over time.
Test and iterate before publishing to production.
3. Govern AI safely at scale
Identify the key moments to loop in colleagues for approval.
Analyze automation usage over time and identify the most active users.
Monitor input and output tokens consumed to manage costs.
Automations are embedded within Cohere’s full-stack agentic platform, North, which enables enterprises to:
Deploy securely across any on-prem or cloud environment.
Interoperate within your tech stack using our first-party integrations, your MCP for any custom tool, or our SDK to power bespoke agentic platforms.
Flexibly choose from Cohere’s highly efficient enterprise-specific generative or search models, or any external LLM.
Maintain full visibility and control with guardrails, analytics, and granular roles and permissions.
