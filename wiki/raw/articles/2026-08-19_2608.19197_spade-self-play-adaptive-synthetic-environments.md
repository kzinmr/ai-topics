# SPADE: Self-Play in Adaptive Synthetic Executable Environments

**arXiv:** 2608.19197 (v1 2026-08-19, v2 2026-08-24)
**Code:** https://github.com/spade-rl/spade

## Abstract (verbatim)
Continuous self-improvement requires an ever-expanding pool of self-generated, diverse, adaptive goals. For language agents, existing training environment pools (hand-curated, statically synthesized, or frozen-verifier) keep the goal distribution fixed as the learner scales. We introduce SPADE (Self-Play in Adaptive Synthetic Executable Environments), a self-play RL framework in which a single LLM plays two roles: an **Environment Designer** that writes complete, long-horizon training environments as executable code, and a **Reasoning Agent** that learns to act in them. Each is a stateful, multi-turn environment with an OpenAI Gym-style `reset()`/`step()` interface.

## How it works (from Import AI 470 summary)
- **Environment Designer**: writes complete, long-horizon training environments as *executable code* (OpenAI Gym-style `reset()`/`step()`).
- **Reasoning Agent**: learns to act in those environments.
- **Privileged-hint reward**: the reward for the reasoning agent is estimated using the gap between its reward with and without a "privileged hint" (h) — task-relevant information the Environment Designer attaches to the environment (e.g. a partial solution).
- **Scale**: works at the 30B scale. The authors train three Qwen3 backbones to test SPADE: Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507, Qwen3-8B, and Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507. Qwen3-30B works best. Each model is tuned via **GRPO** for 400 rollouts of 25 environments each, then assessed.

## Why it matters
- Tackles the "goal distribution stays fixed as the learner scales" problem: instead of a frozen environment pool, the environment itself is *generated* by a model and adapts, enabling continuous self-improvement / self-play for language agents.
- Distinct from the 2024 "SPADE" paper (Shreya Shankar's data-quality-assertion synthesis for LLM pipelines) — this SPADE is an **agentic RL / synthetic-executable-environment** method.
