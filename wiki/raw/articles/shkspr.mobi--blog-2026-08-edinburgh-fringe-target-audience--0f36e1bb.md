# Edinburgh Fringe: Target Audience

Source: https://shkspr.mobi/blog/2026/08/edinburgh-fringe-target-audience/

Easily the best show I've seen at the Fringe.
For plot-related reasons, a television company asks a literary genius to create a "pro-weapons sitcom".
It is a fast paced mix of the sitcoms 2012 and Episodes. The jokes come at a steady pace - some attack with the furiousity of bullets, others the the subtlety of a knife in the night.
All four of the cast are spectacular. Turning the sometimes-stock characters into powerful avatars. The amoral Zuckerbergian horror of data driven decision making is the perfect manure in which to grow comedy roses.
It stands well against any classic farce and I hope it tours until the end of the world.
A special shout out to the Pleasance Above for being the most comfortable venue in Edinburgh! Raked auditorium, cushioned seats, and air conditioning all make for a delightful theatrical experience.
