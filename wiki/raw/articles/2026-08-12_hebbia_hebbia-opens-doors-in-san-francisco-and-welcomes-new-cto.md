# Hebbia Opens Doors in San Francisco and Welcomes New CTO

**Source**: [https://www.hebbia.com/blog/hebbia-opens-doors-in-san-francisco-and-welcomes-new-cto](https://www.hebbia.com/blog/hebbia-opens-doors-in-san-francisco-and-welcomes-new-cto)

Hebbia Opens Doors in San Francisco and Welcomes New CTO
Company
By Hebbia
10.21.25
Hebbia opened a new office in San Francisco and plans to expand technical hiring in Silicon Valley.
We’re thrilled to announce the opening of Hebbia’s San Francisco office at 575 Market Street—marking the next chapter in continuing to build the definitive AI platform for finance.
Building at the Epicenter of AI
The Bay Area isn’t just the epicenter of AI innovation—it’s also where Hebbia’s own network of engineers and operators runs deepest. With leading AI companies and research labs right next door, our new hub puts us shoulder-to-shoulder with the people shaping the future of enterprise AI.
Our expansion unlocks three key opportunities:
Innovating at the epicenter:
Staying plugged into the breakthroughs that will shape enterprise AI—and turning them directly into customer impact.
Bringing on top talent:
Recruiting engineers and AI researchers from the deepest technical talent pool in the world.
Strengthening our partnerships:
Deepening ties with investors, data providers, and financial institutions across the Bay Area.
By marrying Wall Street’s market opportunity with Silicon Valley’s technical firepower, we’re accelerating our mission to build the definitive AI platform for finance.
Welcoming Aabhas Sharma as CTO
Alongside this milestone, we’re equally excited to welcome Aabhas Sharma as Hebbia’s Chief Technology Officer.
A world-class engineering leader joining at exactly the right moment, Aabhas has scaled technology at some of the world’s most complex and demanding companies: at Found, he built financial infrastructure serving millions of self-employed professionals across the United States; at Uber, he directed engineering teams that kept one of the largest global marketplaces running at speed and scale.
At Hebbia, Aabhas will spearhead the next generation of product and engineering innovation, pushing the limits of what’s possible as finance’s workflows become fully AI-powered.
The Future of AI for Finance
Hebbia’s mission has always been clear: give finance professionals superpowers by combining human judgment with machine-level scale and speed.
With our new San Francisco HQ and Aabhas at the helm of technology, we’re scaling that mission further and faster than ever.
This isn’t just a new office. It’s another step towards building the future of AI—products that touch the way everyone works.

in San Francisco.
