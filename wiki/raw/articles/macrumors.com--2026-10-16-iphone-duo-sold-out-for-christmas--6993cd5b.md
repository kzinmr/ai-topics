# iPhone Duo is Sold Out for Christmas as Shipping Estimates Slip to 2027

Source: https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/16/iphone-duo-sold-out-for-christmas/

iPhone Duo is Sold Out for Christmas as Shipping Estimates Slip to 2027
Friday October 16, 2026 6:11 am PDT
by
Joe Rossignol
If you were hoping to surprise someone with an
iPhone Duo
for Christmas, you might be out of luck, as Apple's online store in the U.S. is already showing an
estimated shipping date of January 25
for all configurations following the
start of pre-orders
today.
It is possible that some Apple Stores will have the iPhone Duo available for walk-in customers on Friday, October 23 launch day, but it seems likely that inventory will be very tight. For customers interested in reserving an Apple Store pickup slot, Apple's online store now says to check back on Saturday, October 24 for this.
