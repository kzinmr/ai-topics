Announcing Hebbia's Partnership with Octus
Partnerships
By Paul Boland
10.13.26
Hebbia now integrates with Octus, giving credit teams direct access to credit research, deal terms, and private company data.
Hebbia has partnered with Octus, a leading provider of credit intelligence and data for leveraged finance and private credit markets. The partnership adds coverage that public filings do not reach to the filings, market data, and firm documents Hebbia customers already work from.
How Firms Use Hebbia and Octus
Public filings only tell part of the story when you’re assessing a company’s credit quality. Octus fills that gap with expert reporting and legal analysis, private company financials and capital structures, covenant and restructuring data, deal documents, and court filings.
In Hebbia, credit teams can combine Octus data with their own deal history and other market feeds. Customers are already using Octus data to compare a deal's covenant package with similar deals, catch new restructuring filings, build underwriting and investment materials, and more.
The integration runs on the Octus MCP Connector™, which links every Octus product a firm subscribes to into Hebbia at once. Instead of searching each product separately, users ask one question and get an answer drawn from all of them, live and cited to the source. That makes research faster and more complete.
“AI is only as powerful as the intelligence feeding it, particularly across the sub-investment grade credit universe. Integrating Octus into Hebbia grounds AI agents directly in proprietary credit intelligence and data produced by our nearly 300 financial analysts, legal analysts and expert journalists, as well as deal room documents and private company financials. Credit professionals get the deep, verified context they need directly inside their primary workflow,” said Ben Kovacka, Head of Direct Data Services at Octus.
