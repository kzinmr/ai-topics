The Brief: October 2026
This month's releases let you find matter content in the systems your team already uses, ground motions and research in more local authority, and check the figures behind complex documents.
by
Harvey Team
•
Oct 9, 2026
Find and Query Work Regardless of Where it Lives
Find the documents, emails, dockets, and contracts a matter depends on without hunting for them.
Harvey Connector Library
Bring the tools your team already relies on into Harvey through a curated library of pre-verified connectors, with admin controls over who can use each one and which actions run automatically.
Learn more.
Agentic Search for SharePoint and Google Drive
Find the right precedent or prior work product in SharePoint, OneDrive, or Google Drive by describing what you need, and Harvey plans the search and answers with citations to the source documents.
Learn more.
Gmail Search
Pull client decisions and strategy discussions into Harvey by searching Gmail messages, threads, and labels from within a Harvey thread.
Learn more.
PacerPro Vault Sync
Keep a litigation Vault current by syncing it to a federal court docket in PacerPro, with every existing filing loaded at creation and new filings added through a daily sync.
Learn more.
Import Ironclad Files Into Harvey
Analyze your executed agreements by importing contracts from Ironclad into Vault or Assistant, then asking questions, extracting key terms, and building review tables across them.
Learn more.
Ground Motions and Research in Trusted Authority
Litigators can turn a complaint into a fully cited motion draft, and research teams gain more jurisdiction-specific sources, credit data, and saved filters that keep each query focused.
Motion to Dismiss Workflow With LexisNexis®
Draft a complete, fully cited motion to dismiss from a single complaint, researched against live LexisNexis case law, and approve the grounds and research plan before drafting begins.
Learn more.
130+ New Legal Research Sources
We've increased the number of data sources available to provide the latest relevant jurisdictional information.
Learn more.
Octus Knowledge Source
Support leveraged finance and restructuring work with Octus content in Harvey, including expert credit analysis, private company financials, covenant and restructuring data, and US court filings.
Learn more.
Custom Knowledge Source Filters
Skip rebuilding the same research setup for every query by saving your preferred Knowledge Source filters, including for web search, and setting one as the default for that source.
Learn more.
Produce Sharper Analysis From Spreadsheets, Tables, and New Models
Check the numbers behind a deal model, work through reviews with less clutter, and choose from two new models built for document-heavy legal analysis.
Improved Excel Understanding
Harvey now reads each Excel cell's underlying formula and saved value directly. Answers cite the exact cells Harvey relied on.
Learn more.
Review Table Refresh
Move through diligence reviews with less clutter, using a fullscreen document previewer, a condensed cell popover with key actions up front, and per-column controls for filtering and sorting.
Learn more.
Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra
Choose the right model for complex matters with Anthropic's Fable 5.1, built for document-heavy litigation and dispute work, or OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra, which surfaces unsupported assumptions and turns gaps into drafting positions. Learn more about
Fable 5.1
and
GPT-6 Astra
.
Advance Matters From Anywhere, in Your Preferred Language
Run agents, query connected sources, and dictate long prompts from your phone, in multiple languages.
Agents on Mobile
Run, schedule, and star your custom agents from a new Agents tab in the Harvey mobile app, or add one to a thread when you need to give it more context first.
Learn more.
Connectors and Live Dictation on Mobile
Query your connected sources from your phone and get answers cited to the underlying material with
Connectors on Mobile
, and dictate prompts of any length with real-time transcription using
Live Prompt Dictation
.
English, German, Italian, and Portuguese Localization
Receive responses in English (UK), German, Italian, or Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal) by setting your preferred language.
Learn more.
That's all for October. Try one of these features today or reach out to your Harvey team to learn more. If you're not a Harvey customer,
get in touch for a demo
.
