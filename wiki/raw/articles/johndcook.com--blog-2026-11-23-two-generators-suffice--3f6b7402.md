# Two generators suffice

Source: https://www.johndcook.com/blog/2026/11/23/two-generators-suffice/

Every finite simple group is generated by at most two elements. This is a consequence of the classification of finite simple groups (CFSG).
Not only that, with high probability any two elements chosen at random will work. For non-Abelian finite simple groups
G
, the probability that two randomly chosen elements will generate
G
approaches 1 as the size of the group |
G
| → ∞ [1].
Note that this result was published before the CFSG was complete. The
list
of finite simple groups was complete, and widely believed to be complete, but the
proof
that it was complete was not finished.
[1] W. M. Kantor and A. Lubotzky, “The probability of generating a finite classical group,”
Geometriae Dedicata
36 (1990), 67–87.
