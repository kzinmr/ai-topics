Autotorino
is an Italian automotive dealership group founded in the 1960s. It has a longstanding history of innovation: it was the first Italian company to enter the ICDP’s top 50 European dealer groups, it was the first in Italy to import Japanese SUVs and to show competing brands in a single showroom.
To serve their customers from across Italy, today Autotorino receives about 100,000 calls a month across its dealer network, and its team cannot answer all of them. To close that gap, Autotorino built a voice agent on
ElevenAgents
. The agent answers calls, qualifies requests, and creates leads whereas Autotorino's earlier workflow recorded a static message that could handle just the bare minimum of inbound calls.
Low latency, natural conversation, and analysis in one platform
The company's earlier workflow combined Zapier with separate transcription and language tools to turn a recorded message into a Salesforce lead. Autotorino wanted callers to talk with an agent instead, while keeping the same goal of qualifying each request. Autotorino compared several providers before choosing ElevenLabs. It judged each option on three criteria: latency, human-like interaction, and how well the system understands what a caller says without moving data between separate tools. They selected ElevenAgents as their platform of choice.
Round-the-clock coverage that shows what callers actually ask
Now, the customer service agent answers calls at any hour. Before, calls outside staffed hours often went unanswered, which left customers without service.
With ElevenLabs, we are able to answer 24 hours a day, and this gives customers a continuous service."
Michele Spalla
Head of Project and Innovation Management, Autotorino
With calls now captured and analyzed, Autotorino can see what customers ask. About 70% of requests are post-sales, mainly booking workshop appointments for standard maintenance and arranging vehicle drop-off. Callers also ask about the status of their cars in the workshop, which prompted Autotorino to start work on a way to share that information with customers.
Eleven v4 delivers more natural conversation with the same voice and prompts
Autotorino evaluated
Eleven v4
and reported new gains. With the same voice, settings, and system prompt, the agent sounded more natural and less like an automated system. Autotorino highlighted three changes:
A more natural, less artificial-sounding voice
A more consistent and engaging tone that adapts to the stage of the conversation
Greater fluency across the interaction
Autotorino keeps the agent direct and deterministic so callers get clear answers, and its goal is to make each conversation feel as natural as possible.
Lead volume, hang-up rate, and sentiment analysis are all showing improvement
To track the success of the implementation, Autotorino observes three indicators: the number of leads created, the share of callers who hang up after hearing an agent and before a lead is created, and sentiment analysis from ElevenAgents. After implementation, not only did those qualities show improvement, but the hang-up rate stayed below the limit Autotorino set, because the first goal is to keep lead volume level with call volume so that no lead is lost. Here’s what they’ve seen so far:
More than 2x automated call volume:
the new agents can handle much more inbound capacity than the previous system.
70% of requests are post-sales:
mainly workshop appointment bookings and vehicle drop-off, now visible because every call is captured and analyzed
Building a company-wide AI practice behind the rollout
To build AI skills across the company, Autotorino trains ambassadors from each department and runs workshops on new technology adoption. They do this by hosting Innovation Fridays twice a month, with sessions on topics from 3D printing to the European Union AI Act, including a role-playing simulation with a legal consultant built around an autonomous car. ElevenLabs and voice AI were featured in one of those sessions.

Replacing the IVR system starting in November
In November, Autotorino plans to replace the interactive voice response (IVR) system at its first branch with ElevenLabs voice agents. ElevenAgents gives businesses the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability they need to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
