Apply Institutional Knowledge to Legal AI Workflows with Harvey and DeepJudge
The DeepJudge integration brings a firm’s prior work product into Harvey, enabling lawyers to draw on it as they develop new analyses and drafts.
by
Harvey Team
•
Nov 19, 2026
Watch Video
Every firm builds a body of knowledge over years of practice: positions taken, precedent established, language refined for thousands of matters. That knowledge lives in documents scattered across document libraries, network drives, and email archives. It represents the firm’s most valuable work, and making it possible to access and apply at the right moment — when a lawyer is drafting, researching, or advising a client — is a problem worth solving.
That’s what the Harvey and DeepJudge integration is designed to do. After a private preview with select customers, DeepJudge is now available in open early access to joint customers. The integration enables lawyers to draw on their institutional intelligence from DeepJudge as they use Harvey to develop new analyses and drafts, informed by their organization's judgment, and experience.
From Prior Work to the Current Matter
Consider an M&A associate drafting a purchase agreement for a going-private deal. The counterparty has proposed a termination fee, and the associate needs to know how the firm has historically positioned that structure in comparable transactions. Here’s how the integration changes that workflow:
Draw on prior work.
When drafting, research, and analysis, lawyers working in Harvey can draw on DeepJudge’s understanding of their organization’s work, including relevant precedents, matter experience, and negotiated positions.
Keep knowledge current.
DeepJudge Collections sync with Harvey Vault, so the firm’s curated work product is always up to date and readily available in Harvey where lawyers need it.
Go deeper when needed.
Lawyers can move into DeepJudge to analyze comparable deals, and bring additional relevant context to Harvey, informing the draft without breaking the workflow.
Together, these capabilities mean the firm’s accumulated experience becomes part of how lawyers work in Harvey.
Join the Early Access Program
The DeepJudge integration is now in open early access. Firms using both Harvey and DeepJudge can opt in today by reaching out to their Harvey representative.
