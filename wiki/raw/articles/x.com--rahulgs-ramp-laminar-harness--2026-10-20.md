## Context

Rahul describes Ramp consolidating its agentic systems onto a single harness
("rails"). The named products built on it: Stack (accounting harness), Glass
(general-purpose AI coworker/workspace), Inspect (the existing background coding
agent, already documented in the wiki), Ask Ramp (support chat bot), headless OCR
agents, and the ramp text (HIRAMP/447-267) SMS agent. The new harness is
**Laminar**, sitting on top of **ramp router**, model-agnostic, and supporting a
full modern harness feature set (background tasks, sandboxes, tool hooks, tool
search, durability, forking, compaction, queueing, streaming, observability).
