SDK Updates: Including Touch and Heart Rate
If you don't write apps, this is the section that tells you what your watch is about to be able to do, because everything here shows up in apps!
Apps can use the touchscreen more easily now
. Menus and lists respond to taps and swipes after opting in, so a lot of apps get touch-friendly by barely lifting a finger. Beyond that, developers can now read real gestures: taps, swipes and drags, including direction and speed. They no longer have to detect those themselves!
Apps can read heart rate variability
. HRV is the timing variation between heartbeats, and it's the number most sleep, stress and recovery apps are built on. Your watch has been able to measure it. Until now apps couldn't get at it. Now they can.
Writing apps in JavaScript got a lot more powerful
. Alloy is our JavaScript SDK, powered by Moddable, for people who'd rather not write C. It can now read your health data properly: steps, sleep, activity, heart rate, history minute by minute, and alerts when a number crosses a line you set. Button handling got easier too, so things like long-press and double-press are a setting instead of something every developer rebuilds by hand.
Testing got less painful.
The emulator can now fake health data, so someone building a step or sleep app doesn't have to go for a walk every time they change a line of code. Sounds small. It isn't.

A backlight that manages itself
Backlight can be one of the biggest power draws on your watch, so we simplified it into four modes under Settings > Display > Backlight. Pick the one that matches how you use your watch and stop thinking about it:
Max Brightness
: For people who just want a really bright backlight (at the expense of power drain)
Standard
: The default, balanced for most people
Battery Saver
: Trims backlight use to stretch your battery
Advanced
: If you'd rather tune the individual settings yourself
We also improved the light sensor algorithm, so the backlight kicks in more accurately in every mode.
Let’s get notified! Language packs, RTL, and new emoji
Big developments for haters of ▯▯▯ characters: Expanded language packs and support for Noto emoji!
Have you ever received a notification that had ▯▯▯ instead of a readable message?
It means your watch is missing the required language pack. Luckily our community has built many language packs which we include in the Pebble app.
Check out our help site to learn how to install a language pack (as well as learn how to do anything else on your watch).
Currently available language packs
: Arabic, Bulgarian, Burmese, Catalan, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), English, French, German, Hebrew, Italian, Japanese (regular and Light), Portuguese, Russian, and Spanish.
With the Hebrew or Arabic language pack installed, modern PebbleOS renders notifications with proper RTL ordering and Arabic shaping. The old "elbbeP" backwards-text days are over.
And if you love expressing yourself through emoji,
