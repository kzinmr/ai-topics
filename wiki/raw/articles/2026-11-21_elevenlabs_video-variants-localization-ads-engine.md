Video ads tend to drive the strongest results, but they’re also the slowest and most expensive to produce. Testing a new hook or entering a new market requires reshoots, so some brands make fewer variants when their video ads fatigue, or run original-language ads globally instead of localizing.
Ads Engine now lets brands make refreshed variants and localized versions of their video ads to make the most of their existing creative across markets.
How it works
Ads Engine connects to your ad accounts on Google, Meta, LinkedIn, and now TikTok. It pulls your existing creative and its performance data, so you start with what’s already working. From there, it makes new variants and localized versions, translating the text and captions, adapting the images, and dubbing the video.
You can also have
human reviewers check
each ad before it goes live. The finished creative comes back to your team for approval, or Ads Engine can push it directly to your ad platforms.
Ads Engine monitors ad performance and flags creative that's starting to fade, so your team can refresh it before results drop.
Refresh videos with new variants
Ads Engine can refresh a winning ad without a reshoot. It changes the hook or the soundtrack and leaves the rest of the cut as it is. Your team can replace the original with the variant it prefers, or publish several and test which performs best.
New hooks
Ads Engine finds the opening hook of an ad and writes new ones based on what’s worked in your past ads.
It
creates a voiceover
for each new hook with a digital
voice replica
of the original speaker, preserving their tone, emotion, delivery, and personality. Ads Engine then lip-syncs the speaker in the original ad to the new voiceover so it looks natural, and updates the captions to match.
New soundtracks
Refresh video ads with new soundtracks. Ads Engine uses the ElevenLabs Music API to generate original music that feels made for your video.
It
composes new soundtracks
by analyzing the tone, pace, and emotion of the visuals in your original ad. Sound effects land on key moments, like a whoosh on the end card. The new soundtrack is cleared for broad commercial use.
Localize videos for new markets
Ads Engine also takes video ads into new markets with dubbing and translated captions.
Dubbing
Ads Engine uses
Dubbing v2
to translate and dub each ad. Dubbing v2 adapts phrasing and meaning for each language, so each line sounds natural to native audiences. It preserves the original performance, keeping the speaker’s tone, emotion, and timing. Ads Engine then lip-syncs the speaker to the new audio, so the ad looks like it was recorded in that language.
A native-speaking localization professional can review the dub for accuracy and natural delivery, from timing to the pronunciation of brand and product names.
Translated captions
Ads Engine translates the captions too, removing the burned-in originals and re-rendering the captions in the new language with the original ad’s timing and brand fonts.
