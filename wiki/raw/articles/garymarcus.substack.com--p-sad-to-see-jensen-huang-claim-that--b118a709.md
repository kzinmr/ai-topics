# Sad to see Jensen Huang claim that AGI has arrived, with no evidence and no definitions

Source: https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/sad-to-see-jensen-huang-claim-that

A couple hours ago Jensen Huang declared that the race to AGI is over.
Unfortunately, Huang gave no evidence and no definitions, which feels to me like an effort at a takeover of a scientific question by corporate fiat.
I would urge him to read
agidefinition.AI
by Hendrycks, Yoshua Bengio & many others (including myself), and to consider how Astra is doing on the kinds of examples I laid out in my 10 -item
bet with Brundage
. Autoformalization may finally be in reach, and maybe (?) reliable coding; I doubt that Astra will have hit any of the other eight.
By conventional definitions, Astra still falls short.
Declaring victory without a definition simply muddies the waters.
§
