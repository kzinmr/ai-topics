# Developers bring incredible new app experiences to iPhone Duo

UPDATE
Developers bring incredible new app experiences to iPhone Duo
From streaming and gaming to creativity and productivity, developers worldwide are taking advantage of the unique capabilities of the first foldable iPhone
Starting this Friday, iPhone Duo users can discover amazing apps and games optimized for the first foldable iPhone. With an inner display that’s 50 percent larger than iPhone 18 Pro Max, and the flexibility to use iPhone Duo in a variety of poses, developers are enhancing their apps to create exciting new experiences for capturing and sharing memories, streaming entertainment, gaming, multitasking, and much more. Because both displays share the same aspect ratio, apps transition smoothly from the outer display to the inner display and scale proportionally, so users can pick up right where they left off. And with the breakthrough performance of A20 Pro and new capabilities for iPhone — including Split View — iPhone Duo helps users enjoy their favorite apps and experiences while staying productive wherever they are.
“The response to iPhone Duo from our developer community has been incredible, and we’re inspired by how quickly they’ve embraced its larger display and new ways to interact,” said Susan Prescott, Apple’s vice president of Worldwide Developer Relations. “From watching popular shows and content on the expansive inner display to multitasking across windows or playing console-quality games, developers around the world are reimagining what their apps can do. We can’t wait for users to discover these experiences beginning this Friday, with many more app experiences to follow.”
New editorial collections for iPhone Duo apps and games will go live on the App Store this Friday, with additional app updates rolling out throughout the year. Apps optimized for iPhone Duo are easy to identify with a Made for iPhone Duo badge on product pages across the storefront.

“People watch Netflix in all kinds of moments, and we built our new experience to move with them,” said Tom Richards, Netflix’s senior director of Engineering. “From browsing Clips on the outer display to settling in with a favorite show on the inner display, the experience adapts as iPhone Duo opens, closes, or turns, so discovering something new and sitting down to watch it feels effortless.”

Building for iPhone Duo
iPhone Duo opens up a new canvas for app experiences, and developers are already jumping in. More than 2,000 developers have attended more than 50 in-person workshops in 19 cities around the world — and additional workshops in London, Mexico City, Montreal, Paris, and Toronto will give even more teams the opportunity to experience iPhone Duo firsthand to update their apps and games. Through a range of online and in-person programming, technical guides, and Apple Developer Forums, developers have access to Apple engineers and designers to ask questions, get answers, and follow the discussion about all things iPhone Duo.
Developers can easily adapt their apps for iPhone Duo using new and updated APIs in the iOS 27.1 SDK. Apps that use standard SwiftUI and UIKit components adapt automatically to each display, moving controls to the side within easy reach, expanding into multiple columns on the inner display, and nudging buttons away from the fold when iPhone Duo is partially folded. New arrangement and reserved region APIs help developers build custom layouts that split content across the fold or extend edge to edge, and the new hinge API lets apps respond to the angle of the display as iPhone Duo opens and closes. With scene accessories, apps can show content on both displays at once, like a teleprompter on the outer display for the person being recorded, and new camera APIs support selfies with the rear cameras and the first under-display camera on iPhone.
Device Hub in Xcode 27.1 lets developers open, close, fold, and rotate a simulated iPhone Duo to test their apps in every pose. Design guidance and a series of Tech Talks for iPhone Duo are available on Apple Developer, and developers can submit their optimized apps to the App Store now.
