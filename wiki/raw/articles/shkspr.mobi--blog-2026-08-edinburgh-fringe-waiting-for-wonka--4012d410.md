# Edinburgh Fringe: Waiting for Wonka

Source: https://shkspr.mobi/blog/2026/08/edinburgh-fringe-waiting-for-wonka/

This play is
fucked up!
Such a brilliant concept - what happened to the kids from Charlie and the Chocolate Factory
after
they were rejected by Wonka? Stuck together in a Big-Brotheresque household, they trauma-dump on each other.
New Zealand's Half Trick have shrunk their full-length play into a Fringe-standard 60 minutes. That does make it a little brusque and, simultaneously, a bit repetitive. The characters don't get quite enough room to breathe. Mike Teavee has, of course, become an incel online troll - perfect! - but the rest haven't changed much.
The horrors of industrial accidents mixed with corporate malfeasance makes for an excellent comedy. The cast inhabit their characters perfectly, each totally believable as a childhood favourite who has become a deeply troubled adult.
I'd be delighted to read a full length novelisation of the script. It is a witty and terrifying update to a book I devoured as a child. Only now do I realise that it was also devouring me. Truly when you gaze into the lake of chocolate…
