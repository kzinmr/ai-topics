None, as of Nov 2026. 117 AI-enabled candidates that had entered human trials by July 2025, from 63 companies. 1 Reached Phase III. Rentosertib for lung fibrosis — still investigational, not a cure. What would actually move this to 1? The bar is not mysterious. It is the same bar every other drug has to clear. AI does not get a discount for being exciting in a keynote. Counts All three have to be true ✓ AI found it. The target and the molecule were both chosen or designed by AI — not just “AI was used somewhere in the building.” ✓ Regulators approved it. It finished Phase III and was approved by the FDA, EMA, or an equivalent regulator for that disease. ✓ It cures. Patients are cured — disease eliminated or durably reversed — not just symptoms managed a little better for a little longer. Doesn’t count Impressive ≠ a cure × Reading a scan. 1,614 AI devices are FDA-authorized, mostly for radiology. Detecting is not curing. × Predicting a protein. AlphaFold is a genuine breakthrough. A predicted shape is not a treated patient. × Still in a trial. Phase I, II, or III is a promise with paperwork. This counter only moves on approval + cure. × Helping in the lab. Faster screening, better papers, cheaper discovery — valuable, and still not a cure. Closest to breaking the zero If the number ever changes, it will almost certainly start here. None of these is there yet — here is exactly how far each one is. 01 Rentosertib — Insilico Medicine · idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis The furthest along by a clear margin, and the first drug where AI picked both the target (TNIK) and the molecule. Phase IIa in Nature Medicine (71 patients, 12 weeks) showed a +98.4 mL lung-function signal at the top dose. Phase III started in 2026 — 320 patients, 52 weeks. IPF has no cure today; even success here would first be a treatment, not yet a cure.
