# A quote from Drew Breunig

Source: https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/23/drew-breunig/

23rd August 2026
Prior to Fable, it felt silly to waste
too
much time improving your coding harness or context strategies. A new model would arrive at the same price (or cheaper!) and paper over most of your problems.
But then Fable landed. It was (and still is!)
incredible
. But the cost was so high and Opus was
good enough
(as was 5.6, K3, and even GLM) for
most
of the code we needed.
So we started to think about what work went where.
—
Drew Breunig
,
Fable & The End of the Free Lunch
