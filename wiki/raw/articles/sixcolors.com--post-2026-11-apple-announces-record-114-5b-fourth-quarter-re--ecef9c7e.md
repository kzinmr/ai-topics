Apple announces record $114.5B fourth-quarter results
On Monday, Apple announced
record fourth-quarter earnings
, with total revenue of $114.5B, up 12 percent from the year-ago quarter. iPhone revenue was up 17.5%, Mac revenue was up 23.7%, and Services revenue was up 9.8%. Wearables revenue was down 7.9% and iPad revenue was down 10.4%.
Apple execs spent an hour on the phone with financial industry analysts.
