What's New: September Disclosure 2026
Product
By Aabhas Sharma
08.31.26
Last month had two major launches, Max and Matrix 2.0, plus many more upgrades to the rest of the platform.
We’re back this month after shipping two major platform launches—Max and Matrix 2.0—alongside smarter ways to get up to speed on Projects, access to more data sources, and easier ways to manage and share Skills.
Max
Max
is the first AI teammate that understands how your institution operates. It thinks, reasons, and communicates like your best colleague would. In addition to producing models and decks, Max can generate audio briefs, interactive dashboards, and several other formats.
Matrix 2.0
Matrix
now creates deliverables, including memos, decks, models, and emails, pulling from any data source in Hebbia, your data warehouses, internal systems, and external market data. Matrix is now available directly in Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or any internal application through the Hebbia MCP.
Project Intelligence
Search across an entire Project and get results that stay up to date as the content changes. You can easily access Projects through the Hebbia MCP.
Platform Updates
Enhanced functionality for Skills
Create and edit Skills directly in Max, share them with teammates or publish org-wide, and control whether recipients can view, download, or duplicate what you share. Skills built on other platforms can be imported into Hebbia, so all of your knowledge lives in one place.
Daloopa Integration
Daloopa's fundamental data, including historical financials, KPIs, and the underlying line-item detail, is now available inside Hebbia for joint customers.
New S&P Data Feeds
Access S&P's transaction history, company intelligence, ownership, key developments, and company events in Hebbia alongside your other deal and market data sources.
Canadian Filings
Access Canadian regulatory filings from TSX and TSX Venture issuers directly in Hebbia, sourced from SEDAR+.
Microsoft Outlook Content
Access your Outlook email and calendar history as a data source in Hebbia.
Latest Frontier Models in Hebbia
Access Claude Opus 5 and GPT-5.6 (Sol, Terra, and Luna) in Hebbia.
