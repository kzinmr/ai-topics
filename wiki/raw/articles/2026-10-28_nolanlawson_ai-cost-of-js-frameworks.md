Title: The AI cost of JavaScript frameworks (Nolan Lawson, 2026-10-28)

Benchmark study: Nolan Lawson had AI coding agents build the same small Pokémon-style web app across JavaScript frameworks (React, Angular, Svelte, Solid, Astro, etc.) and measured token spend and time. Headline community findings (from HN thread): Astro came out on top — best time, #2 lowest spend, zero client-side JS; Angular performed worst ("broken even for agents"). The app is small and content-driven, which commenters flag as a limitation: results may not transfer to highly interactive, realtime/async-heavy apps.
