# AI unlocks Kurt Godel's notebooks, said to contain hundreds of proved theorems

The Guardian, November 13, 2026 (paywalled; article body not fetched). Content reconstructed from the HN discussion.

Story URL: https://www.theguardian.com/science/2026/nov/13/kurt-godel-notebooks-ai-helps-unlock-hidden-discoveries-man-who-transformed-maths

## Key facts from the thread

- Jan von Plato (University of Helsinki), the world's leading Godel expert, leads the team; his group is the only one where multiple people can read Godel's Gabelsberger-based shorthand.
- Von Plato started the Godel project in 2017 after facing mandatory retirement; received a EUR 2.5m European Research Council grant; one of only six people able to read the shorthand.
- The notes are said to contain hundreds of proved but never-published theorems; the article mentions notes a few months of work away from proving the independence of the continuum hypothesis decades before Cohen's 1963 result.
- Research group site: https://www.helsinki.fi/en/researchgroups/godel-enigma ; AITP 2026 slide deck: https://aitp-conference.org/2026/slides/JvP.pdf
