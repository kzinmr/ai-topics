We’re excited to be a launch partner for OpenAI’s B2B marketplace, making it easier for businesses building on OpenAI to deploy Decagon’s customer-facing AI agents.
Eligible OpenAI enterprise customers can apply their existing commitment toward Decagon, simplifying procurement and helping teams move from AI investment to production faster.
Extending our partnership
Decagon has been a preferred partner in OpenAI’s enterprise ecosystem since earlier this year, working together to bring frontier AI to enterprise customer experiences. Our launch on the marketplace builds on that foundation.
Here’s what it means for customers:
Streamlined procurement.
Eligible enterprise customers can apply part of their existing OpenAI commitment toward Decagon, reducing the procurement friction that can slow down AI deployments.
Faster time to value.
Decagon goes live in weeks with
Agent Operating Procedures
(AOPs). Purchasing through the marketplace further simplifies the path from decision to deployment.
A technical partnership beyond the marketplace.
OpenAI models are an important part of Decagon’s constellation of models, alongside the specialized models
Decagon Labs
post-trains for CX.
Enterprise-ready from day one.
SOC 2 Type 2, HIPAA, GDPR, and CCPA compliance, configurable PII redaction, and layered guardrails that run before, during, and after every conversation.
Part of a broader ecosystem
Our work with OpenAI is one example of the ecosystem we're building. More broadly,
Decagon Partnerships
brings together technology, alliance, and sales and services partners as part of an ecosystem that's helping make concierge-level customer experience accessible to every company.
To support this community, we offer a Partner Essentials course through
Decagon University
, a training program to help partners build, deploy, and scale Decagon's AI concierge solutions.
Get started
Our listing goes live on the OpenAI B2B marketplace soon, with deployment options, pricing, and details on applying your OpenAI commitment toward Decagon.
In the meantime, if you're interested in learning more, reach out to our partnerships team at partnerships@decagon.ai.

There are very few places where you can prototype with frontier LLMs, ship to production in days, and watch users engage with the systems you built—all while owning the entire stack, from intent parsing and tool usage to API integration and observability. This role at Decagon is one of those places.

Whether I’m building intelligent workflows for customers or designing infrastructure that supports our agent platform, it’s rare to find an environment where the work transitions from concept to production within days, actively powering user experiences and transforming how businesses operate.
