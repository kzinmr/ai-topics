# Execution Is Free. How Ambitious Are You?

**Speaker:** Jason Liu, OpenAI Developer Experience (@jxnlco)

## Description

When execution is nearly free, ambition is about how you approach the work, not how many tokens you spend. Jason Liu of OpenAI's developer experience team takes on three fears: that AI means more slop, that it erodes critical thinking, and that it will replace us. His answers: own the work you ship, build the language to describe what good looks like, and use AI to pursue the fields you set aside for software. He draws on photography's effect on painting, his time in art school and his own projects in drums, 3D printing and chips.
