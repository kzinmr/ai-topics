# Hetzner is working on LLM Inference

*Article sourced from Sliplane blog, published 2026-07-24*

German cloud provider Hetzner is entering the LLM inference market, offering competitive GPU pricing for serving AI models. This represents a significant development in the European AI infrastructure landscape, potentially disrupting the market dominated by US hyperscalers (AWS, GCP, Azure) and specialized inference providers (Together AI, Fireworks, Groq).

Key details:
- Hetzner is known for affordable cloud infrastructure in Europe
- Entering the managed LLM inference space with GPU-backed endpoints
- Competitive pricing compared to major cloud providers
- Supports multiple models and includes an `enable_thinking` option for reasoning models
- Part of a broader trend of European cloud providers adding AI inference capabilities (alongside Infomaniak, OVHcloud, etc.)

The article discusses the growing demand for sovereign European AI infrastructure and how traditional cloud providers are adapting their offerings to include managed LLM inference.
