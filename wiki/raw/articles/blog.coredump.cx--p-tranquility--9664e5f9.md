# Tranquility - lcamtuf’s thing

Source: https://blog.coredump.cx/p/tranquility

The Oval Office. President JAMES CULLEN sits behind his desk. Secretary of Agriculture SUSAN WALLACE and Deputy Secretary DANIEL GUTIERREZ stand in front of him, their hands folded.
PRESIDENT CULLEN:
(without looking up)
As you know, we’re facing minor cost overruns on the Presidential Spa —
DEPUTY SECRETARY GUTIERREZ: A visionary project, Sir.
PRESIDENT CULLEN: Thank you, Daniel. To deal with this situation, we need a bit of belt-tightening across my departments. Checking for loose change under the cushions, if you will.
SECRETARY WALLACE: Of course, Mr. President.
PRESIDENT CULLEN: Which brings me to Agriculture.
(flips a page)
Susan…  four hundred billion dollars? Annually?
SECRETARY WALLACE: Give or take.
PRESIDENT CULLEN: Daniel, remind me — what am I paying for?
Gutierrez’s jaw tightens.
DEPUTY SECRETARY GUTIERREZ: Um… meatpacking inspections. Price supports for wheat. And Forest Service, obviously. Trail maintenance, signage at trailheads —
PRESIDENT CULLEN: Four hundred billion. For trail signs?
DEPUTY SECRETARY GUTIERREZ: And fee stations at entrances. Or collection boxes, depending on the facility —
PRESIDENT CULLEN:
(closes binder, leans back)
Susan, am I going crazy?
Wallace looks at Gutierrez, who is staring at his shoes, then turns back to Cullen and sighs.
SECRETARY WALLACE: It’s… it’s Project Tranquility, Sir.
PRESIDENT CULLEN: I’m listening.
SECRETARY WALLACE: Back in nineteen eighty-six, a wave of violent crime was sweeping the country. Publicly, President Reagan talked about more badges and tougher sentencing. But behind the scenes, he summoned the nation’s leading scientists to look for… for a better approach.
PRESIDENT CULLEN: So you’re telling me that your department is why violent crime dropped by half?
SECRETARY WALLACE: Yes, Mr. President.
PRESIDENT CULLEN: How?
SECRETARY WALLACE: They — the scientists found a mutation. An errant genetic pattern, present in zero-point-one of the general population, but seventy-eight percent of the samples taken from serial murderers, rapists. The worst of the worst.
Wallace’s face lights up with pride.
SECRETARY WALLACE (CONT’D): Once we knew, the path forward was clear. Tranquility piggybacked on the newborn heel-prick screening program already in place at the nation’s hospitals. No one needed to know. Once we identify an affected individual, we simply take steps to remove their opportunity to offend.
PRESIDENT CULLEN:
(taps the binder, nods slowly)
Ah. I see.
DEPUTY SECRETARY GUTIERREZ: And Sir, that’s where the bulk of the cost lies.
PRESIDENT CULLEN: So...
(beat)
we just whack them or  —
DEPUTY SECRETARY GUTIERREZ: What —
SECRETARY WALLACE: Oh, gosh, no! Heavens!
DEPUTY SECRETARY GUTIERREZ: We’re not monsters, Mr. President. Not everyone with the gene —
PRESIDENT CULLEN: Right. My apologies. Go on.
SECRETARY WALLACE: In its early days, Project Tranquility conducted clinical trials. By far the most effective yet humane intervention was to simply monitor the subject and remove environmental triggers. Make sure they never feel an urge to offend.
DEPUTY SECRETARY GUTIERREZ: We can’t help everyone, of course, but we do our best. Speaking of triggers — rejection is a major one.
SECRETARY WALLACE: So we start small. We surround them with warmth. Offer affirmations. Maybe tip the scales so they win a third-grade science fair.
DEPUTY SECRETARY GUTIERREZ: Naturally, the interventions scale up over time. We pull a few strings to get them into the school of their choice. Put a word in with the bank so that they get a loan for the business of their dreams. Ensure the customers keep coming, too.
SECRETARY WALLACE: A loving spouse, of course. Another big one.
Cullen blinks at them.
PRESIDENT CULLEN: So we just help them… live normal lives?
DEPUTY SECRETARY GUTIERREZ: Better than normal. Satisfying, prosperous lives. We count many executives, celebrities, and politicians among our alumni. We don’t just prevent crime — we build a more prosperous society.
PRESIDENT CULLEN: Am I —
SECRETARY WALLACE:
(warmly)
Of course not. You predate the program, Mr. President.
A long pause.
PRESIDENT CULLEN: The public must never find out.
SECRETARY WALLACE: Agreed.
