Andrés Chama runs his own business, Comuni, a digital platform that connects neighbors and makes civic participation easier, so his free time rarely lines up with office hours. When he applied to POSiBLE, Mexico’s
largest entrepreneurship accelerator
run by
Fundación Televisa
, he didn’t have to wait for an opening on a staff member’s calendar. He talked through his business with an AI interviewer when it suited him and got feedback as soon as the call ended.
This summer, Fundación Televisa used
ElevenAgents
to interview more than 1,000 entrepreneurs applying to POSiBLE’s 2026 cohort. The interviews ran from June 29 to July 24, and the POSiBLE team then drew on those conversations to choose 70 ventures for its National Camp, the accelerator’s final stage.
A nonprofit built for scale
Fundación Televisa is a Mexican nonprofit with more than 20 years of experience running large programs in education, entrepreneurship, and digital inclusion. Its work reaches millions of people across Mexico each year. POSiBLE supports businesses at the early, operating, and growth stages with training and mentoring, plus a place in a national community. Another one of its programs, Bécalos, is one of the country’s largest scholarship initiatives.
Programs of this size depend on one-on-one conversations, and those take time. Every POSiBLE applicant needs an interview about growth, customers, and sales before the team can make a fair selection. With more than a thousand applicants, scheduling those calls alone is a major undertaking.
From first demo to full rollout
Fundación Televisa’s technology team began testing ElevenLabs in March, after a demo at the foundation’s Mexico City offices. Led by Julio Alberto Ramos, the foundation’s CTO, the team connected ElevenLabs to its existing chat systems through the API.
In June, the foundation joined the
ElevenLabs Impact Program
, which supports nonprofits with our voice AI tools. ElevenLabs’ Mexico team worked with Ramos and his colleagues on agent setup, call capacity, and integration with POSiBLE’s web platform.
1,000 interviews in four weeks
Interviews averaged five minutes. Applicants opened a voice agent embedded in POSiBLE’s web platform and talked through their business, from revenue to customer base. Because the agent was available any time of day, founders could interview whenever their schedules allowed.
By the time the window closed, the agent had conducted 1,036 interviews. Lizbeth Salazar, Data and AI Strategy Lead at POSiBLE, said the voice agent was the main engine behind the entire interview stage.
“At POSiBLE, we believe that behind every business there is a person, a story, and a dream,"
Salazar said.
"Artificial intelligence allows us to reach more entrepreneurs, but more importantly, it gives us the opportunity to truly listen to them, understand their challenges, and recognize what they really need.”
What entrepreneurs said
Applicants noticed how the conversations felt.
“It had very natural language compared to other voice models,”
said Karina Michel, an entrepreneur chosen to join the POSiBLE cohort.
For Andrés Chama, flexibility mattered most.
“I felt very comfortable, and in the end, the insights were quite accurate,”
he said.
“The feedback after the interview helped me a lot.”
He added that the agent never replaced POSiBLE’s staff, but it let him move at his own pace.
“Even though POSiBLE has an amazing team, having an AI available every day for every part of this process meant I could do it whenever I, as an entrepreneur, had the time,”
he said.
“That made it feel much more approachable.”
Quotes translated from Spanish.
From interviews to the National Camp
The selected founders met in person at POSiBLE’s National Camp in Santa Fe, Mexico City, from September 30 to October 4. The camp focused on innovation, AI, and business strategy.
During the event, Raúl Rodríguez, an account manager at ElevenLabs, and Pablo Rivas, a sales development representative, led a 90-minute workshop on using voice AI to automate and scale business processes. Many of the founders in the room had already used the technology once, as applicants. Attendees also learned about
ElevenLabs Startup Grants
, which help early-stage companies build with ElevenLabs.
Bécalos reached 394 young people in two days
POSiBLE wasn’t the only Fundación Televisa program to use voice agents. Bécalos, the foundation’s scholarship program, used ElevenAgents to conduct interviews for Bécalos English Challenge Movilidad. With the agent available 24 hours a day on July 7 and 8, it interviewed 394 young people in two days.
What’s next
Fundación Televisa is gearing up for its next POSiBLE cohort. Nonprofits working to expand access to education, opportunity, and information can
apply to the Impact Program
.
