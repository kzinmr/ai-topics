# BREAKING: Secret US AI evaluation framework has been partly revealed

Source: https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/breaking-secret-us-ai-evaluation

Remember, even as Trump shouts from the rooftops of Truth Social that he is violently opposed to regulating AI …
… he is in fact regulating AI, with
a secret AI security framework
for evaluating which “frontier” models can be released. Almost nobody knows what’s actually in it.
§
A lot of people are rightly upset about it.
In that spirit, I told you last week about
Protect Democracy’s FOIA request
and their lawsuit to find out what that policy is.
To my astonishment, the government responded promptly! With
132 pages of records
! What’s more
Protect Democracy
kindly shared them with me.
For your convenience and edification, here is the first page, which very much sets the tone for the rest:
Aside from revealing, unsurprisingly, that Michael Kratsios (Director of OSTP) and Ethan Klein (U.S. Chief Technology Officer) are in on the conversation, almost nothing is revealed.

are

Either way, U.S. policy on screening AI remains opaque for another day.
Conspiracy theorists might start to wonder what the government is hiding.
P.S. The good news is that
Protect Democracy
plans to keep fighting for transparency. In an email I just received from the lead counsel on the case, Deana El-Mallawany told me “
Across the spectrum of views on AI risk and how to mitigate it, there's one clearly wrong way: in secrecy, with the terms, criteria, and justifications for regulatory action known only to a few of the most powerful political actors and economic actors in the world. That scenario is fertile ground for corruption and autocratic abuse -- as well as mistakes that could be avoided with greater transparency. That's why we will continue to push the administration to produce its secret review framework via this litigation and expect to reach agreement on a production schedule very soon.”
