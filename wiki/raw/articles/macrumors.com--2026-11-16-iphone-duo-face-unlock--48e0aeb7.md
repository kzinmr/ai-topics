# Apple Adds Facial Unlock Option on iPhone Duo in iOS 27.3

Source: https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/16/iphone-duo-face-unlock/

Apple Adds Facial Unlock Option on iPhone Duo in iOS 27.3
Apple today added a new option for unlocking the
iPhone Duo
using the front-facing camera, giving device users multiple ways to access their content. The feature is available in the second iOS 27.3 beta.
The option to unlock with facial recognition is only available on the Lock Screen, and authentication via
Touch ID
is still needed for
Apple Pay
purchases, adding passwords and logins, and more.
Unlocking with the camera requires a recent ‌Touch ID‌ or passcode authentication, and ‌Touch ID‌ is required again after about 45 minutes of inactivity. Unlocking with the front camera can be enabled in the settings app under ‌Touch ID‌ and Passcode.
Apple is also adding a new option to lock the ‌iPhone Duo‌ when it's closed rather than transferring content to the front display. The is available under Display and Brightness in the Settings app.
