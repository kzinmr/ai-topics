# Kernel prepatch 7.3-rc7

Source: https://lwn.net/Articles/1099798/

Kernel prepatch 7.3-rc7
[Posted October 12, 2026 by corbet]
The
7.3-rc7
kernel prepatch is out for
testing.  According to Linus: "
As always, I would have preferred to see a smaller
rc, but it's not like anything in here looks like a reason to delay
the final release. So that's still set for next weekend unless
something new comes up.
"
One notable change that slipped into the mainline this week is the
deprecation
of the ext4
data=journal
mode; it will be removed entirely at the
beginning of 2028.
