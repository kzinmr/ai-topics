# Jensen Huang agrees to testify before Congress

Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/jensen-huang-agrees-to-testify-before-congress/

Ex-Nvidia CEO
Jensen Huang
has agreed to testify before Congress, House Foreign Affairs Committee Chairman Brian Mast said on Thursday, according to reports from
Bloomberg
and
NBC News
.
Huang, who stepped down as CEO following reports of an alleged affair with a woman suspected of spying for China, agreed to testify Wednesday after several senior lawmakers threatened to subpoena him.
Huang has agreed to testify before Congress by Thanksgiving, which falls later this month.
The development comes after House Republicans Mast, John Moolenaar, and Rick Crawford sent Huang a letter on November 4 requesting that
he appear before their committees
. Moolenaar chairs the House Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party, Mast chairs the House Committee on Foreign Affairs, and Crawford chairs the House Intelligence Committee.
“Jensen and I have been very at odds as some of you already know on export controls, but Nvidia is a very important company to the United States of America,” Mast said to reporters. “I don’t want to see anything hurt in Nvidia, but he has some very serious questions to answer.”
The testimony will take place behind closed doors, though Mast said he expects the briefing to be recorded on video and transcribed.
