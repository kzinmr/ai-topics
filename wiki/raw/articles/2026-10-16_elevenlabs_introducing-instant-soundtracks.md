Add music and sound effects to any video in ElevenCreative
Elevate any video you upload to ElevenCreative by generating sound effects alongside a soundtrack with Instant Soundtracks. The new update to ElevenCreative, launched today, allows you to upload a video, and the model scores it with original music, then analyzes the footage for sound effect cues, and generates sound effects  aligned to those timestamps.
These generated effects are automatically placed on the timeline to match the video. The model watches the video for moments that call for an effect and places each one at the right timestamp, for example, a whoosh timed to the logo at the end of an ad.
Today’s update also brings better timing for short-form content, as well as more accurate mood switching across scenes. You can now also use your video's existing audio as a reference for the new score. The model can analyze the audio — its genre, crescendos, beats per minute, or instrumentation, for example — and use it to shape the new score. The result is music that matches the character of the original, rather than starting entirely from scratch.
Keep refining in the editor
In the ElevenCreative Music Video Editor, sound effects appear as separate layers, so you can review and adjust them independently of the soundtrack. You can also add in more effects, adjust timing, swap out the music, or refine individual moments without having to start over. When you’re happy with your creation, you can export directly from ElevenCreative.
Better short-form timing, more control
Instant Soundtracks is designed for the pace of short-form video, with tighter timing around cuts, transitions, and on-screen actions. It can also shift the music’s mood across scenes. For example, if the vibe shifts from a fast-paced scene, the backing track might be EDM or R&B, but then moves to acoustic guitar for a slower, more intimate scene.
