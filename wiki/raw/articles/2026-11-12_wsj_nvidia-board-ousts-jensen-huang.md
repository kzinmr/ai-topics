## Reported facts (as discussed)

- The Nvidia board is preparing to remove CEO Jensen Huang; an outside firm is working on CEO candidate identification. Blog triage notes circulating names: Jen-Hsun Huang successor candidates reportedly include CFO Colette Kress as interim and Apple hardware chief Johny Srouji as a candidate, with a board meeting reported for Wednesday.
- Trigger context: an NYT article on a second affair — a relationship with an executive who reports to him — on top of an earlier affair plus a "spy relationship"/export-control scandal thread.
