Connor Molloy
Legal Engineer at Harvey
Connor is a Legal Engineer in Harvey's Sydney team, which partners with law firms and in-house teams in APAC to help them understand how to get the most out of Harvey. Previously, he worked in disputes at Wotton Kearney, where he also helped drive the firm's AI strategy. He holds an LLB and B.Commerce from the University of Notre Dame Australia and is admitted to practice in New South Wales and the High Court of Australia.
