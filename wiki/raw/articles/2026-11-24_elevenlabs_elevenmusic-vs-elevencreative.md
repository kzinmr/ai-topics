ElevenLabs offers music generation through ElevenMusic and ElevenCreative.
ElevenMusic
is our consumer app for creating, discovering, remixing, and sharing music. Music on
ElevenCreative
is designed for creators and businesses who need to create, manage, publish, license, and download AI-generated music.
While both use the same underlying technology, they’re designed for different audiences and use cases. This article explains what each product does and when to use each one.
ElevenMusic
is a standalone app for creating studio-quality songs. ElevenMusic is an AI music generator built for creating songs, with tools that give you control over every part of the music you make. It's free to start, then $9.99 per month for Pro, and payment is separate from your ElevenLabs plan credits.
If your goal is to create a full song, ElevenMusic is the place to start. Describe the sound you want in a prompt or upload a track you’ve already produced, then edit and remix it section by section until it's exactly what you want. ElevenMusic is built with licensed music from artists, labels, and publishers.
ElevenCreative
is the ElevenLabs creative platform for voice, sound effects, video, and music, used by content creators, studios, and developers. Music generation is one tool among many, built for adding music to videos, ads, and other projects. It's free to start, and the paid tiers use the credits included in your ElevenLabs plan.
If you need music to go with a video, ad, jingle, or game, use ElevenCreative. Its music tool generates songs in any style and at the length you choose, with editing tools to refine them. Music in ElevenCreative is licensed for nearly all commercial use cases.
What ElevenMusic is for
ElevenMusic is built around the song. You open the app, type a few words about the mood, genre, or story you have in mind, and get a finished track with vocals, lyrics, and production.
From there, you can:
Write or paste your own lyrics, or let the app generate them
Apply a style to shape the sound, or create and save your own
Remix a track into a different genre
Publish your tracks, share them on the platform, or download them and take them elsewhere
The experience is designed for musicians, hobbyists, and anyone with a song idea they want to hear.
You can find ElevenMusic pricing
here
. ElevenMusic is a free app, with a Pro tier that’s $9.99 per month. Pricing is separate from ElevenCreative.
What music in ElevenCreative is for
ElevenCreative is a production environment. Alongside text-to-speech, voice cloning, dubbing, sound effects, and video tools, it includes a music generator that produces background tracks and scores from a prompt. It's the right choice when music is a layer in something else:
Soundtracks and background music for videos, with automatic video-to-music scoring
Ads, trailers, and social content that need licensed, on-brand audio
Podcast intros and outros.
Games and apps that generate music programmatically through the ElevenLabs API
Studio workflows where music sits on a timeline next to voiceover, captions, and sound effects
Pricing follows the standard ElevenLabs plans (Free, Starter, Creator, Pro, Scale, and Enterprise), and the commercial license that comes with paid plans covers the music you generate.
The two subscriptions are separate. An ElevenMusic Pro plan does not add credits to an ElevenLabs account, and ElevenLabs credits cannot be spent inside the ElevenMusic app.

Why use ElevenMusic if ElevenCreative can make songs too?
Both can generate a song with vocals. The difference is what happens next.
ElevenMusic is built around you as the songwriter. Bring your own lyrics or a track you’ve already made, then shape it section by section. Save your own styles, remix it into a new genre, and keep going until it sounds like yours.
ElevenCreative is built around the project. Music is one layer next to voiceover, sound effects, and video, sized and timed to fit what you’re making.
Only available on ElevenMusic:
Remixing:
remix tracks from a community of independent artists.
Muse:
a conversational co-writer that takes you from an idea to a finished track.
Composer:
regenerate a single section, compare takes side by side, and swap, double, or extend sections.
Save to SoundCloud:
take your finished track and save it straight to SoundCloud.
Plus other tools that support the full music production creative workflow.
Frequently asked questions
How much does ElevenMusic cost?
ElevenMusic offers a free tier and a Pro subscription for $9.99 per month. ElevenMusic is not part of the ElevenLabs credit plan, and you do not need an ElevenLabs account to use ElevenMusic.
Can I generate full songs with vocals in ElevenCreative?
Yes. ElevenCreative's music generator can produce vocal tracks as well as instrumentals. The difference is the workflow: ElevenCreative is built for placing music into larger projects, while ElevenMusic is built for writing, remixing, and sharing songs.
Is ElevenMusic the same as music in ElevenCreative?
No. ElevenMusic is a standalone app with its own pricing, separate from music in ElevenCreative.
