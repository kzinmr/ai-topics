Information Governance in Harvey: Retention Policies, Legal Holds, and Audit Logs
Harvey expands information governance with updates to retention policies, audit logs, and legal holds.
by
Harvey Team
•
Nov 20, 2026
Legal AI creates new forms of data and work product: prompts, outputs, uploaded documents, and agent runs. Most information governance policies were written before it existed.
Today, Harvey workspace admins get direct control over that data. Set
retention
at the workspace, vault, or client matter level yourself, and view, filter, search, and export
audit logs
in
Command Center
. Legal holds enter Early Access in early December.
So when someone asks how long you keep this data, whether it’s been preserved, or who did what and when, you have evidence of that activity. Governance is what lets legal teams keep expanding their use of AI with confidence.
Set How Long Harvey Keeps Your Data
Keeping data forever adds cost and risk, and deleting it too soon can put you out of compliance with legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations. Retention policies let workspace admins manage how long Harvey keeps their organization's data, so the schedule reflects your organization's own retention program. Until now, adjusting retention in Harvey meant making a request. Now workspace admins can review and manage it themselves in Command Center.
Workspace admins can set retention at three levels: workspace,
vault
, and matter, where each matter can carry its own custom period.
Retention in Practice:
Sarah is the Director of eDiscovery and Information Governance at a large public company. Her retention program says threads should age out after 12 months, so she sets workspace retention to 12 months. One client matter covers a long-term supply agreement, and its records clause requires the company to keep contract-related records for six years after the agreement ends. Sarah gives that matter a custom policy that keeps its threads and vaults for the full contractual period. Her program runs on her schedule, with no request to wait on, and every retention change lands in the audit log.
Preserve What Matters, When It Matters
Legal teams need a way to preserve potentially relevant information that would otherwise be deleted. Starting in early December, legal holds in Harvey will be available in Early Access.
Early Access focuses on data preservation in Harvey. When you place a hold on a custodian, Harvey suspends routine deletion of their existing Harvey data and preserves the data they create or edit while the hold is active. You can continue to manage hold notices and acknowledgments in the tools you use today.
Legal Holds in Practice:
A former distributor threatens to sue Sarah's company. Without legal holds, her options in Harvey are workarounds. She could extend retention to unlimited for everyone or export everything through the History API. Both are blunt instruments for a targeted obligation. With legal holds, she places holds on the custodians involved in the distributor relationship, and their Harvey data is preserved until she releases the hold. The obligation is met for the people involved, without freezing everyone else's data.
Answer Who Did What, With Evidence
When a regulator asks who did what in Harvey and when, your organization needs evidence. Audit logs give workspace admins that record. Audit logs have been available through the API, which lets you pipe the data into your SIEM solution, for example Splunk. Now you can also view, filter, search, and export them directly in Command Center.
Audit Logs in Practice:
A year into the case, someone asks Sarah about a specific matter. Access to it changed, and certain settings changed along the way. Who made those changes, and when? Sarah filters the audit log by person, action, or date, exports the result, and answers with a documented record.
Retention sets the schedule, legal holds preserve what matters, and audit logs show what happened. Together, they give Sarah, and the workspace admins like her, answers they can stand behind. Start in Command Center with retention and audit logs, and contact your account team to join the legal holds Early Access.
