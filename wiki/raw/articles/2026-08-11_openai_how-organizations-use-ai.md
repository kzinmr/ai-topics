# How Organizations Use AI: Evidence from ChatGPT

## Abstract

The paper studies how organizations use frontier generative AI by linking **ChatGPT Enterprise account records** to usage, worker roles, task classifications, and public-company financial data through March 2026. The worker-level sample at the six-month adoption horizon includes **over 1,500 organizations and over 17 million messages**.

Four facts about enterprise AI adoption:

1. **Rapid growth** — ChatGPT Enterprise usage grew from a combination of new firm adoption and growing intensity among existing adopters.
2. **Concentrated adoption** — U.S.-based public company adoption is concentrated among larger, more valuable, and more R&D- and SG&A-intensive firms.
3. **Broad but uneven use** — active use spans job functions and seniority levels, with especially high usage intensity among early-career workers.
4. **Broad task coverage** — usage encompasses a broad range of knowledge work tasks: writing, technical work, communication, and information synthesis.

In aggregate, firms differ widely in the speed, breadth, and purpose of enterprise AI adoption, and are still actively learning how to integrate AI into organizational workflows.

## Key Quantitative Findings

- Aggregate output tokens from ChatGPT Enterprise customers grew roughly **sevenfold** between June 2025 and March 2026.
- Within a consistent cohort of firms (adopted Jan 2024–Jun 2025), token growth was nearly **fourfold** — about **half** of total growth came from already-adopting firms.
- Adopters are larger, more valuable, and more R&D/SG&A-intensive than non-adopters (suggests early adoption tied to prior intangible/organizational investment).
- Marketing and communications workers send more messages than executives; early-career workers send **~8–9 more weekly messages** than senior employees.
- Most common use cases: writing, communication, information synthesis; also research, planning, data analysis, legal/regulatory, finance. Breadth is consistent with generative AI as a **general purpose technology (GPT)** for knowledge work.

## Context

Most prior evidence on firm AI adoption came from worker/firm surveys (subject to recall/reporting bias). This paper uses internal usage data, linking accounts to job titles and message-level task classifications.
