Source: https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/dumpster-fire/

x.com
links in a popup so you can read the one post and leave.
* It uses the same API that powers X's embed widgets.
* It never sends your cookies.
* The openable
x.com
links are marked with a small dot.
