We’re excited to share that Decagon has been named a Cool Vendor in the 2026 Coolest Vendor Innovations in CRM report by Gartner, Inc.
“We’re thrilled to be recognized as a Gartner Cool Vendor,” said Jesse Zhang, co-founder and CEO of Decagon. “Getting an AI agent into production is only the first step. What will differentiate companies is how quickly their teams can learn from production and improve performance."
That requires visibility into where customers are getting stuck, which interactions are falling short, and which changes will have the greatest impact. Decagon gives teams that evidence and the control to act on it safely.
Putting agent iteration in operators’ hands
From the beginning, Decagon has been built around operator-led AI: the belief that the people closest to the customer should be able to directly shape how an agent behaves.
Agent Operating Procedures
(AOPs) put that concept into practice by giving operators a way to express business policies as natural-language instructions.
Earlier this year, we introduced
Duet
to make building and managing agents faster and easier. Duet Autopilot takes that further by closing the iteration loop, turning production signals into validated changes staged for human review. This allows organizations to act on many small improvements that would be impractical to address manually but compound into meaningful gains over time. As Autopilot takes on more of the iteration work, CX teams can step into higher-leverage roles as AI Experience Architects— setting direction, reviewing changes, and deciding what ultimately reaches customers.
Global enterprises including American Airlines, Delta Air Lines, Deutsche Telekom, Hertz, Mercado Libre, and Oura Health use Decagon to deploy and continuously improve AI agents across complex, high-volume customer interactions.
We’re grateful to our customers for trusting us to build alongside them and to the entire Decagon team for continuing to raise the bar.
