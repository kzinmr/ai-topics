# New Discovery: Readwise & Reader

Source: https://danielwirtz.com/blog/readwise-reader

I’ve recently discovered
Readwise
and
Reader
, and have become a subscriber after a 30-day trial. Especially the Reader is amazing. It’s probably the single best reading and highlighting experience you can wish for regarding PDFs, ebooks, and web articles. Even YouTube videos are supported. In combination with an iPad, it’s a great – and smarter – replacement for a Kindle.
