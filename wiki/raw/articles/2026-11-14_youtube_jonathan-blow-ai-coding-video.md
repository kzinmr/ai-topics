Jonathan Blow — "If AI solved coding, why is my software still the same?" — 1h11m talk posted Nov 2026.

Topic: a senior working game designer/programmer's perspective on why LLM-assisted coding has not, in practice, transformed the experience of shipping large software products. Blow's argument is the structural one: the bottleneck on his team is not typing speed or initial code generation, but the slow accretion of design coherence, tooling debt, and cross-system understanding that AI has so far not addressed.
