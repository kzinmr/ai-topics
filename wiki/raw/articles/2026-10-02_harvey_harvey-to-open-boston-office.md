Harvey to Open Boston Office
by
Harvey Team
•
Oct 1, 2026
Boston is home to universities, hospitals, and businesses that shape industries and drive research globally. Lawyers are central to that progress. This fall, Harvey will open an office in Boston to work more closely with them.
Our customers across the area reflect the breadth of the legal market, from global law firms and specialized practices to in-house teams. They include law firms Ropes & Gray, Mintz, Foley Hoag, and Verrill, as well as in-house legal teams at DraftKings, athenahealth and Toast. Through our law school program, we also partner with Boston University to invest in the next generation of lawyers.
The office will deepen those relationships, bring us closer to legal teams across New England, and support our rapid growth with law firms and in-house teams alike.
Katie Burke, Chief Operating Officer at Harvey said, “Both our Chief Revenue Officer and our VP of Commercial started their sales careers in Boston, and many of our customers are either headquartered or operate here, so it seemed a natural fit for us to invest in opening an office. Lawyers are central to so much of what happens in Boston, and we look forward to building a great team here to support our customers in New England and beyond.”
Jessica Collins, Chief Legal Officer at athenahealth said, "Harvey has been an invaluable tool for our legal team to help drive efficiency and impact at athenahealth. As legal organizations play a more pivotal role in AI transformation at their companies, they need a tool purpose-built for legal with privacy and security as a top priority. We are excited to have the Harvey team opening in Boston.”
We have 10 employees in Boston today across Legal Innovation, Marketing, People, Commercial Legal, Customer Success, and Sales. Boston’s depth of talent is a key reason we’re building that team here as we serve a wider range of customers. We’re actively hiring across sales, recruiting, legal engineering, and customer success.
