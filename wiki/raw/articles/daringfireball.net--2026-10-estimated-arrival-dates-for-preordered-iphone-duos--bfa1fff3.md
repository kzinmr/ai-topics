Observations:
There were way more people who successfully got October ship dates than I expected. It’s very close to even with November ship dates.
The fact that there are fewer December ship dates than January, is to me, a sign of Apple’s appreciation for the holidays. I think Apple is only promising “December” for shipments they are 100 percent certain they can deliver by Christmas. I bet a bunch of the orders promised for January will in fact ship in December — albeit perhaps too-late-for-Christmas December.
Apparently some configurations are getting
an estimated date of February 1
. I wish I’d listed February as an option, but I don’t think it would have made a big difference.
Per MacRumors today
, some pre-order estimates are already moving up, like from early December to late November.
No one should mistake my audience as representative of the general public in any poll, but I bet my audience is at least ballpark representative of the general public when it comes to something like pre-ordering, at the crack of dawn, a new iPhone form factor that costs $2000 to $3200.
