# Jensen Huang Apologizes to Cheering Nvidia Employees on His Return

Source: https://www.businessinsider.com/jensen-huang-returns-to-nvidia-hq-im-terribly-sorry-2026-11

Jensen Huang
returned
to Nvidia's Santa Clara headquarters Monday to cheers from employees, less than two weeks after his stunning ouster.
Nvidia employees gathered at headquarters to mark the moment, chanting "Jensen! Jensen!" as he arrived back at the office, according to a person familiar with the scene. The crowd was so packed that employees jostled for a glimpse. Rather than his
signature black leather jacket
, the source said he appeared to be wearing a navy cotton coat.
Huang put his hand on his heart several times and said, "This is my first day at work," according to a video of the scene. "I'm terribly sorry for putting all of you through all of this."
"I'm gonna be a better CEO the second time around," he said, according to another person in attendance.
"I don't have a computer, so the IT department is gonna give me a new PC," Huang added.
In public posts,
Nvidia employees
flocked to social media
to
celebrate
Huang's return to the company. A "
Stand with Jensen
" petition calling for his reinstatement had previously garnered more than 200,000 signatures, including thousands who identified as current employees.
Nvidia did not immediately respond to a request for comment from Business Insider.
Nvidia's board reinstated Huang as CEO on Monday, reversing course just 11 days after his resignation amid revelations about his
personal relationships
and an ongoing investigation into whether he had been targeted by a
foreign intelligence operation
.
Prominent names in business also
applauded Huang's reinstatement
, while President Trump said he "
convinced
" Nvidia's board to make the decision as the company's stock had
plummeted in his absence
.
