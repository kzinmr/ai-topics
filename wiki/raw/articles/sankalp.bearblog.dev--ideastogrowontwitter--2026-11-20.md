## Framing

The author has posted for 4+ years and explicitly aims the advice at **making
connections** rather than **building an audience**: making friends with like-minded
people, discussing ideas, collaborating, eventually meeting — "an internet version of
pen friends". He reports having met 50+ mutuals so far.

## Recommended readings

- **How To Twitter Successfully** by @nearcyan — "one of the best posters", praised for
  its range-by-range treatment of growth.
- **Guide to This Bird App** — his own 2023 guide, ideas picked up "by f***ing around
  and finding out", plus ideas from @amuldotexe and @visakanv.
- **Twitter Article on How To Tweet** (via Ojasvika) — covers crafting a good tweet and
  "recognizing your style".
- **@visakanv's** mass-twitter thread (Feb 2019) — "lots of universal ideas".

The thesis: growth boils down to **not hacks but actually posting regularly and
interacting with people**.

## The 80-20 basics

**Get Twitter Premium/Premium+.** Accounts without Blue get less reach; Premium boosts
timeline and replies. "Using Twitter without it is playing on hard mode. Most people
underestimate network effects."

### 0–1000 range

The hardest range — you need to **reply-guy and interact with relevant people
regularly**.

- Tell your friends to follow you; find "terminally online" friends. Expect grinding
  and a real time commitment.
- Find relevant people by interacting with those who seem naturally interested in you
  and who pay attention to replies — he knows people with up to 50k following who still
  interact well with mutuals. **Avoid audience-builders** in this range, since they
  don't prioritise paying attention to you. Prioritise people who pay attention once
  you put in effort.
- Mine the follows/lists of people with good taste (e.g. his own AI list) and lurk.
- Interaction yields follow-backs, DMs, group-chat invites — GC participation raises
  the odds of appearing on their timelines. As momentum builds, shift from
  reply-guying toward posting.

### Posting ideas for sub-1k (and 1k–2k)

Post regularly about: things you find interesting; something from founder life; what
you are building; something you want to teach / that is valuable; what you like about
certain products. Posting compounds — "writing and posting leads to more thoughts".

### Post regularly and observe the meta

- At least **1–2 posts a day**, otherwise you fall off until momentum returns (applies
  at all follower ranges; easier to recover with a larger following).
- **Observe the timeline**: what's in the discourse, can you add something meaningful,
  any funny meme ideas. "This is the most important thing to do. Being familiar with
  the meta."
- **Time posts to the meta.** His viral Claude Code 2.0 blog was already in progress;
  when Andrej Karpathy posted about Claude Code he accelerated and published as early
  as possible to ride the wave ("great content + presentation + timing").
- Use X Analytics audience/timing data and run A/B tests.
- Quotes @netcapgirl (May 2025): you have to be tuned into the meta to be in a posting
  flow state — "cant take days off... has to just be second nature, unconscious".
