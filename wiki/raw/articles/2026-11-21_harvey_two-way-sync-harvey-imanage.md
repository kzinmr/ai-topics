Introducing Two-Way Sync With iManage
Two-way sync with iManage lets lawyers save redlines, new versions, and first and final drafts directly to iManage from Harvey.
by
Harvey Team
•
Nov 20, 2026
Watch Video
On a live matter, the documents never stop moving. A counterparty markup comes back, a term gets renegotiated, a new ancillary agreement is needed. Each change runs through several documents, and every one of them has to end up as the current version in iManage.
Lawyers can already search and bring documents from iManage into Harvey to draft, compare, analyze, and then manually export back into iManage. Now they can save redlines, new versions, and first and final drafts directly to iManage from Harvey.
Harvey’s
two-way sync with iManage
, now available in early access, lets collaborators work across a matter’s documents within Harvey. As Harvey works, output is synced back to iManage with version history intact. Users can save several documents in a single approval, and the synced Vault and iManage are updated together. The work for the matter happens in Harvey, while iManage remains the system of record.
One Request. Every Change Saved in iManage
Consider a deal team working from a synced Vault. When the terms are renegotiated, the lawyer asks Harvey to update the purchase agreement and schedules and review a new escrow agreement if needed. Harvey pulls the latest versions from iManage and shows what it will save: two new versions and one new document.
When the lawyer approves the save, Harvey files each document back to iManage as its next version, and the Vault and iManage update together. All three documents are saved within a single approval, while earlier versions remain in each document’s history, providing a complete view of iManage version history within Harvey.
The same pattern applies across a matter: responding to a counterparty markup, refreshing a diligence memo as the data room grows, or updating a litigation chronology with new transcripts. In every case, the lawyer controls when changes are saved back to iManage.
Why Two-Way Sync Matters
Revision is only one part of the job. Lawyers must also draw on the firm’s collective experience, review their work, and share it with colleagues. Connecting these steps creates a continuous process that strengthens both individual lawyers and the broader team. With two-way sync, you can now:
Start with the firm’s knowledge.
Prior agreements and comparable deals help lawyers assess language in the context of work the firm has already done. Bringing those documents from iManage into Harvey gives the lawyer relevant material to reference when drafting and reviewing a revision.
Work at the scale of the matter.
Legal work rarely changes only one document. Harvey carries a change through every document it touches and saves each one back as the next version, with no download and re-upload step.
Keep the team working from the matter’s record.
The updated agreement belongs with the documents and version history the deal team already relies on. Saving back to the existing iManage document gives colleagues a clear place to find the reviewed revision and see the versions that came before it. Harvey checks check-out status and write access before saving, so a colleague’s work is not overwritten.
The firm’s documents provide context for work in Harvey. The reviewed work returns to iManage, ready for the next person who needs to pick it up.
Two-way sync is now available in early access. Contact your Harvey account manager to request access.
