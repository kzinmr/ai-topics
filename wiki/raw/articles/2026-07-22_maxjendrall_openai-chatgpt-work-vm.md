# OpenAI ChatGPT Work: 15GB RAM, 9-Core VM for All Paid Customers

> "pssst people haven't realised that OpenAI literally just rolled out a 15gb RAM 9 core VM in ChatGPT to ALL their paid customers with ChatGPT Work.
>
> Non technical people now have remote machines available for their agents with enough resources to do most economically valuable work"

This tweet by Max Jendrall highlights a significant infrastructure development: OpenAI has rolled out ChatGPT Work, which includes a 15GB RAM, 9-core virtual machine to all paid ChatGPT customers. This effectively gives non-technical users access to remote machines capable of running agents for economically valuable tasks.

Key implications:
- OpenAI is building agent infrastructure directly into ChatGPT's paid tier
- 15GB RAM / 9-core is sufficient for most knowledge-worker automation
- This represents a shift toward "agent-as-infrastructure" where the VM (not just the model) is part of the product
- Non-technical users can now access compute resources through ChatGPT that previously required DevOps knowledge
