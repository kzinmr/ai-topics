# SS&C Intralinks Now Available in Hebbia

**Source**: [https://www.hebbia.com/blog/hebbia-integrates-with-ss-and-c-intralinks-to-connect-live-deal-data-to-ai-workflows](https://www.hebbia.com/blog/hebbia-integrates-with-ss-and-c-intralinks-to-connect-live-deal-data-to-ai-workflows)

SS&C Intralinks Now Available in Hebbia
Partnerships
By Paul Boland
05.26.26
Hebbia now integrates with Intralinks, bringing secure, governed deal content directly into its AI platform.
Hebbia is integrating with SS&C Intralinks to give joint customers access to data from Intralinks DealCentre AI, the AI-enabled dealmaking platform built for secure, high-stakes financial transactions. Teams can connect live deal content from DealCentre directly into Hebbia to streamline diligence, accelerate analysis, and work from a continuously current source of truth, with the security, governance, and compliance controls trusted across the M&A ecosystem.
Closing the Dataroom Workflow Gap
Data rooms are central to most deals, but managing them is manual and time-consuming. Teams spend hours downloading and re-uploading VDR contents, losing version control the moment a file is updated, or responding to diligence questionnaires with answers scattered across the data room. With DealCentre AI connected to Hebbia, teams select a deal and approved content, structure, and updates flow directly into Hebbia, preserving version control and giving teams confidence that analysis is based on the latest information available. Hebbia also surfaces every data room change, so teams can see exactly what the other side has updated since their last review.
Built for the Deal Ecosystem
This integration reflects a broader shift in dealmaking technology toward connected, AI-enabled workflows built on trusted infrastructure. The Intralinks integration is part of an ongoing effort to meet deal teams inside the systems where their documents already live. It joins existing integrations across financial content platforms, data providers, and research feeds, all routed into the same workspace where analysis happens.
"The biggest unlock for customers is removing the manual work between where their documents live and where they actually do the analysis," said Danny Wheller, VP of Growth and Strategy at Hebbia. "By integrating with Intralinks DealCentre AI, customers can work from live transaction data inside Hebbia while benefiting from the security, governance, and compliance standards that are essential in high-stakes diligence. That means faster answers, fewer manual steps, and greater confidence in the output."
The integration will be available to all Hebbia customers with active deals on Intralinks DealCentre AI. For Intralinks, it extends a legacy of innovation that began with inventing the virtual data room and continues with DealCentre AI, the only AI-enabled dealmaking platform designed to help teams move faster while maintaining the control and oversight required for complex transactions.
