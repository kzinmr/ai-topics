# Apple’s iPod recreation

Source: https://unsung.aresluna.org/apples-ipod-recreation/

Just
a week ago
, I bemoaned the lack of any good iPod click wheel explainers, and today the news broke about Apple Music
hiding a functioning iPod on their 404 page
.
I am usually not a fan of easter eggs – most of them are poorly made – but this might be the best iPod recreation I’ve seen, and it’s an interesting thing to study:
I think it generally covers all the modes etc.
(with small exceptions) the typography looks accurate,
it has the little “haptic” beeps,
it has proper acceleration and the scrollbar,
it has a little music library with various playlists,
I have only noticed a few small bugs.
Most importantly, it works well on mobile, too, so you can pretend you’re holding an iPod in your hand. Worth spending a bit of time with it, especially if you have never used an iPod in your life.
(You might have to copy/​paste the link to the browser. Some of the page chrome and ads feel a bit in the way, but it’s not a big deal, and you can remove them using the Hide Distracting Items feature in Safari.)
Kudos to whomever put this together. This is how you do easter eggs.
I tagged this as
emulation
, even though this is a grounds-up recreation in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
