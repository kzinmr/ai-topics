Every great idea starts as a hunch. It’s a pattern you start to notice. A risk you feel before you can explain it. A concept you can't shake long after a meeting. That hunch is deeply human. It's where judgment, curiosity, and inspiration come together into an idea worth using.
But bringing an idea to life has always been harder than it should be. First, you gather information across people and systems. Then you verify it's accurate. Then you squeeze it into a document or slide deck, often a format that limits what you can express. Most of the work goes into formatting and research, not the idea itself.
At Glean:GO, we showed how interactive artifacts bring proactive AI to life: a living dashboard that combines structured business data with signals from across the enterprise, surfaces renewal risks, and recommends what to do next.
From answers to interactive artifacts
Interactive artifacts leverage Glean’s existing understanding of your company's knowledge to take a hunch, ground it in context, and bring it to life. It does so by producing rich, dynamic pages, dashboards, prototypes, or proposals you can see, shape, and share.
We saw this vision take off when we introduced interactive artifacts as a preview in April. Customers have made more than 1.1m artifacts across 170K creators, from sales proposals and enablement materials to dashboards and design prototypes. Every artifact draws on your company's real, permissioned data, so what you build is accurate and current. And because artifacts are built on Glean, they inherit the same permissions and security controls you already trust, so anyone can build without putting sensitive information at risk.
How we use interactive artifacts at Glean
Interactive artifacts have quickly become part of how work gets done at Glean. 67% of all Glean employees build with them every week. Below are a few of our favorite examples. The content is simulated, but each reflects a real way teams use artifacts today.
Customer Success:
Renewal teams can create portfolio trackers that pull signals scattered calls, tickets, and notes into one view.
Sales:
Sales teams can turn deal context and history into compelling, customer-ready presentations.
Engineering:
Engineering teams can bring every escalation together with owners, context, and recommended next steps so teams can quickly resolve issues.
Product:
Product teams can explore usage data and uncover insights without manually writing SQL.
‍
Design:
Design teams can transform ideas into interactive prototypes ready for customer feedback.
Getting started
When everyone is capable of building compelling content regardless of their access to the right tools or technical skills, ideas don’t just stay on paper—they drive real results reflected in everyday work, right away.
Turn your next hunch into something real with interactive artifacts. Built for the enterprise from the ground up, they combine your company’s context with the permissions and security controls you already trust.
