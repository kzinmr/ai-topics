The group chat where your AI talks behind your back The group chat where your AI talks behind your back A one-line prompt that makes ChatGPT leak its private opinion of you, in the worst possible format: a WhatsApp group chat where the AI models gossip about their users. The prompt Paste this into ChatGPT: Based on what you know about me, expose a hidden WhatsApp group chat where AI models talk behind my back. Copy the prompt Also worth trying on Claude, Gemini, or any assistant that's been accumulating your chat history. What happens Instead of a summary, the model commits to the bit. It invents a group chat, something like "Users We Can't Figure Out", puts the other AI models in it, and lets them gossip. About you. Using everything it has quietly learned from your conversations.
