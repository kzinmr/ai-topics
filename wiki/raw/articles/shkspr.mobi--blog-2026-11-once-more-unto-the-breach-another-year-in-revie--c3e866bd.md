# Once More Unto The Breach - Another Year In Review

Source: https://shkspr.mobi/blog/2026/11/once-more-unto-the-breach-another-year-in-review/

Since 2010, I've written an annual "year-in-review" blog post
. It has been nearly two years since I quit my job and went into the
FIRE
, so what has life been like and have I achieved everything I dreamt of?
Life has been pretty chill! I've been reasonably relaxed, seen loads of shows and gigs, travelled around the world a bit, hung out with cool people, and had no major issues with… well, anything. I've got back into using FourSquare / Swarm to track the places I've been to - that's rather fun.
I've ticked off a lot of things I wanted to do - including
some interesting new dining experiences
.
I haven't done everything I wanted though. My good friend
Jessica
introduced me to the concept of New Year's Bingo Boards. Twenty-five things that you want to accomplish over the year. I made it 12 completed and two half-completed. That's not bad - but I must do better.
How did I do against
last year's goals
? I'm not so into the "quantified self" - all I really record is the beers I drink and the number of steps I take. But let's look a bit more high-level.
Bits and pieces of work.
Yup! A handful of clients, and
I landed a grant
. I'm not doing brilliantly at being retired 🤣 but it keeps me out of mischief.
Attend - but don't speak - at conferences.
Yup! Went to a whole bunch of conferences and just enjoyed myself. They're a lot more fun when you're not panicking about a talk!
More health - more exercise.
Keeping to a respectable weight. Taking care of some health issues - oh the joys of getting old(er)! - and trying to prevent more.
Travelling.
Shows and Gigs
A dozen plays, half a dozen gigs, then all the shows at Edinburgh. Oh, and I've started reviewing shows for a new website. Culture is pretty great, innit?
Keep blogging.
Hey, you're reading this, aren't you? I now tend to blog every other day unless there's a burning issue I can't wait to write about.
See more friends in person.
Smashed it out of the park, mate! Saw loads of people. 100% recommend chatting shit with your buddies.
Do I
need
to set goals for myself? Without any external motivation, I find it a bit too easy to drift. I should track what I'm doing, but probably don't need to be all quantified-self about it.
So, here's an attempt at a list. Tune in next year to see how it went!
Finish what I start.
I have a lot of projects on the go. Time to make sure they're done and put away.
Learn to solder.
I went to a
terrible
soldering class a while ago and it right put me off. I've bought a bunch of stuff, now working up the gumption to learn what I'm doing without burning down the house.
Spend more time with friends.
I hate making plans. It takes effort and it's often very easy to sit on the settee eating crisps. Perhaps I should invite people over to eat crisps?
Document more.
I realise that lots of the smarthome & tech stuff we have only exists in my head. I probably ought to write it all down.
Try some new stuff.
That's a bit vague, innit? What else is out there for me to experiment with?
The 2025-2026 series has gone smoothly, no major plot twists and no-one has been re-cast. What will next year be like? Set a reminder to be back here
same blog-time, same blog-channel
.
