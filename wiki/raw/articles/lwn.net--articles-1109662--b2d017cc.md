# Pop!_OS 26.04 LTS released

Source: https://lwn.net/Articles/1109662/

Pop!_OS 26.04 LTS released
[Posted November 25, 2026 by jzb]
System76 has
announced
the release of Pop!_OS 26.04 LTS, which is based on
Ubuntu 26.04 LTS. Notable features in this release include drawing
tablet support, automatic updates, a number of new COSMIC applications, and
more. Updates for users on Pop!_OS 24.04 LTS will be enabled in
December.
LWN
last covered
Pop!_OS and
an early version of its COSMIC desktop in August 2024.
