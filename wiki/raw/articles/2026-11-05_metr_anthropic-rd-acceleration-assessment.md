Independent Assessment of AI R&D Acceleration at Anthropic in October 2026 — METR

METR conducted a deep-access investigation into how much Anthropic's AI systems are accelerating their own capabilities research. Selected findings from the public summary:

Core conclusion:
"As of October 2026, Anthropic is likely improving the capabilities of their models on well-specified tasks at around twice the rate they did between March 2024 and October 2025. This estimate is given with high uncertainty; we would not be surprised if the true acceleration was closer to 1.3x or 3x the historical rate."

Key empirical findings:
- Since October 2025, benchmark scores (measured via AECI, Anthropic's version of the Epoch Capabilities Index) for Anthropic's models have been growing at an estimated 1.9x the pre-October 2025 rate [95% confidence interval: 1.5-2.8x], implying that Anthropic has made roughly two years of historical progress over the last year.
- "We are confident that the acceleration was not due to an acceleration in the scaling of training compute."
- Interviews and surveys are consistent with AI usage causing a doubling in the pace of capabilities progress. Anthropic staff self-report dramatic uplift from use of AI systems.
- A majority of the most-informative evidence METR found was REDACTED by Anthropic. METR: "much of the redacted evidence we viewed had multiple plausible interpretations, and both METR and Anthropic researchers have significant uncertainty about how to synthesize the evidence into a holistic picture. This makes it hard for the public to judge this evidence themselves [...] this report does not achieve 'transparency about evidence'."
- METR's information pipeline was relatively narrow; a small number of requests for data were denied. Staff-level access would have enabled a significantly deeper assessment.

Relevant to Anthropic's Responsible Scaling Policy "dramatically accelerated progress due to AI" threshold, which Anthropic stated in its S-1 it "may imminently meet."
