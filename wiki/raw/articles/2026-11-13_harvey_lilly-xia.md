Lilly Xia
Software Engineer at Harvey
Lilly Xia is a Software Engineer at Harvey, where she builds the systems that bring law firms' tools and data into Harvey. Before Harvey, she scaled the media data ingestion platform for YouTube TV and Primetime Channels at Google, and developed authentication services at Amazon to keep customer accounts secure. She holds a B.S. in Computer Science and Mathematics from the University of Michigan.
