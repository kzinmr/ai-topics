Getting started
Today, at OpenAI DevDay, the company launched Marketplace, a new platform for AI-powered tools for its enterprise customers. ElevenLabs is proud to be included as a launch partner for the marketplace. Eligible enterprise customers will be able to access ElevenAgents through the marketplace and apply their OpenAI commitment to purchases.
The
marketplace directory
is live today with ElevenAgents listed, and purchasing will be available soon. Interested teams can register through the directory now.
Frontier intelligence, real-time conversation
Building production-ready agents requires frontier reasoning, the best audio models that listen, understand, and respond like a human in real time, and a cohesive platform that provides integrations with your existing tools, knowledge bases, guardrails, and built-in analytics. ElevenAgents brings all of these layers together in one place, pairing OpenAI's frontier models with our vertically integrated conversational AI stack.
Teams at Stripe, Deutsche Telekom, and Klarna rely on ElevenAgents every day to resolve inbound support end to end, qualify leads, recover abandoned carts, and automate scheduling. ElevenAgents is the platform enterprises use to build, deploy, and monitor human-sounding agents in more than 90 languages with industry-leading accuracy and ultra-low latency across voice, chat, and email.
How GPT models perform in ElevenAgents
Across our enterprise ElevenAgents deployments, GPT models perform strongly on complex reasoning and reliable tool calling. Customers can now combine OpenAI frontier models with ElevenAgents’ real-time conversational platform under a single contract, making it faster and simpler to deploy AI agents at scale.
"ElevenAgents combines OpenAI’s intelligence with our real-time, human-like voice agents to listen, reason, respond, and take action,” said Ashish Uchil, Head of BD & Partnerships at ElevenLabs. “Now this Marketplace makes it one easy purchase.”
