Harvey’s Connector Library is Now Available
Harvey's Connector Library brings together the tools your legal team already uses, so research, drafting, and analysis run with full context.
by
Harvey Team
•
Oct 21, 2026
Reviewing an agreement means knowing how similar terms were handled before, what the other side has already said, and what the business or client needs from the deal. Those details are often scattered across document management systems, data rooms, contract platforms, and email and chat threads.
The
Connector Library
, which is now generally available, brings that information together in Harvey so lawyers can spend less time gathering background and more time delivering informed advice.
Watch Video
Connect Harvey to the Tools You Use
Developed with our trusted early access customers, Harvey’s Connector Library brings together a growing collection of connectors legal teams rely on, giving Harvey the context to deliver more relevant answers and drafts. With write-back support starting with your DMS platforms, context flows into Harvey and finished work flows back to its source.
Here’s what makes Harvey’s Connector Library different and what you can do with it:
1. Start with connectors reviewed by Harvey
Every connector goes through Harvey's rigorous
security review and monitoring processes
, with governed deployment. This gives legal teams a helpful starting point and reduces the burden of due-diligence, giving them greater confidence to deploy what they enable.
2. Let the Harvey Agent find what you need
Instead of picking and choosing a connector to work with, the Harvey Agent dynamically suggests relevant connectors based on the task you're working on. Once connected, Harvey brings that context into its analysis, with citations that let you trace answers back to the sources. The library becomes part of the workflow, surfacing connected tools as the task calls for them.
3. Tailor Harvey to your tech stack
Harvey’s Connector Library is designed to grow with your team’s needs. If you can’t find a tool in the library, you can add a custom MCP connector for an internal system or third-party platform.
Our early access customers are already putting that flexibility to work, connecting third-party MCPs for everything from business intelligence platforms to niche data sources. They’ve also connected their own context layers, bringing information they’ve already gathered and organized directly into Harvey.
With more of that context at hand, legal teams can deliver informed advice faster, helping clients and the business resolve issues, make decisions, and move work forward.

Turn Institutional Knowledge Into Action
Whether you’re helping your company resolve a vendor issue or preparing for a client’s deposition, the information you need often lives across several systems. Here’s what that looks like in practice:
Vendor termination analysis and action
: A vendor keeps having service outages. Can your company end the agreement? You ask Harvey. It combines Jira outage tickets and IT team Slack threads with structured contract data already extracted by Docusign, including SLA thresholds, obligation history, and termination rights, to deliver an analysis for your review. When you’re ready, Harvey triggers a Docusign workflow to open a termination request, route approvals, and send the notice for signature — all without leaving Harvey. You spend less time piecing together the facts and more time advising the business on its next move.
Deposition prep:
You're preparing for a CFO's deposition and need to establish when they knew the company's revenue forecasts were unreliable. You ask Harvey to draft the outline. Harvey works from the relevant case materials in Everlaw, your firm's own work product in NetDocuments, and the client's latest instructions in Outlook, so the draft is grounded in the actual evidence, your firm's strategy, and the client's priorities rather than generated from thin air. You spend your time refining the questions, not tracking down documents.
In both cases, the Connector Library helps lawyers turn scattered information into informed advice faster, so clients and the business can make better decisions and move work forward.
A Growing Library for the Way You Work
Think about the next urgent question that will land on your desk. For legal teams, that question often starts in a channel like Slack or Jira. With
Intake
, you can configure your connectors so that employees can send a request to Legal and Harvey gathers the context it needs, routes the request to the right agent or lawyer, and replies in the same thread. Harvey’s agents can draw on your business context and relevant legal knowledge to help you connect the dots faster.
The Connector Library is a one-stop shop for connecting Harvey to every tool a legal team uses, regardless of the size of the technology provider. That means growing the library and deepening what teams can do with each tool so that lawyers don’t have to think about what lives where.
