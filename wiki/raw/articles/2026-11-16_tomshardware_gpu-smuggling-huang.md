# Smuggling of 1.5M AI GPUs could be the cause of Huang's departure

Tom's Hardware, 2026-11-14. Analysts claim more than 1.5 gigawatts of banned chips (roughly 1.5
million AI GPUs) were tracked flowing to China despite export controls, and argue this could be the
underlying cause of Jensen Huang's departure as NVIDIA CEO on November 12, 2026 — rather than the
personal-affairs reporting that dominated coverage.

Context from this wiki: [[events/nvidia-ceo-transition-2026]] records the resignation itself, CFO
Colette Kress as interim CEO, the first-ever Board Chair (Harvey C. Jones), and Huang's agreement to
testify before Congress by Thanksgiving following the Mast/Moolenaar/Crawford letter. The WSJ
circular-financing thread ([[concepts/ai-infrastructure-circular-financing]]) and Treasury Secretary
Bessent's demand that NVIDIA's board answer export-control questions within 10 days sit in the same
news cycle.
