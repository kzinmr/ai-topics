# The next chapter of our AI momentum

**Source:** https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/next-chapter-ai-momentum/
**Date:** 2026-08-05
**Authors:** Sundar Pichai (CEO, Google and Alphabet), Demis Hassabis (Chair, Google DeepMind and Chief Scientist, Alphabet)

## Summary

Google and Alphabet CEO Sundar Pichai announced major leadership changes at Google DeepMind:

### Key Changes

1. **Demis Hassabis** → Chair of Google DeepMind and Chief Scientist of Alphabet (previously CEO of Google DeepMind). Will focus on shaping the future of AGI and continue leading Isomorphic Labs.

2. **Koray Kavukcuoglu** → SVP of Google DeepMind (previously CTO of GDM and Chief AI Architect). Will oversee Gemini model development, Frontier AI research, and the Gemini app and developer teams. 13+ years at DeepMind; started deep learning team; led WaveNet and DQN breakthroughs.

3. **Jeff Dean** — Leaving Google after 27 years. Launching an independent public benefit corporation with Google Senior Fellow **Sanjay Ghemawat** to accelerate discoveries in ML, science, and engineering. Google will be a founding investor and Cloud partner.

### Key Metrics Mentioned
- Gemini app: **950M+ monthly users**
- Gemma models: **900M+ downloads**
- Flash model: high demand
- Cyber model: live
- Upcoming model releases including **Gemini 4**
