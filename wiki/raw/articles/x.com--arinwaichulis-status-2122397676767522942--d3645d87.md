Boom! Apple's Full Disk Access changes arrive in latest macOS 27.3 beta.

Mac users can now:

• Limit an app to specific folders, not the whole Mac
• Get clearer warnings about what’s being handed over
• Limit which apps a tool can control
• Have option to make apps re-ask for access
• Menu bar indicator when one app controls another

Full Disk Access is NOT going away, and anything already approved keeps its access.
