# Edinburgh Fringe: Doris, Dolly and the Dressing Room Divas

Source: https://shkspr.mobi/blog/2026/08/edinburgh-fringe-doris-dolly-and-the-dressing-room-divas/

Quite simply marvellous! Three Scottish divas giving us their interpretations of the greats while backed by an excellent pianist.
So many shows at the Fringe skimp on sets and props - not this! A luscious array of costumes, hats, wigs, and ruby slippers bring the characters to life.
The framing is thin but serviceable - the backstage helpers are reminiscing about their favourite stars and bring them back to stage. It is a fun and campy evening, without ever straying into parody or obsequiousness.
Sadly a few technical gremlins crept in, but the cast are absolute troopers and dispensed with them.
Oh, and there's an incredible Sweary Poppins who steals the show.
In amongst the Fringe's high concept art, fiendish new writing, and blokey-standup, it was a real treat to see a good old fashioned revue.
Highly recommend.
