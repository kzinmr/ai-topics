# Daniel van Strien — Britannica illustrated pages dataset (X post, Aug 25 2026)

## Post

**Aug 25 2026** (https://x.com/vanstriendaniel/status/2092295830518562868):
> Uploaded a dataset of 115,293 illustrated pages from the Encyclopaedia Britannica, 1st edition (1768) to 14th (1929) to the Hub https://huggingface.co/datasets/biglam/britannica-illustrated-pages

## What's in it

- **Dataset**: `biglam/britannica-illustrated-pages` — 115,293 illustrated pages scanned from editions 1 (1768) through 14 (1929) of the Encyclopaedia Britannica. Published under the `biglam` (Big Science / GLAM) org on the Hugging Face Hub.

## Significance

Follow-on to Daniel's August 2026 `biglam/british-library-book-images` release (1M British Library book images + crop-tightening model + semantic search Space — see [[raw/articles/2026-08-19_vanstriendaniel_bl-book-images-crop-search]]). The Britannica 1768–1929 run is a distinct, high-value public-domain corpus: nearly 160 years of Western knowledge illustration in one dataset, useful for VLM pretraining/fine-tuning, digital humanities research, and RAG over historical reference material. Continues the `biglam` org pattern of shipping large, curated, culture-sector image corpora to the Hub.
