AI agent swarms unleashed in Minecraft to 'build a thriving civilization' — 50-agent teams fail after falling into paralyzing bureaucracy

Y Combinator's research lab (labs.ycombinator.com) unleashed teams of up to 50 AI agents into Minecraft with the goal of building a thriving civilization. The teams failed — not for lack of capability, but by falling into "paralyzing bureaucracy": agents spent their time coordinating, delegating, and appointing roles rather than building. Reported example: four agents spent 11 minutes appointing a "sole fertilizer operator."

The agents were instructed to build modular, re-usable snippets of useful code as part of "civilization" building. Failure modes observed: agents getting stuck in coordination loops, wasting time on role negotiation, and losing track of the actual task.
