Summary
Music v2.5 is now the default for prompted and reference generation in ElevenMusic.
Songs sound more layered and complex, with better, more natural-sounding instruments.
You own what you make in ElevenMusic, on every plan, including Free.
Free gets five lossless downloads a day, Pro gets 400 lossless downloads per month.
Downloads are blocked on tracks that reference other artists' songs.
When you make a song, it should be yours.
Today we're releasing Music v2.5, with lossless downloads available on every plan, including Free.
The model
Music v2.5 is ElevenMusic’s best music model yet. The instruments sound real. Songs have more layers, more movement, and they hold together all the way through.
In a blind test with the same prompt and two takes (one from each model) across 47,885 pairs, v2.5 was the preferred take the majority of the time.
The gap is widest with songs that are vocal-led and acoustic-heavy genres like R&B, soul, hip hop, rock, metal, orchestral and cinematic music.
2.5 is the new default in ElevenMusic, but if you're attached to Music v2, don't worry. It isn't going anywhere.
Your music
Every track you make in ElevenMusic is yours. On the Free plan you get five lossless downloads a day, and you can use what you make commercially as long as you credit ElevenMusic. On Pro, you get 400 lossless downloads per month. The permissions that apply when you create a track stay with it, so cancelling or downgrading doesn’t change how you can use tracks you’ve already created.
This holds true in the future, too. If we ever change our terms down the line, the change applies to new tracks from that day onwards. The songs you already made are yours to keep.
The one thing you can't take with you is a track built on another artist's song. ElevenMusic was built with artists, labels and publishers, and the rules that protect your music protects theirs too.
Music v2.5 is live in ElevenCreative and the ElevenLabs API, too. Score the video you just made in Studio, run Music as a node in Flows and re-run the track on its own until it fits, and prompt inside of your own product.
