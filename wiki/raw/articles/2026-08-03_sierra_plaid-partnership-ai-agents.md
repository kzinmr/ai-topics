Summary:
Sierra and Plaid announced a partnership to connect AI agents directly to financial infrastructure. Through this partnership, customers can securely connect their bank accounts with Plaid directly within Sierra's AI agent platform. This moves AI agents from purely conversational interfaces to agents that can take real business actions — checking balances, initiating transfers, and completing financial transactions.

This follows Sierra's launch of Horizon, a platform that enables agents to work across long time horizons. The Plaid integration represents a concrete step toward agents that don't just answer questions but complete business outcomes — turning conversations into completed transactions.

Key points:
- Plaid integration available directly within Sierra's agent platform
- Customers can securely connect bank accounts through the AI agent interface
- Moves agents from "one-and-done" Q&A to transaction-completing autonomy
- Builds on Sierra Horizon for long-horizon agent capabilities
- Partnership bridges conversational AI and financial infrastructure
