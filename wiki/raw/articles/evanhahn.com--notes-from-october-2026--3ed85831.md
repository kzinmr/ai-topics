# Notes from October 2026

Source: https://evanhahn.com/notes-from-october-2026/

Happy Halloween! Here’s a little of what I got up to in this spooky month.
Things I did in October
I moved this site from an Apache-powered VPS to CDN-frontend object storage. This means I pay less money, I do less maintenance, and my site loads faster in most parts of the world. Hopefully nobody noticed this change, except that things should be a little snappier! (Also, my old site had a small amount of PHP, which I’m happy to have removed given PHP’s
remote code execution vulnerabilities
this month.)
In an effort to
fill in the gaps of the internet
, I published a short
list of Vim forks
.
At Ghost, my team shipped two additions to our automations feature:
analytics
and
more granular triggers
. Both of these should help our independent publishers grow. More automations stuff on the way!
Like every month, I wrote
a few articles at Zelda Dungeon
, including
a debate over, perhaps, the most trivial disagreement possible
.
