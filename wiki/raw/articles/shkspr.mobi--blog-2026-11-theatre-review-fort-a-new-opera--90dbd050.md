# Theatre Review: FORT - a new opera

Source: https://shkspr.mobi/blog/2026/11/theatre-review-fort-a-new-opera/

Close quarters battle opera! The full force of two very talented singers blasted directly at an audience sat mere centimetres from the stage, and ricocheting around the tiny venue.
This was a free showing of a brand new opera based on the play by Tabitha Hayward.
Daniella Sicari
and
Lucy Gibbs
are ridiculously talented - but I think the score is perhaps a little too basic to do them justice. There's not a great deal melody, just an endless warble of sing-speaking. It ends up feeling a mite repetitive - which shouldn't really happen in a one-act show.
The accordionist,
Alise Siliņa
, is incredible. She was able to conjure a whole soundscape from a single instrument. More than just music, she provided surreal sound effects and a true air of delirium. Having live accompaniment make such a positive difference to these sorts of shows.
The opera is about teenage histrionics which, when multiplied by the natural exuberance of opera, make the show almost unbearably intense. There are a
lot
of plot elements stuffed in to the 75 minute running time. Ghosts! Teenage pregnancy! BFF breakups! Sibling rivalries! Tragedy! It becomes difficult to keep up with what is being sung. And yet the show does drag on a touch. Just when you think it has reached a natural climax, it finds a new diversion to explore.
It is great that new operas are still being works hoped, developed, and performed. There is a lot to like about FORT, but I think it tries to pack in too much into too small a space.
