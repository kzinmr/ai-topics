# How Claude's text watermarking works

Future Claude models will generate text that contains a watermark — a way of determining the likelihood that Claude was involved in writing the text. Anthropic, along with several other major AI providers, is implementing this to comply with the EU AI Act.

## Summary

- No practical impact on quality or content of Claude's outputs
- Watermarked vs un-watermarked text indistinguishable to readers
- Nothing added to text; no hidden characters
- No extra tokens, not more expensive
- Carries no identifying information; can't be traced to a person, organization, or chat
- Not Claude-specific: as of August 2, the EU requires AI providers serving its market to mark AI-generated content. Other major model developers signed the same Code of Practice and will implement their own watermarks.

## How it works

LLMs generate one word at a time, choosing among candidate next-tokens. For low-stakes choices (e.g. "overcast" vs "grey"), the choice is settled by a random number. Watermarking uses these low-stakes choices to leave a pattern that is undetectable to the reader but detectable to anyone holding the key. Instead of an arbitrary RNG, the watermark uses the key and preceding words to settle which word to pick. One can then check whether a sequence is consistent with Claude's key and assign a probability that the text was generated by Claude.

## Method

A version of **SynthID-Text** (Google DeepMind, Nature 2024), in the family going back to Scott Aaronson's 2022 proposal. DeepMind found no statistically significant quality differences in Gemini A/B traffic; human raters saw no quality difference.

**Limitations:** only answers "likelihood this was partly written by Claude" — doesn't confirm human authorship or identify other AIs. Detection weak on small samples; confidence rises with length. Watermark is sparser on factual passages (fewer safe choices) and code (exact outputs carry no watermark; comments can carry it).

## User implications

- No slowdown, no extra cost
- Not traceable to users/organizations/chats
- Detection API coming soon
- Files (png/jpg/svg): C2PA content credentials (cryptographically signed metadata note), an open standard also used by camera manufacturers
- Light editing probably won't remove the watermark; a complete rewrite will
- Translations carry a watermark (every word chosen by Claude)
- Older models (pre-Aug 2, 2026) get a transition period; watermarking to be added over coming months

## Why

Compliance with the EU AI Act. Anthropic, with ~190 total signatories, signed the EU Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content in July 2026. Applied globally at launch (no durable way to scope by region yet). Differs from AI-detection software (e.g. Pangram) which lacks the key and relies on stylistic "tells."
