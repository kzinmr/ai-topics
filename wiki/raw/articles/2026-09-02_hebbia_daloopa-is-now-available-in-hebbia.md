# Daloopa is Now Available in Hebbia

**Source**: [https://www.hebbia.com/blog/daloopa-is-now-available-in-hebbia](https://www.hebbia.com/blog/daloopa-is-now-available-in-hebbia)

Daloopa is Now Available in Hebbia
Partnerships
By Paul Boland
09.01.26
Daloopa, one of the most comprehensive financial data feeds, is now available to Hebbia customers.
Hebbia customers can now access data from Daloopa, which provides comprehensive financial data covering more than 6,000 public companies globally, with every figure linked back to its original filing.
Customers can pull that data directly into their work in Hebbia, where they build and update their own models and analyses on verified numbers. With the integration, customers get the full context to make faster and sharper decisions.
Deeper Fundamentals, Linked to the Source
Financial professionals covering public companies spend much of their time assembling numbers, pulling reported figures from filings, and checking them against the source every quarter. Daloopa captures far more line items per company than a typical feed, so finance teams can build and maintain models in Hebbia with every figure linked back to its filing.
“This partnership brings Daloopa's audit-ready financial data across 6,000+ global tickers directly into Hebbia's customizable AI platform for research and analysis,” said Gabriella Hernandez, Vice President of Partnerships at Daloopa. “Together, we are providing a faster, more streamlined way to surface trusted, source-linked data right inside firm-specific Hebbia workflows.”
The Daloopa MCP integration will be available to Hebbia customers with an active Daloopa subscription. It joins Hebbia's growing set of connections to the data sources analysts already trust, bringing the depth and auditability Daloopa is known for into the AI platform where the work happens.
