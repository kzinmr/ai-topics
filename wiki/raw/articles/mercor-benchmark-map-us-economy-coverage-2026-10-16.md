# Mercor Benchmark Map: 90% of US working hours are uncovered by AI benchmarks

Source: https://www.mercor.com/research/labor-index/benchmark-map/ | Published ~2026-10-16

Mercor compiled existing AI benchmarks and measured their coverage of the US labor force using
the US Dept. of Labor O*NET taxonomy (923 occupations, 18,797 tasks, 22 job families).

Headline findings:
- Only 208 of 923 occupations have any task covered by at least one benchmark prompt. 6 of 22
  job families have NO occupation with a 5+-prompt task.
- ~90% of American working hours are spent on tasks with little or no coverage from existing
  benchmarks. "It is very hard to know how good AI models are at the work Americans actually do."
- 924 benchmarks counted in the index.
- Coverage is NOT random: the AI community focuses on the largest labor markets first. A
  task-level logistic regression shows coverage probability rises with occupational wage bill
  and AI-exposure tag (Eloundou et al. 2023). Benchmarks heavily cover AI-exposed lawyers' tasks
  but barely cover similarly AI-exposed legal secretaries or court clerks. The long tail of
  smaller, middle-income knowledge work is undercovered.
- Positioned as a first step toward a continuously updated resource; adjacent to Mercor's
  Remote Labor Index and APEX benchmark.
