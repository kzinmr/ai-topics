GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol, Coming Soon to Harvey
Announcing GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol.
by
Harvey Team
•
Nov 16, 2026
GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol, OpenAI’s latest models, are coming soon to Harvey.
In our early testing, GPT-6.2 Astra fully completed more than three times as many tasks on LAB as GPT-6 Astra. It performed best on regulatory and litigation work, with strong results in international trade and sanctions, environmental and ESG, and litigation and dispute resolution.
GPT-6.2 Astra also showed strong judgment on contested matters. When drafting both an internal memo and a filing for the same client, it laid out the client’s weak spots in the memo, where the legal team needs to see them, and kept them out of the filing, without conceding ground or raising theories the other side hadn’t. Its drafts were also more often complete documents, ready to file or send, while using about a third fewer tokens than GPT-6 Astra.
GPT-6.2 Sol outperformed GPT-6.1 Sol in most practice areas, with its strongest results in data privacy and cybersecurity, corporate M&A, and corporate governance. It also delivered more accurate work while using less than half as many tokens as GPT-6.1 Sol.
For full results against our Legal Agent Benchmark, you can refer to the public leaderboards on
Artificial Analysis
and
Vals
.
GPT-6.2 Astra will be available in GA for Extended AI customers starting this week. GPT-6.2 Sol will be available to all Harvey customers starting this week.
