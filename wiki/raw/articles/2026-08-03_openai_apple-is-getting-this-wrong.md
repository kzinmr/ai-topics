# Apple is getting this wrong | OpenAI

OpenAI published a public response to Apple's lawsuit, accusing Apple of making false claims. Key allegations from OpenAI:

1. **Apple's lawyers emailed the wrong person**: Apple claimed they contacted OpenAI in February without response. OpenAI revealed that Apple's outside lawyers emailed the wrong person after confusing two Asian last names.

2. **Fabricated meeting with General Counsel**: Apple claimed they had a discussion with OpenAI's General Counsel, which Apple now concedes never happened.

3. **Apple never raised specific allegations**: Apple told OpenAI they were "resolving any issues" and then went silent for five months before suing.

4. **Employee departure dispute**: Apple accuses Chang Li of taking trade secrets. OpenAI claims Li's work was entirely open-source and public.

5. **Broader context**: This is part of Apple's aggressive legal campaign against AI companies hiring former Apple employees, raising questions about non-compete enforcement and talent mobility in AI.

OpenAI characterizes the lawsuit as "careless, aggressive and oddly personal" and publishes email correspondence as evidence.
