Hebbia Partners with EQL Global
Hebbia partners with EQL Global to bring Nordic and European company data into the platform.
By Paul Boland
11.18.26
Hebbia has partnered with EQL Global, a Swedish data provider that collects reports, filings, and earnings call transcripts directly from listed companies. EQL covers more than 33,000 companies in 89 countries, including every listed company in Europe. Customers can now use EQL's filings, transcripts, press releases, financials, and estimates inside Hebbia, alongside their own documents and the other data sources they already use.
How Firms Use Hebbia and EQL Global
Information on Europe's largest companies is very accessible, but small and mid-market firms are much less covered. EQL fills the coverage gap, so deal teams can analyze Nordic and European companies the same way they already analyze US ones.
Asset managers work through an earnings season across EQL’s coverage. Equity research teams review a whole sector of Nordic companies at once. Bankers and private equity teams research a European target before a deal. Every number links back to the company's own report, and new reports arrive within about a minute of release.
“With EQL Global’s first-party data, Hebbia customers now get the same depth on European companies as on US names. They can screen a European sector, read a German annual report, or go through a French earnings call, and because every source comes straight from the original publisher, European banks and asset managers can build AI workflows that meet European compliance requirements.” said Sahng Ibrahim, CEO and co-founder, EQL Global
