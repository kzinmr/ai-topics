# Mistral OCR 4.1

OCR 4.1 is Mistral's latest OCR service powering its Document AI stack, with **native paragraph-level bounding box extraction**, **structural block labels**, and **block-level confidence scores**.

- **Model ID**: `mistral-ocr-4-1`
- **Pricing**: $4 / 1,000 pages (OCR); $5 / 1,000 annotated pages
- **Features**:
  - BBox Extraction (`/v1/ocr`)
  - OCR (`/v1/ocr`)
  - Annotations — Structured
  - Batching (`/v1/batch`)
- **+2 Speed | Performance | Modalities** (marketing emphasis relative to prior version)

The page sits in the Mistral Docs "Inference & Models → Models" hierarchy alongside Z.ai GLM 5.2, Shieldstral 1.0, and Mistral Medium 3.5.
