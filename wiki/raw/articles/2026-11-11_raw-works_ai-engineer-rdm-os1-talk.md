## Key points (from description + existing wiki context)

- Extends the RLM (Recursive Language Model) harness pattern from generative LLMs to
  **decision models / System One classifiers** (the fast-classifier line associated with
  Diogo Almeida's "System One" framing and TypeSafe AI's Jev).
- Introduces **Recursive Decision Models (RDMs)** — already documented at
  `wiki/concepts/recursive-decision-models.md` (Weitekamp's Oct 1, 2026 raw.works post).
  This talk is the first public video treatment of the idea.
- Application areas named: **coding agents, information retrieval, computer use** —
  positioned as "far more efficient workflows" (i.e. not every agent decision needs an LLM).
- New: **"Operating System One"** — Weitekamp's vision for real-time interfaces, named as a
  deliberate counterpart to "System One" decision models. Not yet in the wiki.
- New: **oSo** — a preview of a *computer-use decision agent* (https://oSo.computer).
  Not yet in the wiki.
- Canonical resources: https://recursivedecisionmodels.com , https://github.com/rawwerks/rdm ,
  https://openprose.ai
