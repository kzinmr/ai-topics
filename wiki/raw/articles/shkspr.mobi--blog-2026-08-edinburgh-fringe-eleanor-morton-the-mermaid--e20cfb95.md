# Edinburgh Fringe - Eleanor Morton: The Mermaid

Source: https://shkspr.mobi/blog/2026/08/edinburgh-fringe-eleanor-morton-the-mermaid/

The undisputed queen of short-form viral video comedy snacks takes to the stage. Well, swims to the stage.
The Mermaid is a delightful and gentle amble through the confusing world of a girl who just wants to become a mermaid. Less standup and more storytelling, nevertheless it packs in the laughs with some rather clever jokes, a bit of bawdy humour, and - a rarity on The Fringe - a decently designed set!
It stands out by being well-rehearsed (surprisingly not a given here) and with no technical glitches during the performance (and, again, I cannot stress enough how unique that is). Eleanor Morton is an absolute professional who knows how to put on a quality show.
