## Source: pogueman.substack.com

David Pogue's review of Amazon's Alexa+, the LLM-powered next-generation voice assistant. The review is scathingly critical, describing Alexa+ as a "buggy embarrassment" that fails at basic tasks, hallucinates, and represents a significant step backward from the original Alexa.

Key themes:
- Alexa+ was supposed to be Amazon's LLM-powered response to ChatGPT, but the execution is deeply flawed
- The system frequently hallucinates, gives wrong answers, and fails at basic smart home commands
- Amazon rushed the product to market, using customers as beta testers
- The original Alexa's reliable command-and-control model has been replaced with an unreliable LLM that needs constant correction
- Represents broader challenges of deploying LLMs in consumer voice assistant products

The review raises significant questions about the readiness of LLM-based voice assistants for consumer deployment, the trade-offs between open-ended conversation and reliable command execution, and whether the current generation of LLMs is suitable for the voice assistant use case.
