# What's New: November Disclosure 2026

**Source**: [https://www.hebbia.com/blog/whats-new-november-disclosure-2026](https://www.hebbia.com/blog/whats-new-november-disclosure-2026)

What's New: November Disclosure 2026
Product
By Aabhas Sharma
11.03.26
Team dashboards, Hebbia in PowerPoint and Copilot Cowork, Projects on mobile, and more are now live in Hebbia.
Last month’s updates make the work you already do in Hebbia go further, with dashboards built for teams, Hebbia alongside you in Microsoft Office and on mobile, and more data to analyze.
Dashboards built for collaboration
Dashboards are quickly becoming a go-to way to work in Hebbia, so we’ve made them easier to use. Anything you fill in or check off stays saved, you can leave comments right on the section they apply to, and when someone refreshes the numbers, everyone you’ve shared it with sees the update.
Hebbia wherever you work
Microsoft PowerPoint add-in
Generate a deck in Hebbia and keep iterating in Microsoft PowerPoint, where the same conversation you used to create it comes with you to MS Office. Ask about tables, chart data, and speaker notes, and make deck-wide changes in a single request. The add-in is available now in the
Microsoft App Store
alongside Word and Excel.
Hebbia in Microsoft
Copilot Cowork
Hebbia is now available in Microsoft Copilot Cowork through our MCP, so you can bring Hebbia’s analysis and data into Copilot without leaving the app.
Export Microsoft Office files as PDFs
Export PPTX and DOCX files as PDFs, with or without a password. Share finished work in a format that’s ready to send, and protect it when the content is sensitive.
Collaboration on mobile
Open, browse, and work in your Projects from the Hebbia mobile app, and get a push notification on iOS when someone shares a chat or agent with you or a scheduled agent finishes running. Tap to go straight to the work, and choose what you’re notified about in Settings.
More data sources and models
Octus
Octus is now a source in Hebbia, bringing credit research like court filings, capital structure, and covenants into your analysis. Sign in with your own Octus account and results follow your entitlements.
Datasite
Datasite, a leading platform for M&A data rooms, is now connected to Hebbia. Bring deal content into your analysis alongside the rest of your research.
Hong Kong Stock Exchange
ICE market data in Hebbia now covers HKEX-listed tickers, intraday and historical. Ask about price performance, volume, and trends across Hong Kong equities the same way you would for other markets.
Microsoft Teams
Query your Teams conversations as a data source in Max, so context from your conversations sits alongside your filings, research, and deal content.
Google Drive
Connect Google Drive as a data source in Hebbia, so the documents your team already keeps there are available in your analysis without a manual upload.
New frontier models
Access Claude Haiku 5.5 in Hebbia, now replacing Haiku 4.5 for all customers.
