# A quote from John Gruber

Source: https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/25/john-gruber/

25th September 2026
Muse is getting a lot of attention — including mine — because it’s both groundbreaking technically (each user gets their own entire persistent Linux VM running in Meta’s cloud)
and
because it’s packaged in an easy-to-install easy-to-use way. It’s literally presented as
a cute mascot
. It’s the first consumer-accessible agentic AI system, and Meta has truly done an amazing job with that. But it’s a genuinely open question whether consumers have any understanding what this means. If you buy a power saw that can cut your fingers off, you are almost certainly aware that you are buying a power saw that can sever your fingers. [...] I don’t think people realize how powerful — and thus dangerous — Muse is, especially if it’s running on your Mac.
—
John Gruber
,
Muse Looks Cute, but Looks are Deceiving
