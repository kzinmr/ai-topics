KDE Gear 26.08 released

Version 26.08
of

include improvements in the
signing
features of
Okular
, improved file-grouping
features in the
Dolphin
file
manager, and a number of enhancements to the
Kdenlive
video editor. See the
changelog
for
a full list of updates, enhancements, and bug fixes.
