## Original game

Rescue Raiders was a game by Sir-Tech Software for the Apple II. The player flies a Choplifter-esque helicopter over a user-created armored column; the trick is to build the perfect column (tanks, soldiers, etc.) that can overtake the enemy's column running in the other direction and get a truck full of explosives to the other end of the board.

## Significance

Demonstration of multi-agent iterative creation: Claude iterated "back-and-forth with critic & art agents" to rebuild a beloved abandonware game with new graphics, goals, and tech trees — a non-coding-adjacent creative use of agent loops highlighted by Mollick.
