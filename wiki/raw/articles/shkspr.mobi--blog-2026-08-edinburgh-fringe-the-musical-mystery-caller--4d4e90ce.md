# Edinburgh Fringe: The Musical Mystery Caller

Source: https://shkspr.mobi/blog/2026/08/edinburgh-fringe-the-musical-mystery-caller/

Walking down the street, I noticed a ringing phone box so, naturally, I answered it.
What happened next was a few minutes of pure fringe magic!
I had a little chat with the caller, he welcomed me to the smallest venue in the fringe, and then he improvised a banger of a song based on my peculiar proclivities.
What a treat to have a front row experience at a one man (and one audience member) show. The song was heartfelt and beautiful, played and sung with panache - albeit down a twisted copper pair of limited bandwidth.
He didn't reverse the charges, so it didn't cost me a penny! Which, given the high prices of most shows, was an absolute bargain 😃
If you hear a phone ringing, you should always answer it and say hello.
