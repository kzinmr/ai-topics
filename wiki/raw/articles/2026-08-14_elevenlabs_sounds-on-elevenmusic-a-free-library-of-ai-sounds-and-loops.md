Browse and download AI sounds and loops for free
Sounds gives producers and creators a growing library to explore without needing to buy sample packs. Every sound is free to browse and preview, simply sign into
ElevenMusic
to download the sounds and loops you want.
So whether you need a vocal chop to start an idea, drums to build around, or a bass loop to complete a track, you can find the perfect sound on ElevenMusic and start working with it immediately.
Generate the sound you need
Sometimes you know what a track needs, but finding the right sound takes longer than making it. With free daily generations on ElevenMusic, you can describe the sound you have in mind and generate it in no time at all. Whether you need a single isolated stem or a fuller layered loop, simply describe the character of the sound you are after and move quickly from an idea to something you can use in a track.
A library that grows as people create
We are building a different kind of sample library, one that isn't limited in size. That's why all generated sounds join the Public Library, so Sounds keeps expanding as people use it.
Producers can discover sounds made by others, generate something more specific when they need it, and contribute new material for the next person to find.
Start making music with Sounds on ElevenMusic
Music often starts with something small like a drum pattern, a bass line, a vocal fragment, or a sound that gives you an idea for everything that follows. Sounds brings those starting points into one place, with a free library for discovery and generation for when the sound you need doesn't exist yet.
