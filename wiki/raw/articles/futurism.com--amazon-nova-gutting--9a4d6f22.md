# Amazon Is Gutting Its AI Division After Sustained Failure (Futurism, citing Bloomberg)

Unlike its tech-megacorp peers (OpenAI/ChatGPT, Anthropic/Claude, Google/Gemini), Amazon's in-house flagship model line "Nova" is nowhere near a household name — and Bloomberg reports Amazon is now gutting its in-house AI labs.

Key facts (per Bloomberg insiders, via Futurism):
- Amazon is scaling back ambitions of most Nova models and reorganizing AI development teams to narrow focus on "highest priority" goals after its power-hungry LLMs "turned out to be a bust".
- Most AI models — text Nova models plus video- and image-generation models — are put in a "keep the lights on" state: technically still supported, minimal resources.
- Labor and compute are being diverted to a singular "frontier-model effort" led by Pieter Abbeel (Berkeley Robot Learning Lab director; his robotics company Covariant was acquired by Amazon in 2024). The direction aligns with Amazon's push to automate everything — robotics-first.
- A week earlier, Amazon closed a key AI office in San Francisco: an 80-person site doing AGI research.
- Futurism framing: amid questions about whether LLMs can deliver ROI let alone AGI, Amazon may be "jumping out of the AI hype cycle while it still can".
