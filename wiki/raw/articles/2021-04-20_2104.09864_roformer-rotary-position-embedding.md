## Source: arXiv:2104.09864

RoFormer: Enhanced Transformer with Rotary Position Embedding. The foundational paper introducing Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE), which has become the dominant position encoding method in modern LLMs including Llama, DeepSeek, Qwen, Mistral, and others.

Key contributions:
- Introduces Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE) which encodes position information through rotation matrices
- Unlike absolute position embeddings, RoPE naturally captures relative position information
- RoPE can be seamlessly integrated into the self-attention mechanism
- Theoretically grounded in complex number rotation
- Demonstrates improved performance on long-text tasks compared to learned absolute position embeddings

RoPE has become one of the most impactful innovations in LLM architecture, adopted by virtually all major open-source models since 2023.
