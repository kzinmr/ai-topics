# Gemini 3.7 Flash: our most intelligent workhorse model

> "Our most intelligent workhorse model yet for coding and agents." — Tulsee Doshi, Senior Director, Product Management, on behalf of the Gemini team.

Google DeepMind introduced Gemini 3.7 Flash, building on the Flash series just three weeks after Gemini 3.6 Flash. It delivers substantial improvements across software engineering, knowledge work, and web development workflows, with an introductory price of half the original 3.6 Flash cost per million tokens.

## Better intelligence for complex workflows

Coding gains over 3.6 Flash (debugging, issue resolution, higher first-pass code accuracy, production-ready code):
- **FrontierCode 1.1 Main**: 43.6% vs 34.4%
- **DeepSWE v1.1**: 65.3% vs 49.0%

Web development: more functional layouts and feature-complete apps in fewer prompts. High design adherence/parity from a reference input (screenshot, image, or full design system). **Arena.ai WebDev Arena Elo: 1588 vs 1538** (3.6 Flash).

Knowledge-dense fields (finance, law, biosciences):
- **GDP.pdf benchmark** (complex document processing): 34.0% vs 22.0%
- **AutomationBench** (real-world business workflows): 30.4% vs 17.0%

## Better developer experience and price

Improved developer experience over 3.6 Flash: better adapts to roadblocks, clarifies intent when needed, follows instructions with greater fidelity, "thinks more diligently" with more effort into multi-step planning and tool calls.

Introductory price (through end of year): **$0.75/1M input tokens and $3.75/1M output tokens**.

## Improving Gemini Spark with 3.7 Flash

Gemini Spark (Google AI Pro/Ultra, 160+ countries) uses 3.7 Flash starting today. Spark is Google's 24/7 personal AI agent. Update makes Spark more efficient for knowledge work with improved tool use for Google Workspace apps.

## Built with safety in mind

Shipping with updated Frontier Safety safeguards against misuse in Chemical, Biological, Radiological, and Nuclear (CBRN) and cyber offense domains, while enabling beneficial use cases.

## Availability

- **Developers**: Gemini API (Google AI Studio, Android Studio), Google Antigravity agent-first workflows
- **Enterprises**: Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, Gemini Enterprise app
- **Individuals**: Spark in the Gemini app (Pro/Ultra subscribers)

Full model card available for 3.7 Flash.
