Our AI-native video editor now generates video, image, voice, music, and sound effects directly inside your project - then lets you edit, caption, and export in the same place.
Studio 4.0 is a major upgrade to Studio, the video editor in ElevenCreative. Video production is usually split across tools: one for generating footage, one for voiceover, one for music, one for editing. Studio 4.0 brings the full process into a single project. Describe the shot you need and it generates straight to your timeline, alongside your voiceovers, music, sound effects, and images. Refine any shot without starting over, then finish and export without leaving the editor.
Studio
Generate directly in your project
Every generation lands where you need it: on the timeline. Generate video from a prompt, create images for storyboards or stills, add voiceovers from over 10,000 voices in 32+ languages, and score your edit with generated music and sound effects.
Because generation happens inside the project, iteration stays fast. If a shot is close but not right, refine it in place - no exporting, re-uploading, or rebuilding your edit around a new file.
A rebuilt editing timeline
The Studio 4.0 timeline is built for precision:
Frame-level zoom for exact cuts.
Clip snapping for fast, accurate placement.
Clips that stay in sync as you move them.
Captions work like any other clip. Split a cue, merge two, or fix a mistranscribed word directly on the timeline - no re-transcribing, no external caption tools.
Studio Agent, your AI co-editor
Studio Agent is built into every project. Describe your idea and it drafts a first cut on the timeline - placing clips, generating voiceovers, and syncing sound effects to your footage. It analyzes your clips frame by frame, so you can ask for a sound effect at the exact moment your product appears.
Take over the timeline manually at any point. Make your edits, then hand control back, and the agent continues from where you left off.
Collaborate with your team
Comment directly on a clip or asset, so feedback stays attached to the work instead of scattered across separate threads. Review, respond, and resolve without leaving the project.
A rebuilt playback engine
Studio 4.0 ships with a rebuilt playback engine that stays responsive as projects grow - scrubbing, seeking, and previewing remain smooth on long timelines with many layers.
Built for creators and marketers
Studio 4.0 is designed for creators and marketers producing finished video end to end - social clips, product teasers, narrated explainers, and cinematic sequences composed from generated assets. Generation, editing, sound design, and captions in one project.
