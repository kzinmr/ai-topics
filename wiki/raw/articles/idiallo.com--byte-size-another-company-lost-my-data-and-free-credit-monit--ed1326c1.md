# Another company I've never heard of lost my Data

Source: https://idiallo.com/byte-size/another-company-lost-my-data-and-free-credit-monitoring

I received a letter from Premise Health Holding, a company that I had never heard from before. Apparently sometime in March 2026, one of their employees started fiddling with customers' data. And no one
noticed until September
of this year.
In the letter, they say that my data was affected, including name address, social security. Everything important to impersonate me. I'm happy they have notified me. Truly. But I'm sorry, who are you? And why do you have my information in the first place?
As far as I can tell, I've never done business with them. Why do they have my social security of all things? And the worst part is that their penalty for having lost my data is that they offer me  1 year of free credit monitoring.
Jokes on them because I already have free credit monitoring for a whole year. Or more like 6 months left now. That's right, another company I had never heard of had also lost my data to bad actors this year. And as a result, they had also offered me free credit monitoring.
What happens if the bad actor waits a full year before using my data? After all, my social security will remain the same. I can't really change that.
Unless there is a solid penalty for losing data that cannot be recovered, this will continue to be routine. Just offer 1 year credit monitoring that most people won't use and you are set.
Shortly
after my kids were born
, one of them received a letter, addressed to him by name. It was to inform him that his personal information had been breached.
