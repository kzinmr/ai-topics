# Edits on Desktop: Free Video Editing on Mac

Source: https://edits.instagram.com/blog/edits-desktop-video-editor/

We built the
desktop editor
with creators in mind. It has everything you need to make great videos for any platform, free and customizable by you. Creators get Instagram's licensed and royalty-free audio libraries, hundreds of sound and video effects, and unlimited automatic captions that support multiple languages. To make getting started easier, the audio and text styles you saved on mobile are already waiting for you on desktop. And you can set up the keyboard shortcuts you already know, whether they're your own or ones carried over from another editor.
Filmmaker and creator Alex Cissé (
@alexcisse
) was an early tester for our desktop app. He shares, “What desktop changes is space. You get a bigger screen and more precise controls, but you keep everything that makes Edits, Edits: the Instagram music library, sync with your profile, and direct upload.”
