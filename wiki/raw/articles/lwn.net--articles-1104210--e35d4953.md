# Haas: Postgres 19 is late, and it's AI's fault

Source: https://lwn.net/Articles/1104210/

Haas: Postgres 19 is late, and it's AI's fault
[Posted October 30, 2026 by corbet]
PostgreSQL developer Robert Haas
writes
about the struggles
the project has encountered in putting together its
next release.
In the end, I don't think that the delay in shipping PostgreSQL 19
	is particularly concerning. It is not a long delay in the grand
	scheme of things, and it emerges directly from the project's
	concern for quality.
The long-term picture is less clear and in some ways more
	troubling: given that we have forty years of bugs that humans were
	unable to find but LLMs are quite capable of discovering, how are
	we ever going to catch up, and how will PostgreSQL continue to
	innovate? Right now, I'm not sure. What I do know is that I, and
	many others, are working really hard to figure it out.

looked at the PostgreSQL 19 release
and its challenges in September.
