# Edinburgh Fringe - James Rowland: Team Viking

Source: https://shkspr.mobi/blog/2026/08/edinburgh-fringe-james-rowland-team-viking/

This is a technically precise and emotionally complex show with energetic delivery from a talented performer.
Sadly, it did nothing for me.
I found it a little one note in terms of storytelling. The rhythm of the story followed all the predictable beats which I thought was a little repetitive.
There's no doubt he's a gifted storyteller. The comedy is suitably dark with some good moments of joy and catharsis. It is probably the perfect show for some people but I wasn't the right audience for it.
