Find Every Auto Renewal Clause in Your Portfolio Before its Notice Window Closes
An auto renewal clause can set a notice deadline months early. Sort contracts by renewal mechanism, map 30 to 180-day windows, and give each one an owner.
by
Harvey Team
•
Nov 6, 2026
An auto renewal clause, also called an automatic renewal clause, extends a contract for another term unless a party gives notice by a set deadline. Tracking that deadline on one agreement is simple. A portfolio multiplies the deadlines, and Zylo's
2026 SaaS Management Index
found that the average organization manages 211 SaaS renewals annually, before IT services and facilities agreements are counted.
Take a hypothetical in-house team with about 400 supplier agreements across SaaS, IT services, and facilities. In this example, 260 of them renew automatically on January 1 unless someone gives notice. Their notice periods run 30, 60, 90, or 180 days, so the deadlines fall anywhere from July 4 to December 1.
Reading 400 agreements for the same few renewal facts is the slow part, and AI can take it on. Harvey's review tables pull each agreement's renewal terms into columns linked to the source clause, so the team's time goes to checking those facts and deciding what to renew. We'll follow this team from sorting its contracts to getting each owner's decision in before the notice deadline.
An Auto Renewal Clause Extends the Term Unless Someone Gives Notice in Time
An automatic renewal clause sets four facts to capture: the renewal term, the notice period, who can give notice, and how notice must be delivered. Here's a sample clause from one of the team's agreements, a $240,000-a-year analytics subscription, written for this example:
"This Agreement will renew automatically for successive twelve (12) month renewal terms. Either party may prevent renewal by giving the other written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term. All notices must be delivered to the addresses set out in Section 14.2."
Read as data, it gives a 12-month renewal term, a 60-day notice period, a right either party holds, and a delivery rule that points to Section 14.2. A contract auto renewal clause often splits those facts across sections, so the notice address may sit in a general notices clause or in a later amendment. Missing any one of them can leave a notice of non-renewal open to challenge.
The Renewal Mechanism Decides Whether Silence Keeps a Contract or Ends it
Before any date matters, each contract needs a label for what happens if nobody acts. An auto renewal contract keeps running on silence, while an option to renew lapses on silence, so the two call for opposite actions. Here's how the team's 400 agreements sort, with all counts hypothetical:

Whichever label your team uses, record what silence does, since that decides whether a contract has a deadline to watch. The rest of this example follows the 260 January 1 renewals.

Each Row Ends in a Decision to Renew, Renegotiate, or Exit
The renew, renegotiate, or exit call with the team, and the table gives each owner the facts to make it. Each owner starts from their row on the review date and decides by the decision-due date, which leaves time to deliver notice before the working deadline.
Renew.
The managed IT services agreement met its service levels, and its renewal price increase is capped at 5%. Because the provider holds company data, the security team first checks for changes such as new subprocessors. The IT operations lead then records a decision to renew, and no notice goes out.
Renegotiate.
The company is consolidating offices, so the workplace operations director wants the facilities agreement to cover less space at a lower fee. If talks may run past July 2, the team either gets the deadline extended in writing or sends a notice of non-renewal in late June. Because that notice ends the contract on December 31 unless both sides sign new terms, counsel reviews its wording first. Renewal is also a good moment to bring older
supplier agreements
up to legal's current standard.
Exit.
The business has moved to another analytics platform, and the subscription would renew at the provider's then-current list price. By Friday, October 30, the data team lead emails the notice of non-renewal to the address in the latest amendment and asks for written confirmation of receipt. Unless the amendment replaced Section 14.2's delivery rule, the notice also goes by that method. Privacy and security reviewers make sure the exit covers the return or deletion of company data.
A few states also regulate business renewals.
New York General Obligations Law Section 5-903
can make an automatic renewal in a service or maintenance contract unenforceable unless the provider first sends a written reminder. The team's New York HVAC contract falls in that group, so the statute candidate column sends it to counsel. The workplace operations director decides to switch providers, and the team gives its own notice of non-renewal before December 1 rather than wait for the provider's letter.

Auto Renewal Clause Questions, Answered
Are auto renewal clauses enforceable?
They can be, though some statutes make one unenforceable when the provider skips a required notice or disclosure.
New York and Wisconsin do this for some business contracts. Counsel should check the governing law and the clause's wording.
How can CLM tools differentiate auto renewal contracts from those that require notice?
Give each contract a renewal mechanism field with a fixed list of values, kept separate from its renewal date.
A date field alone can't show whether silence renews a contract or ends it. Fill the field from the clause itself, since intake data may not reflect later amendments.
Can you get out of an automatic renewal contract after the deadline passes?
Sometimes, through a termination for convenience right, an early exit the provider agrees to, or a statute such as New York's when the provider skipped its required notice.
Have counsel check before you stop paying, since withholding payment can put your company in breach.
What happened to the FTC's click-to-cancel rule?
The
Eighth Circuit vacated
the FTC's 2024 amendments to its Negative Option Rule on procedural grounds in July 2025.
The original 1973 rule applies again, and it covers only prenotification plans, so it doesn't reach most automatic renewals. The FTC's March 2026
advance notice of proposed rulemaking
asked whether these practices are prevalent in deals between sophisticated businesses. No proposed rule had followed as of October 2026, and federal consumer rules don't replace the state-law analysis for B2B contracts.
How do you prevent missed contract renewal deadlines?
Name a backup owner for each row so a deadline survives vacations and departures.
Rerun the extraction whenever an amendment lands, since it can change the term, the notice period, or the notice address.
