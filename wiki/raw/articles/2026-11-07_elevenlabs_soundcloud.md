We've launched a new integration with SoundCloud to help get your music heard. You can now create your track in ElevenMusic, and publish it on SoundCloud in a few simple steps.
How it works
Open your profile settings and go to Linked Accounts, then connect your SoundCloud account. From there, open an original track you've created in ElevenMusic, add a description, genre, and tags, and click “Save to SoundCloud”.
That's it. Your track uploads straight to your SoundCloud profile, ready for the world to hear.
A special perk for SoundCloud Artist Pro users
If you’re a SoundCloud Artist Pro member, you can unlock three months of ElevenMusic free, on us. Then it’s $9.99/mo unless you cancel. 18+, ends 31 Oct 2027,
terms apply.
