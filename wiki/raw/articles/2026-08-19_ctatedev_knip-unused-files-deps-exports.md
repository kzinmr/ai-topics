## Knip (webpro-nl/knip)
- **What:** "Find unused files, dependencies and exports in your JavaScript and TypeScript projects. Knip it before you ship it!" — an open-source developer tool (npm: `knip`).
- **Purpose:** remove unused files, unused dependencies, and unused exports from JS/TS projects — reduces repo bloat and attack surface, and is especially handy as a post-AI-coding cleanup step (agentic coding agents frequently leave dead code/unused deps behind).
- Chris Tate's endorsement is one more data point for Knip being a practical, low-friction hygiene tool in the modern JS/TS + AI-assisted toolchain.
