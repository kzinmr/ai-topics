# Why compute might get 10x+ more expensive in coming years

Dwarkesh Patel argues that AI compute prices could increase dramatically, not decrease, as models get smarter. The core argument:

**If a human-level software engineer could run on an H100 equivalent, at current market rates for software engineers, that H100 should rent for over $250k a year — 15x today's spot price.**

Key points:
- Anthropic revenue has 10xed year over year, projecting ~$100-150B by end of year
- For this trend to continue, the models need to get substantially smarter — but smarter models require more inference compute
- The paradox: as models improve, the value they can deliver increases, which drives up demand for inference compute
- Reasoning models (chain-of-thought, test-time compute) consume orders of magnitude more compute per query than simple forward-pass models
- The market may shift from "cheap, fast inference" to "expensive, high-value inference" — similar to how human experts command premium rates

This challenges the conventional wisdom that AI compute will get cheaper over time. Instead, while per-FLOP costs may decrease, the total compute consumed per query may increase faster, driving up absolute costs.
