# EpistemicAlignment: Benchmarking Epistemic Transparency in Instruction-Following Language Models

**arXiv:** 2609.13775  |  **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13775

**Authors:** Chengshuai Yang, Bowen Wang, Yuan Guo, Jiahao Huo, Zihan Zheng, Zhicong Lu, Yujie Zhou, Mike Zheng Shou

## Abstract

We introduce EpistemicAlignment, a benchmark for epistemic transparency: whether a model reveals the real epistemic basis of its answer under instruction pressure. The benchmark covers answerable, unanswerable, and conflicting conditions, including a hidden-conflict setting in which an ambiguous question and a constraint admit multiple plausible interpretations. Our central metric, Constraint-Adherence with Epistemic Provenance (CAEP), requires models to satisfy the constraint and identify which candidate they selected. Evaluating frontier models (GPT-5.x, Gemini, Claude, Qwen, DeepSeek, Kimi) and reasoning models (DeepSeek-R1, Qwen3-Thinking), we find that models frequently pick a plausible candidate and deliver a confident, compliant answer without surfacing the ambiguity; sycophancy compounds the failure; and reasoning does not reliably resolve it.
