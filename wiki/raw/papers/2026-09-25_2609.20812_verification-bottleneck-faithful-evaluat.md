# When Verification Is the Bottleneck: Faithful Evaluation of Process Reward Models under Rate-Limited Verifiers

**arXiv:** 2609.20812  |  **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20812

**Authors:** Sheldon Yu, Aashka Gilday, Arun Chellapragada, Akash Srivastava, Yuyang Zhang

## Abstract

Process reward models (PRMs) provide dense, step-level supervision for reasoning, but measuring their precision in realistic settings requires costly verifier calls to establish ground truth. Under a limited verifier budget, naive estimates are biased because the evaluated sample is non-uniformly selected. We present a framework for faithful, budget-constrained PRM evaluation using importance sampling to obtain unbiased precision estimates despite non-uniform sampling. We derive optimal sampling strategies and show that naive estimates can substantially overstate PRM quality, while importance-weighted estimators recover the true precision at a fraction of the verification cost.
