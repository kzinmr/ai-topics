## Overview

A Vanishing Gradients podcast episode examining the current state of agentic data science — specifically, what data science workflows should be delegated to AI agents and how to verify their outputs. The episode brings together perspectives from across the open-source data science ecosystem:

- **Gaël Varoquaux** — co-creator of scikit-learn, research scientist at Inria

## Key Themes (from tweet context)

The episode title and subtitle frame the central question of agentic data science:

1. **What to Delegate**: Which parts of the data science workflow (exploration, feature engineering, model selection, documentation) are safe and effective to hand off to AI agents?
2. **How to Verify**: Given the probabilistic and interpretive nature of data science, what verification frameworks ensure agent-produced analyses are trustworthy?
