# Claude Duet review — AI by Aakash (2026-10-31)

Aakash Gupta's newsletter issue covering Claude Duet review + Anthropic IPO thesis + weekly AI roundup. Key sections below.

## 1. Claude Duet review (research preview)

- Framing: "There's been a new race in AI this year. Instinct, Muse, Hermes, Grok Bot, all of these are a new sort of proactive agent. The big question has been, where is Anthropic in this race?"
- Duet: proactive agent app; nudges with tasks, completes tasks, keeps working when you stop. Runs on **Opus 5.5**, no model picker, no effort picker (a cost problem for Sonnet-heavy users).
- "For my money, Claude Code is the best AI harness, but can Duet replace it? Spoiler alert, I don't think so, but it's an interesting surface."
- Piggybacks on existing Claude memory, skills, connected apps — the big benefit for Claude power users. Groups/channels structure (Slack-like). "Most of it needs to be prompted" despite the proactive claim.
- Recall quality weak, especially for local Claude Code sessions. Waitlist-gated.
- **Grok Bot Vault contrast**: "Vault, where it can remember your logins, cards and addresses without the bot ever seeing them. As you'll see below, this is a big differ[e]ntiator" — Duet inherits connectors/memory but no equivalent secret-hiding vault story.

## 2. Anthropic IPO financial analysis (S-1 breakdown)

- H1 2026 net loss **$80.7B**, but "$72.4 billion of it is a non-cash charge for the rising value of its convertible notes, derivatives and SAFEs, driven by the higher valuation."
- **Q2 2026 operating profit $974M** (vs Q1 operating loss $1.7B).
- Enterprise: **>11,900 customers paying $100K+/year**, up from ~950 a year earlier.
- Growth: Dario planned 10x this year; "growth ran closer to 80x annualized in Q1."
- Valuation: last private round **$965B (May)**; IPO expected at **$1.5–2T**. Damodaran: "priced rather than valued." $1.5T ≈ 23x annual run rate; still cheaper multiple than Palantir.
- Two pricing paths from today's **$65B run rate**: at ASML's 14.5x and NVIDIA's 16.1x multiples.
- Author's stance: "I'll be a buyer of Anthropic at the IPO, as long as it's... between $1.5 and $2 trillion."
