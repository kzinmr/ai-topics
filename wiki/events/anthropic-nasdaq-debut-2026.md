# Anthropic Nasdaq Debut (ANT) — Largest IPO Ever (November 19, 2026)

Anthropic listed on **Nasdaq under the symbol "ANT"** on November 19, 2026 — the culmination of the roadshow logged in [[events/anthropic-ipo-roadshow-2026]] and the S-1 in [[entities/anthropic]] (§IPO timeline). The HN front-page thread (1,095 pts / 2,168 comments, HN 50562754) was the day's dominant AI discussion. Reuters framed it as putting "AI's promise and peril on Wall Street test."

## Pricing & scale (wire aggregation via Techmeme snapshot, Nov 19)

- **IPO priced at $83/share**, above the marketed $70–78 range (upsized offering).
- **$81.3B raised** (NYT/WSJ/FT); Bloomberg reported **$107.3B** including greenshoe/overallotment — either way **topping SpaceX's record** for the largest offering ever.
- **Valuation ~$1.5T** at pricing (AP, BBC, Fortune). Consistent with the pre-listing $1.5–2T expectation band in `entities/anthropic.md`; below the "$2tn investor speculation" figure.
- Reuters: the IPO "**mints thousands of millionaires**" (Sabrina Valle).
- Cory Weinberg (NYT): the debut sets up an "**experiment in corporate power**."
- **Ownership**: Amazon + Google together will own **nearly 30%** of Anthropic post-IPO (TechCrunch, HN 50547901).

## HN reception themes (thread 50562754)

- **Engineered pop**: underwriters deliberately underprice for a day-one pop; "entirely engineered process until the opening bell" (dofm).
- **Historical scaling**: Google IPO'd at ~0.16% of the total US market cap; Anthropic at ~2% at IPO, 2.74% at the current price (trimbo); "50x Ford's market cap... over half of Google's" (everforward).
- **Lockup / insider-selling watch**: attention on whether VCs sell early and whether Dario/board disclose sales ~90 days in advance (jrflo, cmrdporcupine); founders can borrow against holdings regardless (bagacrap).
- **Meme-stock skepticism**: Tesla-style divorce of price from fundamentals (afavour); mega-IPOs now priced as if success were certain rather than risky (TheAlchemist).

## S-1 deep read & first-day numbers (The Signal / Alex Banks, Nov 20, 2026)

Full-body recovery of the Nov 20 issue "Anthropic's IPO explained (for people who aren't investors)" via `thesignal.substack.com` custom-domain bypass: `raw/newsletters/2026-11-20_the-signal_anthropic-ipo-explained-full.md`. This is the first-party explainer that translates the three S-1 filings (original, Nov 2 amendment, Nov 10 amendment) and the 57-slide roadshow into plain English.

**RSP "AI-accelerated progress" threshold — "imminently" → "met" in 8 days:**
- Nov 2 amendment: Anthropic said it "may imminently meet" the Responsible Scaling Policy threshold defined as "dramatically accelerated progress attributable to AI" — layman's terms: AI doing so much research/engineering that one year produces what two years normally would (i.e. **recursive self-improvement**).
- Nov 10 amendment: wording changed to **"we have met this threshold"** — "from 'we're close' to 'we're already there' in a span of just eight days" (Banks).
- Caveat Banks flags: the claim is based on what Anthropic **expects** from future models and its overall rate of progress, not on what has already been demonstrated.
- Corroborating datapoint from the same issue: **Claude wrote over 90% of the contributions to Anthropic's own codebase in September**.
- Banks's risk framing: the faster AI does research, the less buffer time between a first yes and a launch — "at some point, the limit to growth will become 'how quickly can a human say yes' to the AI's output."

**First-day trading**: peak price **$111.53** on day one (~$1.5T valuation, closer to $2T counting every share including staff options) — extends the $83 pricing figure above.

**Business numbers (S-1 + roadshow, per Banks):**
- Revenue >45x over 18 months while compute costs grew 10x; **gross margin >85%** YTD, up from 29% a year earlier.
- **Net revenue retention ~900%** (a customer who spent $1 a year ago now spends $9).
- H1-2026 net loss $80.7B / $104B over nine months — ~90% a **non-cash convertible-note mark-up** ($72.4B H1 / $94.3B 9M), consistent with the accounting-charge breakdown in [[entities/anthropic]] (§S-1 financial breakdown); $6.2B convertible debt outstanding as of June, almost all held by Amazon (marked ~$146B in Amazon's latest 10-Q).
- **Operating cash flow +$6.6B** over nine months (vs −$2.0B a year earlier); roadshow leads with a 12% *adjusted* operating margin — Banks's honest read: "close to breaking even but not quite there yet."
- Revenue trajectory: $386M (2024) → $4.6B (2025) → **$33.2B in 9M-2026** ($16.3B H1, 14x YoY; Q3 alone $17.0B). Banks: the only one-year comps are COVID vaccine makers; over two years not even they keep up.
- $100K+/yr customers: ~1,500 (end-2025) → **11,900+**; >half the Fortune 500 spend $100K+/yr on AI, yet still <0.1% of their total expenses (Banks: even 10x that = 10x Anthropic revenue). Quarterly revenue growth decelerating 144% → 47% — "14x growth in a year is extremely difficult to repeat."

**Compute & balance sheet:**
- Filing: Anthropic could pay **SpaceX up to $120.5B for compute through 2029** (~$3.1B/month once live); after early 2027 either side can exit on **90 days' notice**.
- Infra-light model shifting: **"Anthropic Direct"** — leased chips run in data centres built just for Anthropic, expected to be a growing share of compute.
- $81.3B IPO raise earmarked for compute/infrastructure; only ~$9B of the $141.1B raised burned so far — **~$132B in cash and short-term investments**.
- Banks's risk to watch: demand slowing before chips reach the end of their useful economic life.

## Second-day collapse and the $4T AI wipeout (Nov 20)

Anthropic's second day of trading erased the debut pop: shares fell 9% on Friday to $88.30, close to the $83 IPO price (Guardian). The Nasdaq Composite erased all its 2026 gains and closed ~5% below its Jan 1 value; the WSJ counted ~$4T of AI-linked market value wiped out in the week. Dow and S&P 500 closed in correction territory. Semiconductor names led the decline (SanDisk -14%, Micron -12%, Nvidia -14%); Asia had its worst week since 2020 (Nikkei -5%, Kospi -5.5%, SoftBank -14.5% its worst day in 2+ years, TSMC -6%, Taiwan exchange chair pledging market stability).Analyst framing: "relentless selling in tech" (Neil Wilson, Saxo) and Anthropic "draining market liquidity" as investors sell other holdings to pay for the newly floated stock (Chris Beauchamp, IG).

HN reception on the Guardian thread (336 pts) centered on OpenAI's missed IPO window: rumors that OpenAI's accounting is not clean enough to file an S-1 (FT link in-thread), Sam Altman's 2025 decision to reject bank price targets being called a "generational whiff" (jordanb), and the read that Anthropic "desperately needed the cash" (randomNumber7). Several commenters argued no founder ever sincerely believed the trillion-dollar AGI valuation and the exit was always meant to precede delivery (27183, blackenedgem).
