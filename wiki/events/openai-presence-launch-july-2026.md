# Event: OpenAI Presence Launch (July 2026)

**Date**: July 26–27, 2026
**Type**: Enterprise Product Launch
**Significance**: OpenAI launched a new enterprise product called **OpenAI Presence**, deploying voice and chat agents that operate company systems and execute approved actions. The product was deployed directly by OpenAI's own engineers at BBVA and SoftBank.

## Overview

OpenAI Presence uses voice control powered by [[events/2026-07-08-openai-gpt-live|GPT-Live]] to direct multiple agents in ChatGPT Work or [[entities/openai-cua|Codex]]. The agents can:

- Navigate and operate **company systems** (ERP, CRM, internal databases)
- Execute **approved, pre-defined actions** with enterprise authorization
- Accept **voice and chat** commands in natural language
- Coordinate **multiple agents** simultaneously through a single interface

## Deployment Details

- **BBVA** — Spanish banking group, first enterprise deployment
- **SoftBank** — Japanese telecommunications and technology conglomerate
- OpenAI deployed **their own engineers** on-site for implementation, following the enterprise services model established with McKinsey and other consulting partners

## Technology Stack

- **Voice interface**: Powered by GPT-Live for real-time voice interaction
- **Agent orchestration**: Multi-agent coordination via ChatGPT Work or Codex
- **Authorization layer**: Pre-approved action framework for enterprise safety

## Significance

OpenAI Presence represents OpenAI's push into **enterprise agent-as-a-service** — deploying custom agents directly into client operations rather than just providing API access. This mirrors Anthropic's Managed Agents approach (Launched with [[events/claude-opus-5-release-july-2026|Claude Opus 5]]) and marks the beginning of **direct enterprise agent deployment** by frontier AI labs.
