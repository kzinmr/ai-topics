# The Plunging Price of Thought — Epoch AI (Sep 2026)

On 2026-09-22 Epoch AI published **"The plunging price of thought"** (Luke Emberson, David Roodman): the cost of achieving a given level of AI performance has fallen **~47% per quarter / 13× per year since 2023** — 4× faster than DNA sequencing, 6× faster than compute, 18× faster than lithium batteries, 54× faster than electricity (century to 1973). Headline: o3 needed ~$0.30/question for 75% on GPQA Diamond (Jan 2025); 18 months later GPT-5.6 Luna matched it for **$0.0004 — a 725× drop**. Declines are steepest at freshly-debuted SOTA (66%/quarter, 75×/year) and halve after ~2 years (32%/quarter). Caveats: benchmaxxing, frontier-switching assumption, noisy 3-year data.

Ethan Mollick amplified it on Sep 23 ("this terrific chart"); it is the anchor measurement for [[concepts/token-economics]] and lands mid price-war week ([[events/claude-opus-5-5-gpt-6-release-sep-2026]]). Full details: [[entities/epoch-ai]].
