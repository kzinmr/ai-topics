# Event: Health in ChatGPT (July 2026)

**Date**: July 26–27, 2026
**Type**: Product Feature Launch
**Significance**: OpenAI rolled out **Health in ChatGPT** to US users, enabling secure linking of Apple Health data and supported medical records for LLM-powered health context understanding.

## Overview

Health in ChatGPT allows US users to securely connect their **Apple Health data** and **supported medical records** to ChatGPT. The integration uses an LLM to provide contextual understanding of health information, enabling users to:

- Ask questions about their health data in natural language
- Get LLM-powered analysis of trends and patterns in vitals
- Understand medical records with AI-assisted interpretation
- Maintain **privacy and security** — users control which data is shared

## Privacy & Security

The implementation follows healthcare data handling best practices:

- **User consent required** — data sharing is opt-in
- **Granular controls** — users choose which health data types to share
- **Secure connection** — Apple Health API integration through authorized channels

## Significance

Health in ChatGPT represents OpenAI's first major move into **healthcare AI as a consumer feature**, bridging wearable health data (Apple Watch, iPhone Health) with LLM-powered analysis. This positions ChatGPT as a personal health assistant alongside its general-purpose AI capabilities. The launch follows the broader industry trend of AI companies entering the health and wellness space.
