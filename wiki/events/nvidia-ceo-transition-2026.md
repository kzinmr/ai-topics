# NVIDIA CEO Transition (November 2026)

On **November 12–13, 2026**, NVIDIA's Board of Directors announced that co-founder **Jensen Huang resigned as President, Chief Executive Officer, and member of the Board, effective immediately** — ending a 33-year tenure as CEO. The official press release confirmed facts that had been *rumor-level* on 2026-11-11 (WSJ report of board preparing removal, HN 1085 pts; see [[entities/jensen-huang]] "Board preparing to oust him").

## Official announcement (NVIDIA press release, primary source)

- **Jensen Huang resigned** as President, CEO, and Board member, effective immediately.
- **Colette Kress** — EVP & CFO since September 2013 (previously ~25 years in finance roles at Cisco, Microsoft) — was appointed **Interim CEO while retaining the CFO role**, until a permanent successor is named. The Board launched a search considering internal and external candidates.
- **Harvey C. Jones** — entrepreneur, technology executive, venture investor, former CEO and Executive Chairman of **Synopsys** — was appointed NVIDIA's **first-ever Chair of the Board**.
- Board statement (Jones): the Board has "full confidence in Colette and the leadership team"; operations, customer commitments and product roadmap expected "to continue without interruption."
- Kress statement: committed to "building on the extraordinary foundation Jensen created over the last 33 years."
- Q3 FY2027 earnings unchanged: Tuesday, **November 17, 2026**, 1:20 p.m. PT release, 2 p.m. PT call — Kress's first earnings call as interim CEO.

## Trigger chain (as reported)

The resignation follows the November 11 WSJ/NYT reports: an alleged affair with a woman **suspected of spying for China**, an export/"spy relationship" scandal, and a board that had retained an outside firm to identify CEO candidates. Huang holds ~3.4% of NVIDIA with **no dual-class voting rights**, so he could not block a determined board.

## Congressional testimony (Nov 12 follow-up)

Per TechCrunch (via Daring Fireball), **"Ex-Nvidia CEO" Huang agreed on Wednesday to testify before Congress by Thanksgiving**, after House Republicans threatened a subpoena:
- **Brian Mast** (House Foreign Affairs Chair), **John Moolenaar** (House Select Committee on the CCP Chair), **Rick Crawford** (House Intelligence Chair) sent a joint letter on **November 4** requesting his appearance.
- Testimony will be **behind closed doors**, but Mast expects it to be **recorded on video and transcribed**.
- Mast: "Jensen and I have been very at odds... on export controls, but Nvidia is a very important company to the United States of America... he has very serious questions to answer."

## Market & industry reaction

**Gary Marcus** (same day, "Jensen Huang resigns."): while conceding Huang "was one of the greatest CEOs in history," he argues Huang "had to go" — he "enabled and even lobbied for Chinese AI, subverted US policy, and carelessly left himself vulnerable to both blackmail and espionage," with blindness to conflict of interest leaving NVIDIA open to "endless litigation and investigation." Concrete market facts:
- Stock: **−8% premarket → −13% at the opening bell**.
- Central question for the AI industry: whether an NVIDIA with an acting CEO still executes the **billions promised to Anthropic in its IPO** — now "front and center" for [[events/anthropic-ipo-roadshow-2026]] (roadshow opened Nov 10).
- Marcus's framing: Huang was "the ringmaster of all the insane circular financing" (cf. [[concepts/ai-infrastructure-circular-financing]], [[concepts/ai-bubble-economics]]) — "all that might collapse." He expects congressional investigations to continue, and names **China as the biggest immediate winner**, with Lutnick and Trump possibly caught in the crossfire.

## Open questions

- Permanent CEO search: internal/external candidates; earlier reporting (Nov 11) floated Apple hardware chief **Johny Sruji** at rumor level — unconfirmed.
- Whether NVIDIA's Anthropic IPO backstop and its $100B–$250B upstream purchase commitments survive unchanged under interim leadership.
- Whether the recorded closed-door congressional transcript is released, and what it reveals about export-control lobbying.
