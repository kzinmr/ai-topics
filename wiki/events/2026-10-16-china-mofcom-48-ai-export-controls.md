# China's First Frontier AI Export Controls (MOFCOM Announcement No. 48)

**Date**: 2026-10-16 (announced) · **Status**: interim registration in effect; catalogue draft open for comment until 2026-11-15

China's MOFCOM + MOST issued Announcement No. 48 of 2026, the first time China places frontier AI under outbound export control: 40 proposed catalogue additions (23 AI), covering model weights/checkpoints/source code/architecture at ≥10²⁶ FLOPs **or** ≥2T parameters **or** global top-10 eval/call-volume rank; agent planning/coordination/tool-use above behavior-defined benchmarks (prohibited for critical-infrastructure/defense agents); and AI-enabled offensive cyber capability. An interim pre-export registration requirement applies **now**, including exports in progress.

Key inversion vs. the U.S. regime (Fable 5 suspension directive): China controls **weight custody and local deployment**, leaves API/SaaS access untouched. Self-hosted Chinese models outside China require supplier licensing; already-public weights are outside scope, but *new* open-sourcing needs automatic licensing after reporting — making Chinese open-weight release cadence a supply-chain variable. The 10²⁶ FLOP threshold mirrors the 2023 U.S. EO; ChinaTalk estimates no released Chinese model exceeds it yet. Comment deadline deliberately precedes an expected APEC leaders' meeting.
