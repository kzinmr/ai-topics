# Modernization Inner-loop — Source Availability and Raw Corpus

Availability register for the 42 titles in [[queries/modernization-inner-loop-reading]], checked on 2026-10-14 through focused author/publisher searches, primary pages and the existing O'Reilly catalog links. The GitHub Wiki's code-modernization page points to these same repository guides.

Public readability and permission to copy/republish are separate findings. **U means unresolved, not unavailable.** Previews, companion articles, source-code repositories and other editions do not count as the requested book's complete text. [[queries/modernization-inner-loop-study-notes]] records the sections actually read.

## Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning | Saved form |
|---|---|---|
| A | Official complete text with a verified reuse license | Original archive, mechanical extraction, attribution and license |
| R | Official complete readable text; republication permission unverified | Reference and availability record only |
| E | Public excerpt/preview of identifiable book text | Reference and independently written synthesis |
| C | Companion/tutorial/pattern/code resource, distinct from full book | Reference and scope distinction |
| P | Author/publisher catalog or preview entry; text extent not established | Reference; no claim of full text |
| U | No reusable public full-text source verified in this pass | O'Reilly reference; unresolved acquisition status |

Commercial book/chapter text was not copied from O'Reilly or third-party PDF mirrors. Authenticated O'Reilly access was used for focused reading and original summaries. The supplied EBooksWorld PDF was not downloaded. A downloadable commercial PDF on a third-party host is not itself evidence of redistribution permission.

Preserve the NC/ND conditions for subsequent uses. Keep attribution and licenses with all three distributions. The SLO materials' publication date was not established; the directory therefore records ingestion date with `unknown`, rather than assigning a publication date from PDF build metadata.

The two book archives retain exact HTML bytes and their original assets. Markdown is a reading aid: HTML/PDF originals remain authoritative for diagrams, layout, tables and hyperlinks. Extra frontmatter identifies the origin, license, source hash and extraction hash. Binary files keep their original bytes; their metadata is external.

## Integrity and reuse checks

[Acquisition manifest](../raw/papers/modernization-open-corpus-2026-10-14.json) records original URLs, the pinned Google revision, distribution scope and checksums. Each of the 75 Markdown extractions also has its own body SHA-256 and original HTML/PDF hash. The corpus comprises 84 files and about 25.7 MB, including 2 source archives and 2 standalone PDFs.

Before promotion into the repository: both ZIP archives passed integrity checks, all 22 secure/reliable chapters were present, and required metadata plus all 75 body checksums passed. The PDFs were parsed as 27/98 pages; selected handbook pages were rendered and visually checked against the extraction. This verifies capture and selected readability, not the truth of every assertion in the books or full review of all archived text.

Future acquisition should resolve U/P items through exact-edition author/publisher evidence. If full-text reuse is unavailable, retain a precise reader location and original notes. Further reading follows the conditions and remaining chapter scopes in the guide; outer-loop remains a separate reading path.
