# Cohere.io (startup)

> **⚠️ Disambiguation**: This page is about **Cohere.io**, the AI customer-support startup acquired by Ramp in 2023 — *not* [[entities/cohere|Cohere]], the Canadian frontier-model lab (cohere.com). The name collision is a long-standing citation trap in this wiki.

**Cohere.io** was an AI-powered **customer support automation** startup (YC-backed) co-founded in 2020 by **Rahul Sengottuvelu** (CTO) with Yunyu Lin and Jason Wang. It applied early transformer-era NLP to deflect and automate support tickets — pre-dating the 2023 "support bot" agent wave that Intercom's Fin and Decagon later industrialized.

## Key facts

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2020 | Founded (with Yunyu Lin, Jason Wang); Rahul as CTO |
| 2021 | Raised **$3.5M** from Initialized Capital, Y Combinator, and others; Rahul concurrently launched the **Jsonformer** project on GitHub (structured-output generation from JSON schemas — an early constrained-decoding tool) |
| June 2023 | **Acquired by Ramp**; the ~6-person engineering team joined Ramp, where Rahul became Head of Applied AI |

## Why it's in the wiki

1. **Talent-flow case study**: a small applied-LLM startup absorbed into a fintech's AI org — Ramp's internal AI stack traces directly to this acquisition (see [[entities/rahul]]).
2. **Pre-agent support automation**: built before ChatGPT-era agents made "AI support rep" a category; its Jsonformer lineage connects to today's [[concepts/structured-outputs]] tooling.
3. **Name-collision hygiene**: entity/concept pages must disambiguate against [[entities/cohere|Cohere (cohere.com)]].
