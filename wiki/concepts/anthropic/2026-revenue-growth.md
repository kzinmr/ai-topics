# Anthropic 2026 Revenue Growth ($44B ARR)

Anthropic disclosed Q1 2026 revenue growth of 80x year-over-year, with Annualized Recurring Revenue (ARR) now above $44 billion. Widely considered the fastest enterprise revenue growth in history.

## Revenue Trajectory

| Period | ARR | Growth |
|--------|-----|--------|
| End of 2023 | ~$100M | — |
| End of 2024 | ~$1B | 10x |
| End of 2025 | ~$9B | 9x |
| May 2026 | ~$44B | Projected 5x this year |
| July 2026 | ~$65B annualized | FT report (Aug 23, 2026) |

The doubling period is approximately **6 weeks**. Analyst Ming Li estimated $96 million in ARR added per day.

## What's Driving It

Growth is driven not by adding more seats but by existing seats consuming at a categorically different rate. 1,000+ enterprise customers now spend >$1M annually on Claude — double the count from February 2026. The consumption-based pricing model enables exponential scaling as enterprises deepen their AI usage.

### FT Report: July 2026 revenue & model spend (Aug 23, 2026)

A Financial Times story (covered via Simon Willison's Link Blog) added new "people with knowledge of the matter" data points:

- **July annualized revenue ~$65B** (up from $47B in May), with Anthropic telling investors it **expects Q3 to be profitable** under the same model it used to declare Q2 profitable.
- **6,000 customers** spending $100,000 annually or more.
- **OpenAI** (same FT piece): annualized revenue jumped **35% in the quarter** to over $40B, with the July GPT-5.6 launch "jolting the company's performance after a sluggish start to the year."
- **Ramp AI Index** (billing data from 70,000 Ramp corporate card–using companies) — July 2026 Anthropic model-spend breakdown:

  | Model | Share |
  |-------|-------|
  | Opus 4.8 | 28.0% |
  | Sonnet 4.6 | 8.3% |
  | Fable 5 | 8.0% |
  | Opus 4.6 | 6.9% |
  | Sonnet 5 | 3.6% |
  | Opus 5 | 3.5% |
  | Opus 4.7 | 1.7% |
  | Sonnet 4.5 | 1.3% |
  | Haiku 4.5 | 1.0% |
  | Opus 4.5 | 0.7% |

  The breakdown supports the view that **Fable 5's cost has made it a less popular model** — its 8.0% share trails the much cheaper Opus 4.8 (28.0%) even though Fable was the headline frontier release. FT headline: *"Anthropic's best AI model struggles to attract users as cheaper tools thrive."*
