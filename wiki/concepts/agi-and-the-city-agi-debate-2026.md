# AGI & the City — November 2026 Capability-Narrative Cluster

"AGI & the City" is the informal label for the cluster of late-October/early-November 2026 statements in which frontier-lab leaders, mathematicians, and the U.S. government each re-anchored the meaning of "AGI" / "super intelligence" — worth tracking as one node because the five sources reinforce (and occasionally contradict) each other within ~72 hours.

## The five sources

1. **Sam Altman — "AI research is now mostly empirical"** (quoted via an X post circulating 2026-11-02). The framing: hypothesis generation in frontier labs is increasingly delegated to models, with humans pruning and interpreting — a methodological claim, not a capability benchmark. (Primary tweet not re-fetched in this run; treat wording as secondhand.)
2. **Ilya Sutskever — "RL is not enough"** (SFT-adjacent podcast remarks, late Oct 2026): scaling RL on verifiable rewards plateaus; novelty must come from new training signals — paired with his standing prediction of a "2027 surprise" that would reframe the AGI question itself.
3. **Amit Sahai, Karthik C. S., Jie Xue — "Egoless Mathematics in the AI Era"** (guest post on [[entities/terry-tao]]'s blog, 2026-11-02; HN 50341736, 167 pts): if a mathematician's worth is attached to producing results first, AI erodes the practice; the authors re-anchor on Platonist discovery and community understanding. Tao notes the post itself was "converted using AI."
4. **U.S. Census Bureau — "Super Intelligence" survey wording** (Simon Willison, 2026-11-02; HN 50336840, 118 pts): Executive Order 14434 renamed federal "Artificial Intelligence" to "Super Intelligence", and the Census Bureau complied by updating instrument wording for the October 19, 2026 collection — government statistical language now encodes the capability narrative, with Willison (citing an updated Apollo chart) as the data-integrity watchdog.
5. **Weight-space programming** (MIT CSAIL, 2026-11-02; HN ~2,751 pts) — the capability artifact that gives the whole cluster new force: see [[concepts/weight-space-programming]]. Unlike September's talk-based declarations, it ships reproducible numbers.

## Reading the cluster

- **Synchronization**: labs (Altman), ex-lab (Sutskever), academia (Sahai et al.), and government (EO 14434/Census) all moved their AGI vocabulary in the same week — the narrative layer is now multi-institutional, not lab-marketing-only. Compare [[concepts/agi-declaration-controversy-2026]] (September, executive-claim-driven) — the November cluster is more heterogeneous and less deniable.
- **Tension**: Sutskever's "RL is not enough" cuts against Altman's "mostly empirical" complacency; the Sahai essay denies both the ego-economics that powers lab competition; Census compliance makes skepticism institutionally awkward ("which survey question measures super intelligence?").
- **Data-integrity angle**: renaming the object of measurement inside the measurement instruments (Census wording) is the government analogue of benchmark contamination — flagged by Willison's thread.
