# Open Secure AI Alliance

The **Open Secure AI Alliance** is a security-focused industry alliance formally launched by **NVIDIA** in July 2026. Founded by CEO Jensen Huang, the alliance advocates for an open ecosystem spanning both open-weight and closed frontier models as a defensive necessity in the age of AI-powered cyber attacks.

## Founding Thesis

Jensen Huang framed the alliance's core argument starkly: **attackers already have strong AI, so defenders need an ecosystem spanning open and closed frontier models, plus shared tooling and research.** The key premise is:

> "During the Hugging Face incident, closed AI blocked essential forensics. An open-weight frontier model helped contain the intrusion."

This argument reframes the open-vs-closed debate from a development philosophy question to a **security operations necessity** — defensive capability and auditability require open access to frontier models, not just proprietary APIs.

## Confirmed Participants

The alliance quickly accumulated credible infrastructure and tooling members:

- **Hugging Face** — confirmed participation
- **LangChain** — confirmed participation
- **Nous Research** — confirmed participation
- Support from open-ecosystem voices including **Unsloth AI** and **Yuchen Jin (UW)**
- **OpenAI** reportedly decided NOT to join, triggering internal employee backlash
- **Anthropic** also did not sign NVIDIA's open-weights letter, later clarifying its position

## Policy Context

The Open Secure AI Alliance launched amid intensifying policy pressure around open-weight models:

- **Anthropic** published a position statement saying it has "never advocated for a ban on open-weights models," instead supporting chip controls on China, anti-distillation measures, and mandatory safety testing
- The **US government** reportedly sought up to 30 days of pre-release access to frontier systems for evaluation by NSA and CAISI
- **NYT reported** that OpenAI and Anthropic quietly lobbied Washington regulators to restrict open-source AI models even while publicly supporting openness
- The alliance's framing directly counters these restriction narratives by arguing that open access is essential for security

## Significance

The Open Secure AI Alliance represents a significant realignment of the open-weight debate. Rather than arguing openness for transparency or democratization reasons, it makes a **pragmatic security case**: in an era where attackers already have strong AI, the defense ecosystem must be equally capable — which requires open access to frontier-level models.

This positions NVIDIA (historically an infrastructure provider, not a model publisher) as a political voice in the AI governance debate, alongside established players like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Hugging Face.
