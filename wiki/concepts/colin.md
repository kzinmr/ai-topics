# colin (colinrand)

**colin** (handle: `colinrand`) is a community commenter whose quote was
recorded in the coverage of the **"No AI Slop" manifesto** discussion
(AIE, 2026-11-15) — an industry pushback against AI-generated documentation
and content.

## Notable Quote

> "Plus, I find reading slop docs takes cognitively much more effort. When I
> find problems in human docs, it's easier for me to point out the issue,
> comment etc. But on these AI slop docs, I find it overwhelming to comment
> on, because the issues are often woven throughout the document in a way
> that makes it so I can't isolate the comment in a single place. It's hard
> for me to describe."
> — colinrand, AIE "no AI slop manifesto" thread
> ([[raw/articles/2026-11-15_aie-no-ai-slop-manifesto]])

The quote captures the **"diffuseness" critique** of AI slop: unlike a human
doc bug (localizable, commentable), LLM-generated errors are distributed
across the text, raising review cost super-linearly.
