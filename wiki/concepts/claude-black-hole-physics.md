# Claude Solves Two Open Problems in Black Hole Physics (November 19, 2026)

[[entities/anthropic]] announced that **Claude Fable 5.2 solved two open problems in theoretical physics**, both about black holes and the search for quantum gravity (HN 50563148, 512 pts / 241 comments). Two preprints were published alongside the post. Anthropic's own caveat: "**Neither result describes our own universe directly, and neither has practical applications**, but both contribute to a long-running theoretical effort to understand how quantum mechanics and relativity fit together."

## The two results

1. A **20+ year-old unfinished calculation** completed: a sudden transition associated with black-hole formation **persists even where spacetime no longer has a definite shape**.
2. The **exact upper limit on black-hole mass consistent with a given central charge** — i.e. the precise bound for the simplest bootstrap test of candidate quantum-gravity theories; any theory predicting above it is not a quantum-gravity theory of that universe.
