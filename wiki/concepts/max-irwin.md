# Max Irwin

**Max Irwin** is a search-relevance engineer and the third co-author (with [[concepts/trey-grainger|Trey Grainger]] and [[entities/doug-turnbull|Doug Turnbull]]) of ***AI-Powered Search*** (Manning, December 2024, foreword by Grant Ingersoll) — the wiki's standard reference for integrating LLMs into production search architectures.

## Professional Profile

- **Founder of Max.io** — search/relevance consulting practice
- **Former Managing Consultant at OpenSource Connections (OXC)** — the search-relevance consultancy behind the "Test Driven Relevance" methodology and the relevant evaluation tooling ecosystem
- Co-author, ***AI-Powered Search*** (Manning, Dec 2024, ISBN 9781617296970, ~520 pp): RAG, semantic search, hybrid retrieval, learning-to-rank, click models, knowledge graphs, multimodal search, deployment patterns

## Relevance to This Wiki

The *AI-Powered Search* triangle (Grainger / Turnbull / Irwin) is the recurring citation cluster behind the wiki's hybrid-retrieval and LLM-search pages:

- [[concepts/trey-grainger]] — first author; Searchkernel founder
- [[entities/doug-turnbull]] — second author; Reddit Principal Engineer; SearchArray, "Cheat at Search"
- Irwin's OXC background anchors the **evaluation-driven relevance** tradition (judgments, nDCG/MRR discipline) that the LLM-era RAG pages inherit
