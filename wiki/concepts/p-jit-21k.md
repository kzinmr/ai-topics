# P-JiT 21K (Apache 2.0 Text-to-Image at $21K)

**P-JiT 21K** is an experimental 2.18B-parameter pixel-space text-to-image diffusion model trained by **Linum** (a 2-person research lab building generative video models from scratch) for **$21,038 total compute** — 8,415 H100-hours at $2.50/hr on 64 H100s — released under **Apache 2.0** with model, code, and weights (Nov 2026, "Road to Linum v3 · Issue 04"). HN: 274 points.

> **Confidence: medium** — all quality/cost claims are Linum's own; the release is explicitly framed as a proof-of-concept "pressure test," not a benchmarked product. Authorship note on the page: text human-written, gallery/diagrams and HF card/code repo generated by Claude Opus 5.5.

## The bet: kill the VAE, compress harder

Linum's thesis chain (from their Field Notes series):

- Attention is O(n²) and is the cost bottleneck for training **and** inference of image/video diffusion.
- VAEs (the standard compression stage) have an **empirical compression ceiling**, and better VAE reconstruction doesn't reliably improve downstream generation.
- Diffusion models are a more powerful generative family than VAEs → let the diffusion model **learn its own latent space** via aggressive token reduction instead of borrowing a VAE's.

**Pyramid-JiT (P-JiT)** implements this as a pixel-space DiT with **32×32 token reduction** (vs the industry-standard 16×16, e.g. FLUX-2): a 512² image occupies the tokens a normal model spends on 256² — a **4× attention-context reduction**, converging in **11× fewer samples** than their prior Wan-VAE+DiT baseline (Linum v2).

## Architecture essentials

- Frozen **Qwen3.5-4B** text encoder read at its three full-attention layers (7/15/27); separate 2-block text/image "refiners" then a 22-block shared DiT trunk (hidden 2,944; 23 heads × 128; SwiGLU FFN).
- **Trunk readouts** (after blocks 5/10/16 + final head) predict the clean image at ¼/½/full resolution — exploiting the fact that in pixel space every block already predicts the image, boosting weak early-block gradients (adjacent to REPA / RAE-v2 / Self-Flow findings). A 2-layer **PixelREPA** head aligns block-5 latents to DINOv3-L features (20% token masking); all training-only heads dropped at sampling.
- Low-rank **AdaLN** timestep conditioning (Z-Image style), zero-init scale/gate; Muon on 2D block matrices + AdamW elsewhere; curriculum over the noise schedule (LogitNormal(0.8,0.8) → widened (−0.2,1.0) after 80M samples); EMA from 100M samples with spike-aware pausing.
- 610M samples at 512² + 50M at 1024² (RoPE interpolation, fresh optimizer from 512² EMA weights, noise scale doubled).
- Post-training: **FDFO** (Finite Difference Flow Optimization) with **JEV-Image** and human-in-the-loop judges — $690 of the budget.

## Limitations (stated)

No rendered-text training data (cannot generate text/posters), and as a 2B model it struggles with full bodies, many-entity scenes, multi-person interaction, and world knowledge. Linum expects a 7B variant plus more post-training to mitigate.

## Why it matters

- A datapoint for the **"LLM-style budget realism"** argument: a *decent* (their word) Apache-2.0 image model for the price of one mid-range GPU cluster month — relevant to price-performance debates like [[concepts/price-performance-frontier]].
- Their framing of **diffusion post-training as a young discipline** — DPO overfits aesthetics, Flow-GRPO only arrived fall 2025, FDFO (June 2026) is "the best articulation so far" — pins down why RLVR-style gains haven't transferred from LLMs to diffusion: no strong likelihood estimates.
- The FDFO + Jev-as-reward-judge stack connects to the [[concepts/system-one-models]] / [[concepts/calibrated-escalation]] decision-model trend.
